## 1. What the course teaches, in one page

1. **Hook, Story, Sell.** Hook stops the right buyer, a 2 to 4 sentence story makes them trust you, the sell gives proof and a reason to buy now. "Hook → the right person stops / Story → they trust you / Sell → they get proof and a reason to buy now." (call 9/22)
2. **Who you are decides what you can sell ("unfair advantage").** "Would a stranger believe you about this product before you said anything?" (call 9/29). A prompt scores creator fit as "PERCEIVED AUTHORITY × AVATAR RELATABILITY × PRODUCT FIT × CLAIM BELIEVABILITY", minus "CONTRADICTION RISK" (Frameworks PDF, "$5,000 prompt" thread).
3. **Product trust vs creator trust.** "If the product needs trust → YOU build the trust. If the product creates trust → let the PRODUCT build the trust." Visible results (shapewear, lashes, gadgets, fashion) show the product at once; delayed results (skincare, supplements) get pain + a short trust story first, "introducing the product around 7 to 10 seconds into the video" (Loom "When should I introduce the product?").
4. **Big Fish.** Keep asking "why" until you reach the biggest shared feeling: dry skin, then makeup sits badly, then "You're not ugly without makeup." The same script made $500 with the small pain and over $200,000 commission on a 35M-view video with the big one (Big Fish lesson).
5. **Outsider effect.** Every extra qualifier in the hook makes buyers scroll ("if you're a dad / with a daughter / whose daughter's name is Emma", call 11/3). Call out the broadest buyer who has the pain: "For all my tired moms who don't have time to eat" (call 9/22 recap).
6. **Every line earns its place.** "Every sentence has to earn its place in the script." Per-line test: "If I removed this line, would the video actually get worse?" (Script checklist PDFs). Domino test: each sentence must knock over the next (call 11/3). Max three pain points, then the product (Live Script Audit 11/06).
7. **Sell the emotion, prove it with one feature.** "The ingredient isn't the selling point. It's evidence that supports the selling point." Formula: "result + because of + ingredient/feature", 2 to 3 selling points max (Selling the Product; call 9/22).
8. **Talk about private behaviors, not results.** "I used to put concealer on just to walk my dog." It sells and avoids violation flags: "show the behavior instead of stating the claim" (Avoiding Violations; Sam, community 10/19).
9. **Common enemy.** Never attack the viewer; blame bad products, bad advice or "them". "If they feel like you are judging them... they won't buy." (Gibran, community 9/11)
10. **On-screen caption = a second hook.** "5 to 8 words max", one capped word, one emoji that names the feeling, never repeat the spoken hook, never "commercial scent" ("Run don't walk", "Best Amazon find", "You NEED this"). Five buckets: warning, behavior interrupt, belief interrupt, unexplained reaction, unexplained consequence (Caption Framework PDF). A missing text hook was called a failure in every live audit (11/06).
11. **Pick products by data.** About 4.4 stars or more, $5 to $39, 10,000+ units sold but not 800,000+, solves a real problem, a brand with ad money; "80% of commissions come from 20% of products" (5 Keys; call 9/22).
12. **Scale a winner by changing one thing.** "Change → Compare → Learn → Keep or Adjust → Repeat", one variable per test (call 11/3). Diagnosis: no views with good production = hook problem; views without sales = body problem.
13. **UGC is a second paycheck from the same skill.** Lead sources, hottest first: "Reactivation → Outbound → Inbound → Platforms". Pitch "2 to 3 written concepts", rates only after a concept is picked; "Understand → Quote → Negotiate"; "Willing to take $800? Quote $1,500"; usage rights, whitelisting (Spark codes) and raw footage are priced as separate add-ons (UGC calls 9/23 to 10/21, Brand Response Templates PDF).

## 2. Free things to add now (no rule changes)

### Grace's videos
1. **Big Fish + outsider test in every hook plan.** Write the why-ladder in RESEARCH.md, take at least one hook from the last rung, then run the stadium test on each hook. (Big Fish lesson; calls 10/06, 11/3)
2. **Per-line script audit as a gate proof.** Green / yellow / red per line, the "would it get worse without it?" question, a repetition pass ("did I just say this already?"), max 3 pain points before the product, max 3 selling points each with one proof. (LINE-BY-LINE PDF; audit 11/06)
3. **Private-behavior lines from real comments** in RESEARCH.md, used in place of result claims. Also safer for wellness products. (Avoiding Violations; community 10/19)
4. **Common enemy + "attack the problem" check** on every script. (Common Enemy lesson; Gibran 9/11)
5. **"If you" openers over "I" openers**, unless the "I" carries a true lived experience. Our hook library has many "I" hooks (H006, H009, H024, H031, H033, H034, H036, H049, H081): keep them word for word but prefer "you" ones when choosing. (Hooks lesson; call 9/15 notes)
6. **"Make the mess you clean" and "show it, don't say it".** Each claim line names the shot that proves it; for visible-result products the hook shot is the problem being fixed (stain poured, then wiped). Fits our AI stills well. (Live Script Audit 11/06)
7. **Product screen in every plan, one line:** pain level, stars, price band, units sold, shipping. Weak products get flagged to you before any spend. Also add it to Grace's Creator Desk scoring. (5 Keys; Sellability formula; call 9/22)
8. **Caption library** in `grace/hooks/`: the five buckets, the emotion-emoji table, the 10-point check. Offered as the optional text line in each plan, and used on any version you OK text for. (Caption Framework PDF)
9. **Ban the "commercial scent" phrases** in the gate: "Run don't walk", "Best Amazon find", "My new favorite product", "I found the BEST", "You NEED this". (Caption Framework rule 04)
10. **Unfair-advantage interview for Grace, once.** The prompt asks up to 7 questions; the result (her avatar, selling lanes, credibility traps) becomes `grace/AVATAR.md` and tells us which products she is a weak fit for. Needs her answers. ("$5,000 prompt" thread; Frameworks PDF)
11. **Contradiction-risk check:** does the script credit one product for a whole result the viewer can explain another way? Then reword to "part of my routine". (prompt "CONTRADICTION RISK")

## 3. Where the course disagrees with our rules (your answers, 2026-11-09)

| # | The course says | Our rule now | Evidence on our side | Your answer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | A short on-screen text hook on frame 1 is a second hook; missing it is a failure (Caption Framework, audit 11/06) | Hard rule 6: no on-screen text unless you say yes per ad | Our own sales tracker: all 6 of Grace's selling AI videos had a short text line on frame 1 (she typed them in TikTok) | Keep no text (Grace can still type a line in TikTok) |
| B | Show the product first only when it proves itself; for skincare and supplements, pain + trust story first, product at about 7 to 10 s | PLAYBOOK §5: product working in a hand from frame 0, every product | Our 6 sellers all showed the product in second 1, but all 6 were visible-result products (dilator, glasses, bag...) | Split by product (saved) |
| C | Scale a winner by changing one thing at a time | Hard rule 6: every video changes shots, order, framing, grade and background | TikTok flags repeated footage, the reason for our rule | Yes, one-change remakes, new footage (saved) |
| D | No background music under talking or VO; talking on camera beats voiceover | Most Grace AI ads are VO or music-only | Our music-only 11 to 12 s cuts sold; the course's advice is about music under a voice | Yes, A/B one VO ad with no music under the voice (saved) |
| E | Borrowed proof on screen (a customer result, an older photo for before/after, a Google screenshot under an ingredient) | No product cards; condition photos, never before/after | Course says "Overlays do the work your credibility can't" | Keep bans, small opening: one real proof screenshot on your yes per ad (saved) |
| F | CTA with real stock or sale urgency ("they're low on stock", "it's still on sale") | No price, no "on sale", no false scarcity | Price and sale talk trigger violation cards | Keep no price; true, checked stock urgency on your yes per ad (saved) |
