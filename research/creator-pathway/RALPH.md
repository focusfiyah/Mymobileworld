## 1. Know what people believe from you

1. **Run the "unfair advantage" interview on yourself.** The course's prompt asks up to 7 questions and returns your creator avatar, who naturally trusts you, your strongest lanes, what you can credibly say and your "credibility traps". Its test: "If 100 strangers saw this creator for five seconds... what would they naturally trust this person talking about". ("$5,000 prompt" thread; Frameworks PDF)
2. **Money claims are a trap unless shown.** Members' results flagged lines like "Let me teach you how to make $20K/month" as credibility traps when no result is on screen. Keep AI on Autopilot claims to what you can show. (prompt thread)
3. **A true, short authority line early.** "Viewers cannot give you credit for experience they do not know you have." One line in the first seconds, for example that you run AI video work for a TikTok Shop creator. (prompt thread)
4. **Creator gear is a natural lane for you.** Several members' results listed phone mounts, tripods, lights, mics and "creator workflow" tools as a fit for working creators; you use these daily. (prompt thread)
5. **One audience per account.** "If your views are dying and you think you're shadowbanned, look at how many random products you've been posting." Keep one For You audience; off-topic posts go on a separate account. (TikTok Shop course, Stage 3; call 9/29)

## 2. Hooks and captions

6. **Open on the viewer's problem, not "I".** "Use 'you,' not 'I.'" Start with "If you..." plus one broad, real pain, and never answer it in the first line ("You're not tired you just need magnesium gummies" became "If you're exhausted every morning even after a full night of sleep"). (Hooks lesson; calls 10/06, 11/3)
7. **Big Fish: climb to the biggest shared want.** Keep asking "why" until you reach the feeling everyone shares (for you: time, money, being left behind). Same script, small pain: $500; big pain: a 36M-view video. (Big Fish lesson)
8. **Don't shut people out.** Every extra qualifier makes viewers scroll ("if you're a dad / with a daughter / whose daughter's name is Emma"). (call 11/3)
9. **Caption framework on every text hook.** 5 to 8 words, imply instead of explain ("you've been sleeping wrong", not "3 tips for better sleep"), one capped word, one emoji that names the feeling, never the same as the spoken hook, no "commercial scent" ("Run don't walk", "You NEED this"). Test: "If I froze the video on frame one would I want to know what happens next?" Check with `hook_check.py` as now. (Caption Framework PDF; Loom 9/10)
10. **Open-ended text beats direct text.** "They want us to pay more…" style, readable in half a second, gone by about 5 s. (Live Script Audit 11/06)

## 3. Formats that grew accounts

11. **A journey series with an open loop.** "Day 1 of N" of what you are really building (AI ads for a client, Day One AI), with honest numbers: "I pitched 30 brands today and I got a reply from two of them." A journey post got about 900 followers from 30,000 views. (calls 9/30, 10/7, 10/28)
12. **A documented stake.** The challenge winner counted down "about 42 days before I have to" go back to a 9 to 5, "documenting completely everything". (call 10/28)
13. **Short formats.** Two 11-second videos drove 452,000 views and 527 followers in three days. Cut one idea at 7, 15 and 45 s and post each; "every video doesn't have to be 30, 40, 50 seconds long". (call 10/14)
14. **Value from a beginner's seat.** "I set up these profiles and made $100 this week" relates more than expert advice. Mix with personality posts, and don't "become a coach by accident". (call 9/30)
15. **"Get Found" plan.** One real ongoing story, four content categories, three pinned videos, one video a day for 30 days, real results only. (UGC Personal Brand Prompt)
16. **Under 1,000 followers: storytimes with a part two.** Story posts with a reason to come back grow a new account. (Getting a TikTok Shop Account)
17. **Try TikTok LIVE for reach.** "You can get dropped into random people's FYP while you're live"; talk to whoever shows up. Try a few mornings and measure. (Sam, community 11/01)

## 4. Delivery and editing

18. **FaceTime delivery.** "Talking to a friend on FaceTime and giving them insider information. More informal and nonchalant." Car or phone-down openings work because "the viewer has their guard down." (Gibran, community 9/26)
19. **Tone matches the words.** "Your script is the text. Your tone is the emoji." Fix: "How would I say this if I were complaining to a friend?" Calm beats hype: "let me put you on". (calls 10/13, 11/06)
20. **Talking beats voiceover.** The coaches say VO converts worse than talking on camera. Your cartoon presenter is a middle ground; test a few face-led posts. (calls 9/29, 10/13)
21. **Edit simply.** Cut dead space and breaths, one overlay at a time, "No background music on talking videos, unless it's very intentional storytelling", under 60 s. (Editing lesson) If a reel has music under your voice, try one without it.
22. **Comment replies.** Reply videos to comments of 5 to 7 words you actually answer. (Comment Replies lesson)

## 5. Growing what works

23. **Change one thing at a time.** "Change → Compare → Learn → Keep or Adjust → Repeat." On any signal, make three versions that each change one variable (hook, setting, delivery), and keep a winning setup until it stops working. (calls 10/27, 11/3; Sam, community 10/13)
24. **Read the numbers.** No views with good production means a hook problem; views without sales or follows means a body problem. Run the "Core 4" on every flop, in order: production, product or topic, script, delivery, side by side with the top creators in your niche. (calls 10/13, 11/3)
25. **Remix winners, don't copy.** Paste a top post's transcript into one long-running AI chat that already knows you, rewrite it in your own story, then Humanizer. "Borrow the mechanism, not the words." (calls 10/14, 11/3)
26. **Keep posting past the slump.** In the 14-day challenge most people stopped on day 6; growth went to the ones who kept going. Treat volume as what you control. (call 10/28; Three Rings PDF)
27. **Pick a topic with signal, then test on it.** Use the 5 Stages idea: once a topic shows signal, test hooks and formats on it instead of switching topics. (5 Stages lesson)

## 6. If you pitch brands yourself

28. Send 2 to 3 written concepts first, rates only after they pick one; quote above your floor with "I'm currently accepting projects starting at $X"; when told it's high, ask "What budget did you have in mind?" (calls 10/21; Brand Response Templates PDF)
29. Be searchable for the people who hire: the role they search for in your display name, email and portfolio link in your bio, and ask every inbound where they found you. (calls 9/30, 10/14)
