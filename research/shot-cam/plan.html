What I would build

Part A · The phone camera (first)

  • A web page that opens from a link on any iPhone or Android phone, nothing to install. One link per video.
  • It loads the shot list from our plan: shot number, the spoken line, the direction (angle, move, framing outline on screen), HAND or HAND-FREE.
  • 3-2-1 countdown, record, keep or retake, film in any order, a tick on each done shot. Optional line prompt on screen for talking shots.
  • "Send all" hands the takes to Google Drive through the phone's share menu, into the product's Originals folder, at full quality. That also fixes the Telegram draft problem.
  • Hosted free on Netlify (already connected). Our own name and look, not theirs.

Part B · The studio flow

  • One command in a session: pull the takes from Drive, match them to shots by number, pick the best take, build the first cut in script order with all our QC, put it on a review page.
  • Notes in plain words ("open faster", "use the other stairs take") make a revised cut, same as now.

Part C · Camera roll mode

Drop a folder of clips; I find the best moments from frames and speech, propose a story, cut it after your OK.

Part D · Style memory

One style file per creator (Grace, you), updated from each cut you approve: pause length, music level under the voice, hook length, pace. Every next cut starts from it.
