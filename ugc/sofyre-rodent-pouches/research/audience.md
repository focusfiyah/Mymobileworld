# Audience: SoFyre Rodent Repellent Pouches (2026-10-13)
Data: `research/audience/questions.md` (2,710 autocomplete phrases from Google, Bing, YouTube, Amazon, TikTok), `comments.md`, `voc.md`.

## Who
Homeowners and renters, mostly moms, with a pantry, a garage or a basement, who start thinking about mice when the weather turns. They search "how to get rid of mice in house" (5 of 5 sources agree) and "mouse repellent for garage", "mouse repellent for cars", "mouse repellent kid safe". Many have pets and kids, so they search "is mouse repellent safe for dogs" and "what mouse repellent is safe for cats".

## Pain point experience
- It starts with a sign, not a mouse: "finding droppings in your pantry" and "I kept finding signs of mice in my kitchen, but I couldn't figure out where they were coming from."
- It is seasonal and feels unfair: "Mice ain't coming in your house cause it's dirty, they coming in because it's cold outside." and "Mice come into the basement when the weather changes in the fall".
- They hate the alternatives: "I don't have to kill a mouse and then have to dispose it."
- They worry about the kids and the dog: "Is it pet friendly" and "So you don't gotta worry about your kid's fingers or your pet's health."
- They doubt it works: "do mouse repellent pouches work" is one of the top questions, and one buyer wrote "I think it is working as advertised, although I still see some evidence that mice are around."

## Communication style
Plain, a bit grossed out, practical. The winners talk like a neighbour: "guess who's trying to look for somewhere warm to live?", "Throw these in your garage, near your doors, anywhere that mice can get in." They show WHERE the pouch goes (pantry, under the sink, garage, car) and the smell ("it smells like a fresh pack of gum"). No science lecture, no fear-mongering past one gross detail.

## What it means for the script
- Hook on the season (cold coming), the way the 3.1M and 1.5M winners do, and show the product in the first second.
- Pain = the droppings in the pantry, said once, shown small (not a horror show).
- Show the placements one after another: pantry, under the sink, garage, car (people search garage and cars by name).
- Answer the two buyer questions on screen: kids and pets ("Kids and Pets Safe" per the listing) and does it work (no promise past the listing: it deters rodents; no "kills", no "guaranteed").
- Sell the count and the season: twelve pouches, up to three months each (brand site: 30 to 90 days), do the whole house before it gets cold. Grace's voice, no personal-use claim.
