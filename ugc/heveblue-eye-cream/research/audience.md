# Audience: HEVEBLUE auto eye cream (2026-10-27)
Data: research/audience/questions.md (2,595 autocomplete phrases), research/audience/comments.md (456 TikTok comments, 7 videos), research/audience/voc.md (retailer reviews, TikTok comments; Reddit 403 this session).

## Who
Women about 30 to 50 who already buy eye cream and doubt it does anything. The top searches are "are eye creams worth it", "do eye creams actually work" and "are eye creams a waste of money". The creators selling this exact tube say "pushing 40" and "pushing mid 40s". K-beauty fans who buy because it looks like the future (caption of the 1.8M winner: "Korea is definitely living in the year 2200").

## Pain point experience
1. Eye cream feels like plain lotion: "Esthetician here (I don't currently work as one but am licensed). She's right! 80-90% of them are just moisturizer repackaged." and "I really just use the Kiehls for under my makeup at this point because I agree that it doesn't really do much".
2. Dry under eyes and concealer that cracks: "I have dry under eyes and the Kiehls is the only thing that seems to help." and "Can I wear concealer over it without it rolling or peeling?"
3. They want something that works and keep asking: "So which ones work??" (658 likes).
4. They doubt a gadget: "It probably doesn't even do anything vibration wise."

## Communication style
Short, funny, a little sarcastic, friend-to-friend. They react to sound and surprise ("can you hear that?"), and the comments joke about the buzz. Questions are practical: "would you do this before or after moisturiser?", "is it waterproof?".

## What it means for the script
- Open on the proof, not a claim: the tip touching water so the rings show the buzz (the 1.8M and 730K winners both open this way), with the real buzz sound.
- Pain beat in their words: plain lotion in a tiny jar, half of it left on your finger, concealer that creases.
- Answer the gadget doubt on screen: the tip turns on by itself and works the cream in; say the 12,000 a minute number (listing).
- Keep it clean and on the face; never play to the innuendo.
- Real reason now: cold weather dries out under eyes, holiday gifting (mom, sister) = more than one unit.
