Product description
Discover HEVEBLUE Eye Cream
✨
HEVEBLUE Black Yuja Bean Lifting Auto Eye Cream
is a smart solution for sensitive skin. It features an auto-vibration applicator that gently massages and refreshes the delicate eye area. The device activates upon touch, providing fine vibrations for enhanced absorption and a soothing massage. Formulated with Yuja Water, Mung Bean Extract, and Black Soybean Extract, it nourishes and revitalizes tired skin. Vegan and hypoallergenic, it's perfect for sensitive skin.

Q: How does the device work?
A: The applicator activates gentle vibrations upon skin contact for enhanced absorption and a soothing massage.

A: Yes, on smile lines or between the brows.

A: Morning and night as the final step of your skincare routine.
