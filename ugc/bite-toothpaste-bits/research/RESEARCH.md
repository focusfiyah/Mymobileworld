## Product differentiation
Bite is "the original viral toothpaste tablet" (listing), on Shark Tank, in business since 2018, with 21,000+ five-star reviews. Each bit is made with nano-hydroxyapatite and xylitol, with no sulfates, parabens, harsh chemicals or artificial flavoring, made in the USA in small batches. It comes in a refillable glass bottle with a silver cap, no plastic; one 62-bit bottle is a month of brushing twice a day and equals 7 travel-size tubes. Against NOBS (934K sold, plastic-free pouch), DR.DENT (purple, whitening angle) and Huppy, Bite's edge is the name people already know, the glass bottle that looks good on a counter, and the gentle formula ("Gentle Foam": less foam by design, no SLS).

## Target buyer
Women 25-45 who swap to clean versions of daily things and travel a few times a year. They search "toothpaste tablets for travel", "is bite toothpaste legit" and "are toothpaste tablets worth it". The moment it becomes relevant is packing a toiletry bag, or wiping dried paste off the sink again.

## Pain points and desires
Pain: the tube (crusty cap, blob in the sink, the last squeeze), the liquids bag, plastic guilt, and doubt about the new format ("Do these ACTUALLY work??? I need ASAP!", "how do you literally chew?"). Desire: a clean counter, one less thing to think about when packing, teeth that feel "just as clean as they do when using tubed toothpaste", and "no plastic guilt".

## Buyer motivation
Holiday travel is weeks away: carry-ons and toiletry bags get packed now. It is also an easy "one small switch" that feels good every day without changing the routine (still brush twice a day).

## Objections and purchase justification
- "Do they work?" -> nano-hydroxyapatite + xylitol (title); people say teeth feel "just as clean" as tubed paste (mygreencloset.com review).
- "How do I use it?" -> chew 5-10 seconds, brush with a wet toothbrush (listing image 2).
- "Less foam?" -> it foams once you brush ("froths right up like regular toothpaste"); the gentle formula skips SLS on purpose (listing FAQ image).
- "Is it legit?" -> Shark Tank, since 2018, 21,000+ five-star reviews, made in USA (listing images).
- "Worth it?" -> one bottle is a month and equals 7 travel tubes (listing images). No price talk.

## Overall verdict
People buy Bite because brushing stays the same while the tube mess and the travel hassle go away, from a brand they have already heard of. Lead with the tube annoyance and show the chew-and-brush demo right away; close on travel season with one bottle for home and one for the bag.
