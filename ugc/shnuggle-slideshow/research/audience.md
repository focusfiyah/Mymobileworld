# Audience: Shnuggle toddler tub response videos (2026-10-16)
Data: research/audience/questions.md (984 autocomplete phrases, seeds toddler bath tub / toddler bathtub / baby bath tub), research/audience/comments.md (107 TikTok comments, 4 videos), research/audience/voc.md.

## Who
Moms (and some dads) of babies around 9 to 18 months who just outgrew the infant tub: Grace says on her own 81K video that her baby girl outgrew her infant stage tub, and people search "baby bath tub for 1 year old" and "toddler bathtub for 2 year old". Many have only a stand-up shower or a big tub that feels unsafe: "toddler bath tub for stand up shower", "toddler bathtub for walk in shower". These videos answer two commenters on Grace's own video.

## Pain point experience
1. Price shock first. The top comment on Grace's video is "over priced" (13 likes), followed by "Damn. $70" and "$$$$$". They need the value reframed without a number.
2. The in-between gap: too big for the baby tub, too small for the big tub. Searches like "do toddler bath tubs exist" and "are toddler bath tubs worth it" show they doubt the category exists at all; a buyer wrote "I just ordered the baby version for mine because I thought this would be too big… but maybe I should’ve grabbed the toddler tub😵‍💫".
3. Fit and safety worries: "Does it actually fit in the bathtub? it looks bulky", "Do they have to be able to sit without assistance?", "are toddler bath tubs safe".
4. Emotion: "Watching your baby move into a new stage is such a bittersweet feeling" (348 likes). Growth milestones land.

## Communication style
Casual mom-to-mom, short, emoji-heavy, a bit of humor about money ("If i follow TikTok shop, mi nav nuh money"). They respond to a real mom showing her own child's stage, the exact age in months, and plain feature-by-feature proof (backrest, non-slip, plug). They save gear videos for later ("Who else is saving this but isn’t pregnant and don’t have children?").

## What it means for the script
- R1 must answer price head-on (Grace's hook reframes it against daily coffee, no number said) and then justify value with the long use window (her line: up to age three) and reuse for a next child. Her deal-hunting CTA matches the money-joking tone.
- R2 must validate the commenter ("just the right size") and the category gap ("nobody knows this toddler tub exists"), then name the safety features, then a save CTA (this audience saves).
- Visuals: show scale (infant tub vs big tub vs Shnuggle), the backrest, the base and the plug, because "does it fit / is it bulky" is the open question.
