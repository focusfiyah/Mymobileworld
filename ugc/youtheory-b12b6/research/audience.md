# Audience: Youtheory B12B6 Liquid (2026-10-26)
Data: audience/questions.md (1,746 autocomplete phrases, Google/Bing/YouTube/Amazon/TikTok, seeds "b12 liquid",
"b12 energy"), audience/comments.md (22 TikTok comments on the 5 researched videos), audience/voc.md (Reddit
r/Supplements threads, creator transcripts, label).

## Who
Tired adults who want more energy without another pill or another energy drink: shift workers, busy moms and grandmas,
mostly women 30-60 on the Youtheory videos (car selfie nurse-style 12-hour shifts, a grandma, a 2 pm-crash mom). Their
first question is whether B12 works at all: "does b12 give you energy" (3 sources agree), "does b12 liquid work",
"do b12 energy drinks work". The second is the format: "b12 liquid vs pill", "is b12 liquid better than tablets",
and they already search "b12 liquid pouches" and "b12 energy packets". Many come from caffeine: "b12 energy vs caffeine",
and on Reddit "I don’t want to have to drink Celsius every day though."

## Pain point experience
The pain is being tired when sleep should have fixed it: "Here i am using up entire weekend and still feel like I require one more day."
and "Without it, I get brain fog and anxious and super tired." The Youtheory sellers name the same moments:
"Been a long week, and you tired as all get out." and "You ever hit two in the afternoon and feel like your energy is completely gone?"
They are also tired of the routine of pills ("Don't want to swallow another vitamin pill?") and of coffee jitters.
Doubt is real and must be respected: "No. None of the Bs made any noticeable difference." and
"You have to be deficient otherwise you pee it i think". Practical questions before buying: "how to take b12 liquid",
"does b12 liquid need to be refrigerated", and taste ("Is it salty? lol").

## Communication style
Friend-to-friend, a bit funny, no lecture: "Girrrl you need a shot of B12" and the grandma skit's "Rip it and sip it."
They trust a lived moment over a claim ("When I tell you I do 12 hour shifts and I stay stock with these.") and simple
ease ("No pills, no mixing. Just grab one and go."). Buyers who like it say so plainly: "I love these! Keep mine in the refrigerator".
Skeptics are present, so the tone stays soft ("may", "support"), never a promise to end tiredness.

## What it means for the script
- "A pouch a day keeps laziness away" matches the label (one packet per day). The audience says "tired", never "lazy": the hook works as a playful twist, but the opening shot should show a tired moment (afternoon slump, long shift) so it reads as tired, not as calling people lazy.
- "Your body may need B vitamins!" keeps the "may": right for this crowd, since Reddit doubters say B12 only helps when you are low. Do not add any promise of feeling it fast.
- "delicious raspberry-flavored" is on the label ("Great Tasting. Ready to Drink"); taste questions ("Is it salty? lol") make a visible sip worth a shot.
- "12 in a box" is true (12 x 1 fl oz). Show the box and the 12 pouches fanned out (kurvykara, jenniferdaniel do this).
- "B6 and B12 support normal energy metabolism" is supported: label "Helps Convert Food Into Energy" / "helps support energy production", Walmart listing "Supports Mood, Nervous System & Metabolism".
- "B12 supports normal nervous system function" is supported by the brand's Walmart listing title ("Nervous System") but is NOT printed on the TikTok-visible box or pouch, which say "promotes brain health". OK to say; on screen show the pouch line "promotes brain health".
- "while they're in stock" matches a winner ("they go fast. It took me a while to get these").
- Contradictions to avoid: no caffeine claim, no "instead of coffee" promise (Reddit: "B12 would be the last thing" vs caffeine); not for under 18 or pregnant/nursing (label), so the hand is adult and nothing suggests kids or pregnancy.
- **Straw: NO.** Every Youtheory B12B6 winner drinks straight from the pouch's pink-capped spout or holds it; the label says "Shake well and consume one (1) liquid packet per day directly by mouth." Show shake, twist the cap, sip from the spout. No straw, no glass.
- Visual ideas from winners: pouch in a purse or car cup holder (portability), box + pouches on a bright kitchen counter, a 2 pm desk slump before the sip.
