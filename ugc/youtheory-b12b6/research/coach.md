## What the winners did
- Tiredness first, product second: every Youtheory seller opens on a tired moment (12-hour shift, grandbabies, 2 pm crash, "woke up tired AGAIN").
- Format is the sell: "Don't want to swallow another vitamin pill?" and "No pills, no mixing" beat the vitamin science.
- Coffee is the foil: "not gonna give you that jittery feeling like coffee does" (glenny), matches the "b12 energy vs caffeine" searches.
- Portability shown: purse, car, gym bag; box + pouches fanned out on a counter (kurvykara, jenniferdaniel).
- **No straw.** All 4 Youtheory videos drink straight from the pouch's pink screw spout or just hold it ("shake it up a little bit, I open it, and then I drink it like that"); the label says "directly by mouth". Unlike the collagen packets (josina's straw), this pouch has a spout.
- Raspberry/taste is said by all (buffy: chilled "it'll taste like, well, fruit punch"); buyers ask "Is it salty? lol".
- The big money in liquid B12 is the expert-teacher format in Spanish (86.7k, 987 saves): rules + "methylcobalamin, not cyanocobalamin". Youtheory IS methylcobalamin, an unused angle.
- Lengths: 15-61 s; the Youtheory videos are small (under 1.4k views), so this product has no proven big winner yet: model the hook on the category, not one video.
