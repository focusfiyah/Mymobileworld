## Not used on purpose
- Listing bullets "Make more money faster and easier than ever before", "power and control over ... virtually other people", "personal genie who will grant you your every wish": outcome promises TikTok can flag (income claims) and Grace can't stand behind. Scripts say what the book has you DO, never what it will get you.
- Price ($46.94 on the listing): never said (house rule).
- Author's background: Kevin Trudeau has an FTC history and a 2014 federal sentence (criminal contempt, Goodreads author bio). Scripts never name him in a hook or lean on him as an authority. Raised with Ralph once.
- "Secret society / Brotherhood" framing other creators use: left out.
