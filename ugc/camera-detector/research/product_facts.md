## Claims we do NOT make (flagged)
- "It beeps / blasts an alarm the second it finds a camera" (said in Ralph's example 1 and in 3 of the top videos): nothing on the box or listing backs it, and a buyer asks "why does mine not beep? when finding cameras?". The alarm is the door/vibration alarm.
- "GPS tracker", "RF signal" (example 1's caption, other listings): not this product.
- Battery: the listing says "Battery Required? No" but the box says 60 mAh rechargeable and a buyer says "It only works being plugged in"; we say nothing about battery life.
- "Finds all kinds of hidden cameras": box wording, but lens finders only see lenses in their view; we say "a hidden lens shines back", not "finds every camera".
