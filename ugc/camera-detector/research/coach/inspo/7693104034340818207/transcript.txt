I'm really amazed by this little gadget. If you stay in hotels a lot, you really should keep this camera detector in your bag. You never know what might be hiding behind an outlet or a smoke detector in your hotel room. With this, even tiny pinhole cameras you can't see will trigger a loud alarm the second it finds one. Hang it on the door handle and if anyone touches the door, it buzzes loudly so you can sleep peacefully.
