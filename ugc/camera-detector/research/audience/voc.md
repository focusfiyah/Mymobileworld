## How lens finders are meant to be used (tests and maker instructions)
- Data Doctors tester: "a specialty handheld detection device that uses a series of flashing red LEDs around a lens that you hold up to your eye works better"; "A dimly lit room also gave us the best detection results."
- Scout instructions: hold it to your eye with the LEDs pointed away, turn the LEDs on, look through the aperture; a red pinpoint of light coming back suggests a camera.
