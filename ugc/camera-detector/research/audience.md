# Audience: Hidden Camera Detector (2026-10-28)
Data: research/audience/questions.md (958 autocomplete phrases from Google, Bing, YouTube, Amazon, TikTok), research/audience/comments.md (50 TikTok comments, 2 videos), research/audience/voc.md (listing reviews, comments, lens-finder tests).

## Who
Women who travel and stay in hotels and Airbnbs, alone or with friends, and think about it before they get there. The searches are "how to check for hidden cameras in airbnb" and "do airbnb have hidden cameras". The comments are travelers: "As much as I travel, I would definitely need something like this" and "Where was this when I was traveling? The door alarm would have been great." The sellers aim at women by name ("If you're a girl staying in a hotel", "my best friend recommended").

## Pain point experience
1. Not knowing if someone is watching in the room or the bathroom: "There’s also people that put cameras in the bathroom of a airplane because I’ve seen a couple of times."
2. Not trusting the gadget. The top question is "do hidden camera detectors work" (3 sources), plus "are hidden camera detectors legit" and "do hidden camera detectors really work".
3. Not knowing how to use it. Buyers who never looked through the window call it "merely a flashing red light that you self activate"; others say "why does mine not beep? when finding cameras?", "Tell me how you threaded the strap?" and "Need the instructions".
4. Feeling unsafe at night with a door that might open: "is the door alarm movement activated?"

## Communication style
Plain and a little scared, friend to friend, quick to call something junk. They react to a found camera ("I would call the police immediately if I found one in a hotel room") and want proof on screen, not promises. Short practical questions: does it work, how do I use it, does it beep, where's the vibration mode.

## What it means for the script
- The hook answers "do these work" by showing the part nobody shows: look THROUGH the window, a hidden lens shines back as a red dot. That also answers the "just a flashing red light" buyers without naming them.
- Never claim it beeps when it finds a camera (the comments prove buyers expect it and it doesn't).
- Show the loop on the door handle, and say what sets it off (the door moving).
- Name the hiding spots on screen (smoke detector, shower head), the way the listing does.
- Real timing: holiday travel. Two units: one for you, one for the friend you travel with.
