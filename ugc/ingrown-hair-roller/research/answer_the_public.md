# AnswerThePublic-style research (2026-10-06)
Method: Google autocomplete (the same source AnswerThePublic uses), 9 seed phrases x 18 question/preposition modifiers, ~770 unique queries. Raw: answer_the_public_suggestions.json. Free, no login.
What people ask (clusters, with real queries):
- **Is it acne?** "are razor bumps acne", "are razor bumps pimples", "are razor bumps and ingrown hairs the same", "ingrown leg hair vs pimple" -> Script 1 hook + pain line ("You Google it. Acne? Ingrown hair?").
- **Why does it keep happening?** "why do i keep getting ingrown hairs on my legs", "why do i have bumps on my bikini line", "how long after shaving do razor bumps appear" -> Script 2 alt hook, Script 3 pain ("they keep coming back").
- **What do I put on it?** "what to put on bikini line razor bumps", "best ingrown hair serum for bikini area", "best ingrown hair roller", "ingrown hair roll on bikini area" -> product-intent searches exist for the roller format.
- **Does it work? (objection)** "do ingrown hair rollers work", "does ingrown hair serum work" -> Script 3 alt hook. (Competitor roller name appears in queries; not named in scripts.)
- **Dark spots**: "bikini line bumps and darkness" -> listing claim "helps fade dark spots" added to Script 3.
- **Areas**: bikini line (biggest), legs, underarm, face, neck; "for sensitive skin"; "for men" -> multi-area line kept.
- **Timing/after hair removal**: "after shaving" and "after waxing / brazilian wax" are the biggest modifiers. Scripts say shaving only; waxing is NOT in the listing, so not claimed.
- Usage questions (how often, when, how long): "how often to use ingrown hair serum", "how long do razor bumps last". Not in the listing, so left out until we have the label.
- Scary medical queries (herpes, cyst, pus, fever, cancer) appear a lot: deliberately NOT touched (no medical claims, playbook §1).
