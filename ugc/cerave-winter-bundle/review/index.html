Research behind it

Music-only CeraVe videos had the best save rates in the research: @lordkierra 199.6K views, 0.18% saves (cleanser pumped into the palm at frame 0); @shakyra._ 0.39% saves (cream texture, 11 s, a cut every 1.4 s). Deal voiceovers got views but 0.03-0.08% saves.
