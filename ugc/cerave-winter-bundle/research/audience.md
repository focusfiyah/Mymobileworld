# Audience: CeraVe Winter Dry Skin Bundle (2026-11-02)

## Who
People whose face, lips and hands get dry every cold season, mostly women 20-45 who already know CeraVe from drugstores and TikTok, plus a smaller group of men with very dry skin ("Asking as a men, best face moisturizer for extremely dry and flaky skin"). They search "winter skincare routine for dry skin" (bing, google, tiktok all agree in questions.md), "dry skin problems in winter" and "cerave winter skin care". Many have tried a lot already: "I have used different products each year to help, and nothing seems to get rid of my dry skin." On TikTok the bundle buyers are deal-minded: "Finally an actual deal", "half the price at Costco right now".

## Pain point experience
It comes back on schedule: "Every. damn. winter." The face flakes in public and makeup or a sleeve shows it: "Even at the slightest brush of a sleeve against my face, a patch of skin will flake off but stay stuck onto my face." Moisturizing does not seem to be enough: "Everyday I get skin flakes despite moisterizing religiously", "the cooling weather this year is ravaging my skin", "its unbearable in winters". Lips hurt: "My lips are cracked and super dry and it hurts to even open my mouth!" and "I cannot go without Vaseline for a second." They doubt a cream is strong enough for real cold: "some seem to not find it moisturizing enough, others find it heavenly". The Intensive cream is the step up when "this winter my skin is drier than usual".

## Communication style
Short, casual, first person, a bit dramatic about the cold ("Every. damn. winter."). They trust people who have lived it, like the Alaska commenter: "I use Cerave Moisturizing Cream and I think it works great." They ask practical how-to questions under videos: "no need to rinse? just pat dry?", "do it last all day?", "Help which one is better". They praise in plain words ("Love it. No hassle wash", "saved my skin this winter") and push back fast on hype ("Ruins my skin. Turns it into a dry, patchy, red, spotted, irritated mess."), so a calm, shown routine beats claims.

## What it means for the script
- 10 s music-only video: show the routine in order (wash, cream, lip balm, SPF lotion in the morning) so the four products read as one winter routine, answering "which one is better" by showing what each one is for.
- Winter cues in frame from 0 s (cold window, sweater, scarf, steam) carry the "every winter" pain without words; no flaking-skin close-ups needed.
- Show texture: the cream scooped/rubbed in and the balm on lips (the winners show the product in use and the cream's texture).
- Spoken script for later: open on the yearly pain in their words (dry every winter, lips that hurt), keep claims to the label (ceramides, 5% Hydro-Urea, SPF 50) and the bundle being one routine; no cure or "works for everyone" lines, since commenters push back.
