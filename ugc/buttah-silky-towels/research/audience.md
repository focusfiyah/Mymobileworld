# Audience (2026-11-02): Buttah Silky Soft Towels 24 ct

## Who
Women who already have a skincare routine and wash their face daily, many with acne-prone or sensitive skin, plus people who travel (hotels, gym bags). They have heard of "clean towels" and are on the fence about whether they are worth it. Their searches:
> "are clean towels worth it"
> "are clean towels a scam"
> "should i use a separate towel for my face"

## Pain point experience
The pain is the washcloth itself: it never feels fully clean, it dries the skin, it leaves lint, and hotel cloths are a guess.
> "I never thought about how dirty towels are, even when washed."
> "it leaves fuzzies on my face which I don't like."
> "We always take them to Europe because they don’t always have facecloths in the hotels."
> "Ok but even how much you clean them they still have some bacteria on them don’t trust a clean towel"
The doubt: "So wasteful" and a dermatologist's "stop with this madness" video (203.6K views) say they are not needed.

## Communication style
Friend to friend, short, a little dramatic about the old habit, then relieved: "holy moly I can never go back". Buyers love the feel and the travel use:
> "I can never go back"
> "Ngl I LOVEEEE these it gets the job done 💪🏾 super soft and convenient 🥰🔥"
> "I just added to the cart. I need those when traveling."
Their questions are practical: "Do they leave lint?", "Are they fragrance free?"

## What it means for the script
- Hook on the old washcloth, not on the product (H006: "you won't know how much your washcloth bugs you until you stop using it"), so the fence-sitters ("are clean towels worth it") see their own habit first.
- Pain line is the damp cloth going back on the face, in plain words; no bacteria or breakout claim (the derm video attacks exactly that, and the listing only says "helps prevent the appearance of breakouts").
- Solution names what buyers praise: big, soft, used once. The Ulta reviewer who found them rougher than Clean Skin Club is why "soft" is shown (towel hanging open, waffle texture) rather than oversold.
- Travel is the real urgency and the multi-pack reason: a pack in every bag, holiday travel coming, grab a few.
