Before you use your face scrubber, think about what happened overnight. You rinse it, put it down, and probably don't think about it again until tomorrow. It spends one minute on your face and the next 23 hours sitting in your bathroom, warm, damp, the kind of conditions bacteria love. But almost nobody thinks about that, which is weird because we obsess over keeping everything else that touches our face clean. That's why when I designed this face scrubber, I care just as much about what happens between uses. The open silicone rinses clean and dries fast, but that only solves part of the problem. So we infuse the silicone with silver and zinc to help actively resist bacterial and fungal growth between uses. And once people start using it consistently, we started hearing the same thing over and over. Their skin felt cleaner, smoother, and a lot of them said their skin started looking clearer, too. Because how a face scrubber cleans your skin is only half the story. What happens to it between washes matters, too.
