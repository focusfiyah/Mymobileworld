# Audience: Vans Premium Authentic, sequin checkerboard (Grace, 2026-10-21)

## Who
Women who already love the classic checkered Vans and like a bit of sparkle ("sparkly sneakers for women" and "sequin vans for women" come up on 3-4 search engines each), plus people shopping a gift for one of them (vans gift searches, holiday season). The winners' viewers are fashion/outfit girls: comments are about the bag, the pants, the song ("Omg I love how these match ur bag 😩💕", "Okay, but what about those pants? They’re so cute. Where did you get them").

## Pain point experience
Not a pain product, a want product. The hesitation is style and wear: "are sequin vans still in style", "are sequin vans worth it", "are sequin vans comfortable", "can sequin vans be worn with dresses". The money objection shows up as a joke: "not right now i’m broke". The want is instant: "These are a need", "Dang now I need to buy these".

## Communication style
Short, visual, music-led. The top Vans shop videos (70K-196K views) are 7-16 s, POV of feet in the shoes with gold anklets, the shoe held over the feet, no voiceover; comments answer the look in one line ("These might be the best fall shoe everrrr!!", "I love this new take on the classic checkers", "Those are freaking adorable!"). Grace's own 27 s talking version of this exact shoe has 305 views.

## What it means for the script
Show, don't tell: product on screen (held or worn) from frame 0, sparkle catching light, the anklet + white pedicure moment, the outfit around it. Any words answer the real questions in one breath: still in style (the classic checkers, new take), comfort (cushioned insole, people say very comfortable), wear it with jeans or a dress. Gift videos: the present moment + "for the girl who loves sparkle", real season (holidays). Music-only by default; optional Grace C lines stay under ~25 words.
