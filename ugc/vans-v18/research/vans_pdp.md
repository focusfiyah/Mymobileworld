Description

* Style [VN000DB8705](/en-us/p/shoes/icons/authentic-5310/beaded-lx-authentic-44-VN000DB8705?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=organic-shopping#panel=barcode_VN000DB8705)

  Born in 1966, the Authentic set the standard for clean, functional design. This premium build stays true to its roots while stepping things up with a lightweight canvas upper, a refined fit, and a cushioned insole for lasting comfort and a softer step. It’s a new level of wearability for a timeless original.

Details

* Heritage low top shoes
* Cotton lace and metal eyelets
* Higher sidewall with gloss finish
* Foxing tape inspired by original 90's Osnaburg reinforced outsoles
* Upgraded footbed and refined construction combine for a more seamless fit
* Sola Foam ADC (All-Day Comfort) insole for soft and durable cushioning, it is made using at least 30% biobased PU foam partially derived from plants and it is USDA Certified Biobased
* Signature waffle outsole pattern for reliable grip since '66
* Vulcanized build for the original look and feel
* Checkerboard branding is unmistakably Vans
* Sequin checkerboard artwork
* Includes extra set of laces
* Canvas upper for a light but durable finish
