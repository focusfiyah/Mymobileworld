# Research: Vans Premium Authentic, sequin checkerboard (Grace)

## Product differentiation
The classic Vans Authentic low top (the shape people already know and trust) with raised black sequins on the black checkerboard squares only, plain off-white canvas on the white squares, a red insole with the white Vans box and the gum waffle sole. Competitors: glitter slip-ons and fully sequined sneakers that look like a costume, and the plain checkerboard Authentic (product_truth.json, research/product_facts.md).

## Target buyer
Women who already wear checkered Vans and want a little shine for everyday outfits, and people buying one of them a holiday gift. Viewers of the reference videos are outfit and shop-finds followers (research/audience.md).

## Pain points and desires
Plain sneakers make a casual outfit look flat; full glitter shoes feel too much. They want "a new take on the classic checkers" that dresses up jeans or sweats without trying (comments in research/audience/voc.md).

## Buyer motivation
Holiday season and a restock moment: the top reference caption says "Not many in stock! If you see your size I would grab them!" (367.8K views). It is an impulse want product: they see it shine on a foot and want it now.

## Existing content research
Six shop videos Grace sent (proof/coach_research.md): the winner (367.8K) mixes held close-ups in a car with feet walking on a sidewalk; the rest are held-shoe turns, seated try-ons and styled close-ups, all 8-19 s, mostly music-only, shoe in frame 0.

## Objections and purchase justification
"Are sequin vans still in style?": it is the classic Authentic shape (fact 1). "Too much sparkle?": sequins are on the black squares only (fact 2). "Comfortable?": same Authentic build people already wear (fact 1; no new comfort claim). "Worth it?": shown inside and out, insole and sole real (facts 8-9).

## Content opportunities
Hand-held close-ups of each real detail (side tab, insole, heel label, sole), worn shots in real places (car, sidewalk, bedroom rug, coffee table), outfits from sweats to a satin skirt, the restock urgency only if Ralph checks stock.

## Big Fish (why ladder)
- They want sneakers with some shine
- Because plain sneakers make a casual outfit look flat
- Because they want to look put together without trying hard
- Because looking put together on a normal day makes them feel like themselves, just a bit more special
Biggest feeling: "I look cute and I didn't even try"

## Overall verdict
People buy it because it is the Vans they already love with just enough shine to make a plain outfit look done. Lead with the shoe up close and on the foot from frame 0, and let the detail shots answer "is it too much?".
