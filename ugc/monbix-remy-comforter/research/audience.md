# Audience: Monbix Remy Stripe comforter set (2026-10-26)
Data: research/audience/questions.md (2,243 search phrases, Google/Bing/YouTube/Amazon/TikTok autocomplete), research/audience/comments.md (304 TikTok comments from the 5 researched videos, 83 buyer questions), research/audience/voc.md (listing reviews).

## Who
Women redoing their bedroom for fall, renters and first-apartment people who want the whole bed to match in one buy. They change bedding with the seasons ("Y’all tryna tell me you change your bedding to match the season?!") and they shop by colour first: the most repeated comment is "What color is this?" (also "Which color is this?? Autumn leaf?", "Is that the Autum or Carmel color?"). Searches: "do comforter sets come with sheets", "what is a comforter set", "are comforter sets worth it".

## Pain point experience
They are not sure what is in the bag ("So it comes with the stripes sheets, two striped pillowcases, two solid and the comfort???", "Does this specific one come with the striped sheets?", "How do I make sure I selected the option with striped sheets?"). Hot sleepers are wary of heavy comforters ("Are they cool? I sleep hot affff and always have issues with how suffocating most comforters are", "Is it hot? Cause my room doesn't cool very well so I need a lighter comforter"). They doubt softness ("Be honest though, are the sheets soft or they are cheaper thread count?", "Is this soft???"). They have watched it sell out ("Omg when will this restock?!", "Will they be restocking this color?"). Pocket sheets are a new idea to many: "what are pocket sheets", "are sheets with pockets worth it".

## Communication style
Casual, excited, lots of question marks and emojis; they talk like friends in the comments ("that warm rust color made the whole bedroom feel sooo cozy"). They trust what other buyers say ("The pocket is great and holds my kindle!", "Fell so so soft and amazing washing!"). Short, plain sentences; no specs talk.

## What it means for the script
1. Say the colour name out loud (Autumn Leaf): it answers the most-asked question.
2. Say what is in the set in one breath, striped sheets and pillowcases included.
3. Lead with the pockets (Ralph's ask and the thing that is new to them), shown on screen right away.
4. Soft + lightweight from the listing; no "cooling" or "breathable" claim (the listing doesn't make it).
5. Urgency that is real: this colour has sold out before. More units: one for the guest room.
