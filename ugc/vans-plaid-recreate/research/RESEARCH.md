# Research: Vans Old Skool, Tartan Oil Green plaid (Grace)

## Product differentiation
The classic Vans Old Skool low top (the shape people know) in a dark oil-green tartan with red, white and yellow lines, white leather side stripe, white laces, brass eyelets, cream insole with the black Vans box logo, gum waffle sole (product_truth.json, from the TikTok Shop listing photos).

## Target buyer
Women who wear classic Vans and like a holiday outfit, and people buying one of them a Christmas gift (research/audience.md).

## Pain points and desires
They want something festive that still looks like everyday Vans, not a costume; the worry is "only in December".

## Buyer motivation
Holiday season: both references label it Christmas Vans; parties and tree-up season start now (Thanksgiving Nov 26).

## Existing content research
The two references Ralph sent (proof/coach_research.md): 5,213 and 1,015 views, 7-8 s, music only, shoe in frame 0, hand-held turns, POV worn shots, heel lifts. V18's top Vans shop video (367.8K) mixed held close-ups with worn shots, the same mix.

## Objections and purchase justification
"Only for Christmas?": dark green ground with a white stripe, worn with leggings or jeans (references). "Real Vans?": insole logo, heel label and gum sole shown up close (product_truth facts).

## Content opportunities
Shot-for-shot recreations of both references with Grace's skin tone (this job); later: the same shoe at a holiday party, a gift reveal.

## Big Fish (why ladder)
- They want festive shoes
- Because holiday outfits feel unfinished in plain sneakers
- Because they want to look like they made an effort for the season
- Because looking festive makes the season feel like theirs
Biggest feeling: "My outfit is ready for every holiday thing"

## Overall verdict
People buy it because it is the Vans they already trust in a holiday plaid. Lead with the shoe held up close from frame 0 and let the worn shots show it with real clothes.
