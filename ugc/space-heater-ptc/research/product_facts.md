## Compliance / safety in the video (my rules for this product)
- Never in a bathroom near water, never on a bed or under a blanket, never with fabric touching the grille (fire safety):
  heater sits on the floor or a hard surface with clear space; a throw nearby is fine.
- Never "safe for babies/pets", never "leave it on all night", never energy-bill numbers.
- No fake orange heat streaks (the listing art has them; TikTok viewers know it's not real).
