# Audience: small space heater (Antarctic Star 1500W PTC), 2026-10-19
Data: research/audience/questions.md (1,226 phrases, Google/Bing/YouTube/Amazon/TikTok autocomplete), comments.md (257 TikTok comments), voc.md.

## Who
Women 25-50 who run the house and feel the cold first: renters and homeowners with one room that never warms up, people at a desk all day,
parents with kids or a dog. Top searches are by room: "space heater for bedroom" (5 sources), "small space heater for office" and
"small space heater for bedroom" (4 each), "small space heater for under desk". They want something small: "perfect for small spaces".

## Pain point experience
- The cold moment: getting out of the shower, a cold bedroom, a cold desk. "I love my little heater esp after getting out of the shower", "I need this at work", "I need a heater in my dining room".
- Money: they do not want to heat the whole house for one room, and they are scared of the bill. "and how much electric does it use?? shit ain't free", "How’s that electric bill?", "My question is what's cheaper running the furnace or just using a space heater." Searches: "do space heaters save money", "are space heaters expensive to run".
- Safety worry: "are space heaters safe", "are space heaters dangerous", "why do space heaters cause fires", "are small space heaters safe for pets", "is space heater safe for baby".
- Noise and size: "quiet space heater"; the reviews praise "super quiet" and "Larger than I expected".

## Communication style
Casual, jokey, a little blunt ("shit ain't free", "My emotional support heater"), lots of quick questions in the comments
("wattage?", "What square footage does this cover?"). They trust what other buyers say and what they can see working on screen.
Short sentences, everyday words, no specs talk beyond "1500 watts".

## What it means for the script
- Open on a room or a moment they know (one cold room, 6 a.m. floor, kids and a dog) with the heater on screen and moving.
- Answer the money question without numbers: set the exact temperature, ECO mode, warm the room you are in instead of the whole house.
- Answer safety with what each feature does (tip-over sensor shuts it off, overheat protection, child lock), never "safe for babies".
- Pass on buyer words as "people say" (bigger than expected, quiet).
- Real urgency: the cold is coming (late October). CTA = one per room ("one for the bedroom and one for your desk").
