# Audience: Plant Therapy Christmas Cheer 3 Set (2026-10-30)

## Who
Women who decorate early and own an ARTIFICIAL tree, already have a diffuser at home, and shop TikTok for cozy holiday
home things and small gifts (comments: "I love a good Christmas essential oil.", "What a great gift .. even as a White Elephant gift!").
Many grew up with a real tree and miss the smell ("Growing up, we only had fresh Christmas trees, no artificial trees.").

## Pain point experience
The tree is up and the house looks like Christmas, but it does not smell like it. People search "christmas tree smell for fake trees"
and "artificial christmas tree smells musty"; they try candles ("candles that smell like christmas tree") and wonder
"are christmas diffuser blends worth it". Light pain, high want: "Nothing like the smell of Christmas 🎄".

## Communication style
Cozy, playful, a little funny, no science. They answer to scene lines ("Does it smell like Christmas in here?",
"My house smells like a non toxic North Pole." got the comment "spoke straight to my soul"). They talk about scents in
pictures ("fresh cut pine", "fresh cut trees"), not ingredients. Short, warm, excited.

## What it means for the script
- Open on the scene they know: the fake tree is up but the house does not smell like Christmas.
- Name ONE smell picture ("smells like a fresh cut tree"); the scent words come from the listing ("freshly cut evergreens").
- Brag-style payoff = a guest reaction (people asking about a real tree), not a claim.
- Gift angle for more units ("What a great gift"), real urgency = the holidays are close.
- Never "non-toxic", never health or calm/sleep claims (not on the listing).
