# Audience: OQQ wide-leg pants (black + brown)
Data: research/audience/questions.md (2,709 autocomplete phrases, seeds "wide leg pants", "high waisted pants"), research/audience/comments.md (225 TikTok comments from the inspo and the top 3 sellers).

## Who
Women who live in comfy pants but want to look put together, many of them tall or short and tired of guessing the length. They buy from try-on videos and ask the creator's height and size before they buy: "Thank you for putting your height and weight. I hate when people do this and we don't even know how tall they are." They also want the whole outfit ("Where you buy the top ?", "I need that top too! 😍").

## Pain point experience
Length is the fear. Tall buyers: "Us over 5’7” knowing it may not actually be tall girl friendly 😭" (73 likes) and "Not tall girl friendly at all. I’m 6’1 and as soon as i put these on they beating my ankles up." Short buyers: "cute😍 I need short girl friendly friend im 5ft🫣". The listing has two lengths, so the top question is which one: "Did you get the "regular" or "longer" option?", "Are you wearing the regular or longer? 🥰". Second: nobody shows the back, "Can we see what the back look like?!? Asking for the people!!" (52 likes). Third: it won't look like that on me, "I’m 5’8 240… I’m nervous they ain’t gone look like this on me". Searches ask the same things: "are wide leg pants good for short women", "how high waisted pants should fit", "do wide leg pants make you look taller".

## Communication style
Casual, warm, girl-talk ("Girl you wearin’ them pantalones🤩🤩", 289 likes; "you look good sis ."). They reward plain facts said like a friend: "Finally someone that knows how to do a review. Measurements ✅️showed the length ✅️". Short lines, no hype words.

## What it means for the script
- Open on the back (H021, the most-liked question) with the pants moving at frame 0.
- Say Grace's height, size and which length she got (Regular or Longer) out loud, and show the hem reaching her shoes: that answers the length fear.
- Show the side pocket with a phone going in (listing feature, top-3 winner #3 does the pocket).
- Friend voice ("y'all", "girl"), short lines, no price.
- Sell more units with the second colour: she got black, then brown.
