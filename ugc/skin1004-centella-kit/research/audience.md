# Audience research (2026-10-07): SKIN1004 Poremizing Travel Kit
Data: `audience/questions.md` + `raw.json` (Google + YouTube autocomplete), `audience/comments.md` (TikTok comments), `audience/transcripts.md`, `audience/voc.md`.

## Who
- Oily and combination skin, often also sensitive or acne prone, who already follow K-beauty on TikTok. They search "which centella toner is best for oily skin", "is centella toner good for sensitive skin" and "centella toner for open pores".
- Product aware but unsure where to start: "how to use skin1004 poremizing", "how to use skin1004 travel kit", "skin1004 what to buy".

## Pain point experience
1. Pores that look big and makeup that sits in them. They ask "can enlarged pores shrink", "is enlarged pores permanent" and "does enlarged pores mean oily skin".
2. Fear of reacting to a new product: "I love the ampoule. I want to try the mask. But I’m always afraid of new creams" and "ah, I wish the pink ampoule worked for me".
3. Confusion about order and frequency: "How often do you use the toner?", "can i use centella toner everyday", "Can I use the ampoule same night as retinol?".
4. Social proof from peers: "That moisturizer is my holy grail, my oily acne prone skin just loved it!" and "As an oily girly, it’s a YES!".

## Communication style
- Peer to peer, yes/no ratings and step by step routines (the top videos are a rating video and a routine video).
- Ingredient names are welcome (AHA, BHA, PHA, centella, niacinamide), said plainly.
- No shrink or permanent promises: "minimise the appearance" only.

## What it means for the script
- One angle on trying the whole line in minis (answers the fear of new products and "what to buy").
- One recognisable moment (makeup in pores by lunch) with the why: oil plus dead skin.
- One for oily and sensitive skin, built on centella in every step.
