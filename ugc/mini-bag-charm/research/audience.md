# Audience: mini bag charm (2026-10-26)
Data: research/audience/questions.md (2,257 autocomplete phrases), research/audience/comments.md (452 TikTok comments, 6 videos), research/audience/voc.md (Reddit + captions).

## Who
Women who collect mini things and dress up their everyday handbag; gift buyers shopping stocking stuffers now. They comment in the mini voice (50 likes on @the_little_beannn 331K): "Why am I so obsessed with the mini version of anything? So cute!" Another: "PLEASE WHERE DID YOU GET EVERYTHING!? I need it all i collect tiny things for my purse" The fall 2026 trend shows in search: "are bag charms in for fall 2026" and "are bag charms still in 2026"

## Pain point experience
1. Is it just decoration? "Can you actually like use it?"; on Reddit, "I'm trying to avoid getting something that won't be useful", "Did it fit??" (AirPods). They want proof that earbuds and small things go inside: "bag charm for airpods" is a top search.
2. Charms look cheap or cost too much: "Cute but why are they $37 each?!?!?!", "It's too much money for a charm.", "i can't justify $60 on a bag charm", "are bag charms tacky".
3. Small things get lost at the bottom of a big bag (the reason "functional charmies" exist on r/handbags).

## Communication style
Excited, short, exclamation marks, mini and tiny said over and over, questions to the viewer (@the_little_beannn's caption asks how many minis you counted), and where-did-you-get-it comments: "WHERE DID YOU GET THAT BAG?! ITS ADORABLE!!!" No expert talk.

## What it means for the script
- Open on the identity call-out (the mini lover), the product already in hand at frame 0, like @the_little_beannn.
- SHOW the earbuds and lip balm going in (answers "can you actually use it"). The script's stash line covers it; the shot must prove it fits.
- "look so much more expensive than a typical bag charm" answers the cheap/tacky worry without a price.
- Stocking stuffer = the real season (Christmas shopping is live); CTA should make them buy two (one for you, one to gift).
