Why am I squinting harder at headlights than I ever do at the actual dark? That's the problem. Your eyes settle. Then another one. Blast everything out again. That constant change from dark road to bright glare is what gets tiring me. There for 2 overs. I already need prescription glasses, so I didn't want some separate pair I'd have to keep changing into.
