# Audience: Baby Girl Floral Cherry 10-Piece Set (2026-11-25)

Data: audience/questions.md + raw.json (`grace/audience.py` seeds "baby girl clothes set", "baby pajamas", 1,297 phrases), audience/comments.md (10 TikTok comments on the 5 researched videos), audience/voc.md (verbatim Reddit quotes with URLs), coach shop data.

## Who
Parents of a baby or toddler girl, mostly moms, plus the people who shop for her (grandmas, aunts: the top researched video was bought "for my niece", and a comment asks "for baby alina ?"). They buy sets in bulk: the coach shop data shows 6-piece and 10-pack baby sets selling in the hundreds. Holiday shoppers right now: searches like "baby pajamas for christmas" and "baby christmas pajamas" show up on Amazon, Google and TikTok.

## Pain point experience
The pain is volume, not style: babies go through clothes all day and the parent is always behind on laundry. In their words: "I think we went through 5 outfits a day in the first 5 months of her life." and "she used all of them in the first 9 hours between vomiting and blowouts". Care tips repeat the same need: "You will want a change of clothes in case of blowouts." and "there's always at least one blowout so a change of clothes". Comfort is the standard they judge by: "Babies absolutely don't care as long as they're comfortable." Softness is what they remember: "these hideous but so soft pajamas my mom got me". Their questions are practical: "baby pajamas soft", "baby pajamas matching", "do baby clothes need fabric softener", "how to store outgrown baby clothes".

## Communication style
Tired, practical, warm, a bit of humor ("Mine is a very juicy baby"). Short plain words: outfits, changes, blowouts, spit up, soft, comfy. They react to seeing all the looks at once ("Love all three"). They tune out hype; they respond to a real everyday moment (the next spill, bedtime) and to occasions (holidays).

## What it means for the script
- Hook takes the blame off the parent (Grace's "You're not lazy" model): the enemy is how fast babies go through clothes, not her laundry habits.
- One pain beat: outfits faster than you can wash them.
- Connection: ten pieces in one set = a clean one ready for the next spill.
- Selling points as uses, max 3: soft + breathable (play all day, sleep at night: the listing's home wear + sleepwear), stretch (quick changes on a wiggling baby) [confirm stretch with Grace].
- Show all ten pieces together on screen (the format with the best save rate).
- Close on the real occasion: holidays + Black Friday (in the listing title), then "It's in the orange cart."
- Plain words, grade 3-4.
