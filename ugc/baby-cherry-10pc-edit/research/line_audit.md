# Line audit: Baby Girl Floral Cherry 10-Piece Set (Grace script method, PLAYBOOK §4)

## Script V2: voiceover over Grace's footage
Buyer: parents of a baby or toddler girl (and grandmas/aunts shopping for her) who go through several outfits a day
Pain: the Big Fish feeling "I'm always behind" (laundry never catches up with how fast she goes through clothes)
Connection: ten pieces in one set means a clean one is ready for the next spill
Outsider test: hook "You're not behind on laundry." has no qualifier (no "if you're a mom of a girl under two..."); "baby" first appears in line 2; dropped "girl mom" call-out
Common enemy: how fast babies go through clothes, never the parent's laundry habits
Contradiction check: no whole-result claim; line 3 says a clean one is ready "for the next spill", not "you'll never do laundry again"; no warmth claim (listing says spring & fall, so "cold months" was not used)
Repetition pass: "ten pieces" said once; "clean" once; soft/breathable once; prints never said (shown on screen); the cut prints line also repeated the "looks good" idea of the hook shot
Pain points: 1
Selling points: 3 (ten pieces = a spare ready because of the 10-piece count, proof: all 10 laid out; play all day and sleep at night because it is soft and breathable home/sleepwear, proof: fabric squeeze close-up; quick changes because it stretches, proof: stretch demo [confirm])

Domino test: hook (not your fault) -> cause (too many changes) -> fix (ten pieces) -> why she'll wear them all day and night (soft) -> why changes are easy (stretch) -> why now (holidays, Black Friday) -> where (orange cart).
