# Audience, NovaTabz toothpaste tablets (2026-10-30)
Data: research/audience/questions.md (1,932 autocomplete phrases), comments.md (107 TikTok comments from the winners), voc.md (NovaTabz Amazon buyers).

## Who
Women 25-45 who run the house bathroom and travel for the holidays, plus "clean ingredients" shoppers who have heard of hydroxyapatite on TikTok. They search "toothpaste tablets for travel", "toothpaste tablets travel size" and "is hydroxyapatite toothpaste safe". Most have never used a tablet: one viewer wrote "You spit it out then?!😳 I’ve been eating them!😳".

## Pain point experience
- The tube itself: messy sinks, crusty caps, squeezing, leaks in a bag (NovaTabz creator @tiktokshopprincessdi: 'they got toothpaste everywhere', and Grace's audience searches "toothpaste tablets for travel").
- Doubt that tablets work like paste: "do toothpaste tablets work" (4 sources), "do toothpaste tablets foam like regular toothpaste", "how do you literally chew? I think I can only chew".
- Ingredient worry: "is hydroxyapatite toothpaste safe" (4 sources), "does hydroxyapatite toothpaste have fluoride".
- Travel: "toothpaste tablets during travel", "toothpaste tablets travel case".

## Communication style
Casual, curious, a little suspicious ("Do these ACTUALLY work??? I need ASAP!", "ok this is actually genius why didnt i think of this"). Buyers describe the feel in plain words: "Chew a bit and they start to foam. Brush like regular paste." and "left a nice tingling aftertaste". No dental jargon beyond the one ingredient name they already search.

## What it means for the script
- Open on the mints confusion (they really do mistake them) and show the tablet at frame 0.
- Pain = the tube mess and the leaking bag, shown, not lectured.
- Answer "does it foam / how do I use it" in one line: chew, wet brush, foams like paste.
- Name the ingredient once with "no fluoride", the listing's words; no whitening or enamel claims (listing doesn't make them).
- Real urgency: holiday travel, TSA (not a liquid). CTA: one for home, one for the carry-on.
