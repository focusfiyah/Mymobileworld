# Research: Christmas Fridge Magnets (Grace), toddler activity angle

## Product differentiation
A full magnetic Christmas scene, not a single magnet: a big flat cartoon tree (two 25 cm halves, about 20 inches tall), a peeking Santa, a peeking reindeer, a snowman, round ornaments and seven wrapped gifts, all flat die-cut magnets with a white border (product_truth.json). Competitors: the Warmtime Party set (13,335 sold, same design family), LeEi 24 pcs, 12-piece ornament-only sets, and the new vinyl "music player" fridge magnets. Ours is rated higher (4.8 vs 4.5) and is a whole tree kids can decorate, not loose stickers.

## Target buyer
Moms of 1 to 4 year olds at home in December, and grandmas buying for the grandkids' visits (research/audience.md). The situation: cooking or cleaning in the kitchen with a toddler underfoot, and a real tree they keep grabbing.

## Pain points and desires
Pain: "how to keep toddlers busy" while mom gets things done, and the real tree that toddlers are not allowed to touch. Desire: a Christmas activity at home that the toddler can do again and again, that holds their attention ("a good chunk of time numerous times throughout the day") and that looks festive in the kitchen, so the kid feels part of decorating.

## Buyer motivation
Christmas is weeks away and decorating starts now; parents want a memory ("a sweet little holiday tradition", @hannahv2246) and some peace in the kitchen. Grandparents want something ready for when the kids come over.

Details in research/coach.md.

## Objections and purchase justification
- "TikTok ENLARGES EVERYTHING ... AS BIG AS A FINGER": the tree is about 20 inches tall (size chart), Santa 7.5 in, gifts 3.8 in; show it next to a toddler's hand.
- "No magnets will stay" on a silver fridge: it works on anything a magnet sticks to (listing: fridges, metal doors, filing cabinets); show a second surface (dishwasher, as @heyitsmichellemarie did).
- "are magnets safe for toddlers": the listing makes no safety claim, so we claim none; show a grown-up nearby and the big pieces.
- Will it last: reviews say "how durable the magnets are", listing "reusable ... retain vibrant colors".

## Content opportunities
Toddler's small hand from frame 0 putting the star on top; the "their own tree" contrast (real tree they can't touch vs the fridge tree they can); on-off-on loop shot; scale shot (toddler hand next to the tree); grandma's fridge as the second-unit beat; a kitchen moment (mom stirring a pot while the toddler decorates beside her).

## Overall verdict
Parents buy it as a December toddler activity that gives them a few minutes in the kitchen and gives the kid their own tree to decorate. Lead with the toddler-in-the-kitchen moment, prove the size, close on a second set for grandma's.

### The toddler's own tree is the hook.
A tree the toddler is allowed to touch, decorated from frame 0 by a small hand.
