## Lessons used
- Frame 0 = a hand or small hand putting a piece on a fridge (every winner).
- The kid owning "their own little Christmas tree" is the line that sells (hannahv2246, thefoundbymama caption).
- AI versions with a toddler from behind reach 50-150K: no face needed.
- Buyers doubt size and stainless fridges in comments: answer size in words, show it on a fridge.
