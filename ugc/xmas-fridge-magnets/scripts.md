# Christmas Fridge Magnets V1 script (Grace VO, about 30 s)

Hook: You know that moment when you're trying to cook and your toddler won't leave the kitchen?
Pain: And right now, the big tree is the one thing they can't touch.
Solution: So give them their own. A magnet Christmas tree for the fridge, with Santa, a reindeer and little presents. On, off, and on again.
Objection: And it's big. The tree is almost twenty inches tall.
Honest line: People say it keeps their toddler busy for a good chunk of the day. Only problem? Now the grown-ups want a turn.
Urgency and close: Christmas is only weeks away. Grab one for your fridge and one for grandma's. It's in the orange cart.
