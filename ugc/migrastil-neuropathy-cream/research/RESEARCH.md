## Product differentiation
A 4 oz cobalt-blue jar of body cream sold for feet, legs and hands, labelled "Gentle, Non-Burning Relief". The ingredient list has no menthol, camphor, capsaicin or lidocaine: it is aloe, organic shea butter, jojoba, olive, almond and coconut oils, vitamin E and essential oils of Roman chamomile, rosemary, ginger, eucalyptus, lavender, marjoram, clove bud and ravensara. The listing says it "sinks right in without leaving any residue" and "You won't smell like a medicine cabinet", made in the USA, HSA/FSA eligible (title). Competitors on TikTok Shop lean on menthol: Hrvsta "with Menthol" (7,953 sold), Earth Essentials "High Menthol ... Camphor" (1,820), Raitera "Menthol" (1,741). Migrastil is the category leader at 109,295 sold (4.3, 7,075 reviews), 13x the next cream.

## Target buyer
Adults 45+ whose feet burn or tingle in bed, many diabetic, plus grown kids buying for a parent ("I got this for my mom and she loves it"). A large Spanish-speaking share. The moment it matters is lying down at night: they search "tingling feet at night", "how to get rid of tingling feet at night", "nerve pain feet burning", "do neuropathy creams work".

## Pain points and desires
Pain: "the burning keep me up all night", "mines us horrible at night", "I've tried everything, nothing works", and creams that make it worse: "Majority of this stuff wakes my neuropathy up". Desire: to get into bed without dreading it and sleep ("best sleep I than got in years", "I slept like a baby" in the 1.3M video), soft feet ("Made my feet nice. ## Buyer motivation
Every night it comes back, so the need is daily, not someday. The practical push: a gentle cream they can use every bedtime without the burn of menthol rubs, from the one everyone on their feed is talking about (109K sold). For the gift buyers, the holidays are close and a parent will not buy it for themselves.
