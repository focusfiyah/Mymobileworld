Flashlight V2 · Gemini kit

9 stills for Grace's flashlight video, built from your real photos: light blue-grey body, panel then power button, dark metal end cap with the Smiling Shark logo, 15.7 cm with the clip.

How to do each shot

  1. Save the 6 images below once (press and hold an image, then Save). They replace every earlier set.
  2. For each shot, open a new Gemini chat and pick the image model (Nano Banana Pro if it offers a choice).
  3. Upload that shot's images in the order shown, tap Copy prompt, paste and send.
  4. Send the picture back here. I'll check it at 2x and do any free fixes. A Gemini mark in the corner is fine; I crop it.
