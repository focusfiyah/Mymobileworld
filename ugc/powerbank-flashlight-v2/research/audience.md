# Audience: clip-on power bank flashlight (Grace), 2026-10-21

Data: `research/audience/questions.md` (2,597 autocomplete phrases from Google, Bing, YouTube, Amazon, TikTok),
`research/audience/comments.md` (188 TikTok comments from 6 winner videos), `research/audience/voc.md` + exa1/exa2 (Reddit).

## Who
Not flashlight hobbyists. Grace's viewers: women and moms who keep a light just in case in the car, the purse, the backpack or the camping bin, and people buying a small gift.
Searches lean practical and car/keys based:
- "rechargeable flashlight for car" (4 sources)
- "keychain flashlight phone charger" (3 sources)
- "car keychain flashlight"
- "keychain flashlight and phone charger"
Winner comments show who buys it for others: "My mom got one it actually amazing"

## Pain point experience
The real moment is the dark plus a dying phone. People use the phone as the light and lose both:
- "my phone was about to die but i needed to use the flashlight. it died while the flashlight was on"
- "I pulled it out to use it as a light, and it was dead."
- "What do you do when the power goes out and your phone is dead?"
Hobbyists say the same in their words, and hate carrying two things:
- "I wish the charging port on flashlights could also provide 5V output power to charge your phone in a pinch."
- "I like my 20000 mAh power bank but it is heavy and bulky"
Buyers' first questions are practical:
- "Would be this good for a power out?"
- "How long does the battery last"
- "can power bank flashlight charge phone"
- "are power bank flashlights good for camping"

## Communication style
Short, funny, low-stakes. The top comment on the 13.7M winner is a joke ("Bought it. Still can’t find my dad."), and the
fear-of-the-dark lines get likes ("Would legit remove all my fears at night"). The winners talk plainly and show the light
right away, in the dark, at frame 0 (@zin5bamu's thirty-versus-ten-dollar flashlight test; @macson102's power-went-out
caption, see coach_research.md). Nobody quotes lumens to win; the one spec that lands is that you can focus it.
They are skeptical of cheap lights: "I bought this thinking it was bad but it's really good like actually".

## What it means for the script
1. Open on the moment, not the specs: the phone light dying in the dark (car, parking lot, power out, tent).
2. Answer the buyer questions on screen, in order: it's rechargeable (USB-C, same as your phone), it charges your phone,
   it clips on so your hands are free, the side panel lights the whole tent, you can focus the beam.
3. No lumens, no hours, no phone-charge counts (not on the listing; product_facts.md).
4. Real urgency: clocks go back on Sunday, November 1 (2026), so it's dark before five; plus camping/holiday gifting.
5. CTA sells more units: one on the car keys, one in the backpack / one for mom (the "My mom got one" buyer).
6. Light on at frame 0 (all three winners), but bright and neutral per Ralph: blue-hour dusk, not a black night.
