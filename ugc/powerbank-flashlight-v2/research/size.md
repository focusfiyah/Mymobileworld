# Flashlight size (TINEKO 3000mAh power bank flashlight), researched 2026-10-22

## What the sources say
- Maker's dimensions: NOT FOUND. No TINEKO listing on the web; the TikTok Shop listing screenshots (refs/listing_*.jpg)
  give no size; TikTok Shop product pages need a captcha. Web searches (4, see the chat) found only other brands.
- Comparable product with a published spec: Xiaomi Mi 3250mAh Power Bank Flashlight, 120 x 25.5 x 25.5 mm, 103 g
  (mi.com/uk/3250mah-mi-power-bank-flashlight/specs). Same class: one 18650-size cell (18 mm x 65 mm) in a metal tube.

## Measured from the real listing pixels (refs/product_sheet.png, front view cell)
- Body width 185 px, body length (lens end to the hook root) 644 px -> body = 3.5 x its width.
- Clip adds about 138 px -> whole thing with clip = 4.2 x its width.
- Anchor: a 3000mAh cell is almost always an 18650 (18 mm wide); a metal shell with walls needs ~22-24 mm.
- Result (estimate): body about 2.3 x 2.3 cm square, about 8 cm long; about 10 cm with the clip; weight about 100 g.
- Not usable for scale: the USB-A port in the sheet render (not to scale), the backpack inset (product pasted oversized).
- Cross-check: last week's approved r4 (out/powerbank_flashlight_r4.mp4) shows it about 1.3 finger lengths long in
  Grace's hand and about 1.5 finger widths wide: matches.

## Prompt block (goes FIRST in every product still and clip prompt)
SIZE: a small keychain flashlight, about 8 cm long and 2.3 cm wide (as long as an adult index finger, as wide as a
thumb), about 10 cm with the clip. Held in one hand it fits across the palm with the lens end and the clip sticking out
past the fingers; it is never longer than the hand is wide. It is about two-thirds the length of a phone and much
narrower. Hanging from a tent loop or a backpack strap it is a small object, about twice as tall as the strap is wide
(a 5 cm strap). Never bigger than this, never a big torch.
