# Super Blanky wearable blanket: 3 scripts for Grace (v3, 2026-10-05, new process)

Three talking-head scripts, about 30 seconds each. Built from what buyers ask in the comments: how big is it, can I wash it, what is it made of, is it just a robe. The blanket is on you or in your hands the whole time, and every video shows your arms sliding into the pocket sleeves.

[Brackets] = what to do. Film the hook, body and CTA as separate takes, 2 or 3 takes of each hook. Say the hook exactly as written.

## Script 1: The questions everyone asks

Hook H113 (Question). Modeled on the top buyer questions in the comments: size, washing, fabric, "so a robe?".

**HOOK** [hold the blanket open to full width]

"My biggest question before ordering was how big it really is."

**BODY** [stand next to it, show the length against your body]

"It's seventy-one inches wide and sixty inches tall. [Say how tall you are and where it hits on you.] People ask if it's just a robe. It's not. It has pockets inside for your arms, and nothing goes over your head."

[rub the fleece, then hold it up to the light]

"It's soft fleece, and it goes right in the washer. It stays soft after a lot of washes. Fair warning, you won't want to take it off."

**CTA**

"It gets colder every week from here. Get one for the couch and one for your desk at work. It's in the orange cart."

Other hooks to film (word for word):

## Script 2: Stays on

Hook H061 (Identity). Modeled on @neilaswgza6 (2.3M views, always cold) and @evenmorevmc (falls off, doesn't fall off). Comments back it: "I need this I'm always cold".

**HOOK** [on the couch, blanket already on, arms in the pockets]

"This is for the people who always have a blanket on, even in October."

**BODY** [take your arms out, let a plain throw slide off as you reach for your phone]

"You get cozy, you reach for your phone, and the blanket slides right off. Then your arms are cold anyway."

[put the Super Blanky on, arms into the pockets, stand up, walk to grab a snack, sit back down]

"This one has pockets inside for your arms. Stand up, still on. Grab a snack, still on. Your hands stay free. It has four point eight stars from over five hundred reviews. People say their teenager grabbed it and ran off with it."

**CTA**

"Cold nights are here. Get one for you and one for whoever would steal yours. It's in the orange cart."

## Script 3: A warm gift for mom

Hook H056 (Gift). Comfort gift only, no health claims. October is Breast Cancer Awareness Month. Grace's blanket is royal blue (more royal than navy): use that one and say nothing about its color. Warm and calm delivery. Comments back it: "Perfect Christmas gift".

**HOOK** [to camera, blanket folded in your arms]

"This immediately made me think of my mom."

**BODY** [unfold it, then slide your arms into the pockets]

"A regular blanket slips every time she reaches for her phone or a cup of tea. This is Super Blanky. It has pockets inside for her arms, so it stays on her shoulders and her hands stay free."

[hold up the fleece]

"Nothing goes over her head. It's soft fleece and it goes right in the washer. Fair warning, she may never give it back."
