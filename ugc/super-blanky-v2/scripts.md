# Super Blanky wearable blanket: 3 scripts for Grace (v2, 2026-10-05)

Three talking-head scripts, about 30 seconds each. The blanket is in your hands or on you the whole time, and every video shows your arms sliding into the pocket sleeves.

[Brackets] = what to do. Film the hook, body and CTA as separate takes, 2 or 3 takes of each hook. Say the hook exactly as written.

## Script 1: Always cold

Hook H061 (Identity). Modeled on @neilaswgza6 (2.3M views): "That one person who is always saying they're cold."

**HOOK** [on the couch, blanket already on, arms in the pockets]

"This is for the people who always have a blanket on, even in October."

**BODY** [take the arms out, let a plain throw slide off as you reach for your phone]

"You get cozy on the couch, reach for your phone, and the blanket slides right off. Then your arms are cold anyway."

[put the Super Blanky back on, slide your arms into the pockets]

"This is Super Blanky. It has pockets inside for your arms, so it wraps around you like a robe and your hands stay free. It's soft fleece and it's huge, seventy-one by sixty. And it goes right in the washer. The only problem is you won't want to take it off."

**CTA**

"It gets colder every week from here. Grab one for the couch and one for your desk at work. It's in the orange cart."
