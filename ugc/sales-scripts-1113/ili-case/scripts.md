**SCRIPT** "If you also hate when you dig through your purse for your glasses, look at this. This one holds your sunglasses and your readers, so you stop grabbing the wrong pair. Each side snaps shut, so neither pair falls out in your bag. Over eleven thousand sold. Thanksgiving trips are around the corner, then Christmas shopping and holiday parties. Grab one for your purse and one for your carry-on. It's in the orange cart."
B-roll:
- Hook -> hand digging in an open purse, pulls out loose sunglasses (new shot), cut to the snap close-up from frame 0 of her winner
- "holds your sunglasses and your readers, so you stop grabbing the wrong pair" -> both flaps opened side on (her winner), sunglasses slide into one side, readers into the other
- "Each side snaps shut, so neither pair falls out in your bag" -> fingers press each snap closed, close-up, then the closed case tipped into a purse
- "Over eleven thousand sold" -> closed case held up against the shelf, slow turn
- "Thanksgiving trips ... Christmas shopping and holiday parties" -> case drops into a purse on the way out the door
- "one for your purse and one for your carry-on" -> case into a purse, then into a carry-on pocket
- Close -> case in hand, flaps shut
On-screen text option (only if Ralph says yes): "less digging through your purse now"

## Talk to camera

**SCRIPT** "You know that moment when you need your readers and you only brought your sunglasses? This one has two sides. Sunglasses here, readers here, and each flap snaps shut, so both pairs stay put. It's real leather, so it looks nice when you pull it out at the register. Thanksgiving is around the corner, then holiday shopping and Christmas dinners. Grab one for your purse and one for your car. It's in the orange cart."
Delivery: Grace at the bathroom shelf from her winner, case closed in one hand. Hook: holds up sunglasses in the other hand, a little fed up. "two sides": opens both flaps to camera. "here ... here": slides sunglasses in one side, readers in the other, snaps each flap. "real leather ... at the register": runs a thumb over the pebbled leather, then pulls it out of her purse like she is at a counter. "Thanksgiving ... Christmas dinners": drops it into her purse. "one for your purse and one for your car": holds up two fingers. Close: smiles to camera.
