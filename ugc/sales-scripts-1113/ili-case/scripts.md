# ili Double Flap Case: scripts (2026-11-13)
Grace's 8 s hands-only video sold on "double the storage" and "less digging through your purse for your glasses"; both scripts keep that purse pain, the snap close-up and both flaps opening, and add a voice.

## Voiceover
**HOOK** (H008) "If you also hate when you dig through your purse for your glasses, look at this."
**SCRIPT** "If you also hate when you dig through your purse for your glasses, look at this. You carry two cases and still grab the wrong pair. This one holds your sunglasses and your readers. Each side snaps shut. Over eleven thousand sold. The hard part is picking a color. Grab one for your purse and one for your carry-on before Thanksgiving. It's in the orange cart."
B-roll:
- Hook -> hand digging in an open purse, pulls out loose sunglasses (new shot), cut to the snap close-up from frame 0 of her winner
- "two cases ... wrong pair" -> two loose pairs on the bathroom shelf, hand picks the sunglasses
- "holds your sunglasses and your readers" -> both flaps opened side on (her winner), sunglasses slide into one side, readers into the other
- "Each side snaps shut" -> fingers press each snap closed, close-up
- "Over eleven thousand sold" -> closed case held up against the shelf, slow turn
- "picking a color" -> sage case on the shelf (other colours only if she has them; otherwise stay on the sage case)
- "purse ... carry-on before Thanksgiving" -> case drops into a purse, then into a carry-on pocket
- Close -> case in hand, flaps shut
On-screen text option (only if Ralph says yes): "less digging through your purse now"

## Talk to camera
**HOOK** (H062) "You know that moment when you need your readers and you only brought your sunglasses?"
**SCRIPT** "You know that moment when you need your readers and you only brought your sunglasses? Or both pairs are loose in your bag. Girls, this one has two sides. Sunglasses here, readers here, and each flap snaps shut. It's real leather. The only problem? Now I want every color. Grab one for your purse and one for your car before the holidays. It's in the orange cart."
Delivery: Grace at the bathroom shelf from her winner, case closed in one hand. Hook: holds up sunglasses in the other hand, a little fed up. "loose in your bag": tips both pairs out of a purse onto the shelf. "two sides": opens both flaps to camera. "here ... here": slides sunglasses in one side, readers in the other, snaps each flap. "real leather": runs a thumb over the pebbled leather. "every color": a grin, shrug. CTA: drops it into her purse. Close: smiles to camera.
On-screen text option (only if Ralph says yes): "wrong glasses in my purse again"
