# ili New York Double Flap Eyeglass Case: product research for Grace (2026-11-13)
Sources: TikTok Shop listing (research/listing_raw.txt), TikTok Shop search pages (research/coach/shop-*.json), TikTok videos and comments pulled 2026-11-13 (research/coach/, 135 comments from 4 more niche videos), web search. All numbers pulled 2026-11-13.

## 1. Understand the product
Slim leather case with two compartments side by side. Each side has its own flap and snap. Sunglasses go in one side, readers or prescription glasses in the other. 6.75 x 2.75 x 1 inches, 100% leather inside and out, soft (not a hard shell), 21 colours.

What makes it stand out on TikTok Shop: it is the only real leather double case on the "double eyeglass case" search page. The others are faux leather, EVA hard shells or zip pouches (DL Department Store 524 sold, QINA 137, YG Home Goods 115). It is the number two seller on the whole "glasses case" page (11,208 sold, 4.8 stars, 967 reviews), behind only the Fintie desk stand (42,282 sold). It is also priced near the top of both pages.

Feature → benefit buyers care about:
- Two sides → both pairs go in one case, no second case in the purse
- Snap on each flap → neither pair slides out when the bag tips
- 1 inch thick, soft → fits a small purse, a car console, a carry-on pocket
- Real leather → looks like an accessory, not a pharmacy case; buyers mention the smell
- 21 colours → match it to your glasses or your bag; easy gift

## 2. Target buyer
- Women, mostly 35+, who wear readers or prescription glasses AND sunglasses every day. Every review and comment we found is from a woman.
- Situations: switching pairs in the car, at the register, reading a menu; makeup in the car (review K**4); travel days with a carry-on.
- Gift buyers: moms, aunts, a travel friend ("I will be gifting one & keeping one!!", o**7).
- What makes them spend: being tired of the daily swap, wanting something that looks nice, and the holidays (Thanksgiving 2026-11-26, Christmas 2026-12-25).

## 6. Honest verdict
- Selling potential: HIGH for the right buyer. 11.2K sold and 4.8 stars on this exact listing, number two on the whole glasses case page. The ceiling is price: it costs far more than the faux leather and EVA double cases next to it.
- Strongest selling points: both pairs in one slim case; real leather; colour choice.
- Objections: price; will big sunglasses fit; is it bulky; does it look old fashioned.
- Competitive advantages: only real leather double case on the page; proven sales; gift worthy look.
- Product market appeal: narrow but real; two pair wearers feel it daily, sunglasses only buyers pick cheap pouches.
- Best audience: women 35+ who wear readers or glasses plus sunglasses, and their gift buyers.
- Strongest angle: the wrong pair moment (readers vs sunglasses), closed with keep one, gift one.

## 7. The four elements
- HOOK: "You only brought your sunglasses again?" A moment every two pair wearer knows; almost no video uses it.
- DESIRE: Both pairs in one pretty leather case in your colour. Buyers love the colours and the leather smell.
- OBJECTION KILLER: Show both flaps open, a full size pair sliding in, each side snapping shut. It kills the fit and fall out worries on camera.
- PURCHASE JUSTIFICATION: Real leather you use every single day, one for you and one as a gift. Daily use makes the higher price feel earned.
