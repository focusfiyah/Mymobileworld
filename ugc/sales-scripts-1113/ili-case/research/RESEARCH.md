# Research: ili New York Double Flap Eyeglass Case (Grace)
Built from research/product_facts.md, research/coach/*, research/audience/* and Grace's selling video (research/winner/), 2026-11-13.

## Product differentiation
Two compartments side by side in one smooth leather case (100% leather inside and out), each with its own flap and snap, 6.75 x 2.75 x 1 inches. On the TikTok Shop "glasses case" page it is the number two seller (11,208 sold, 4.8 stars, 967 reviews), behind only the Fintie desk stand (42K). Most double cases on the page are hard EVA shells or PU leather pouches (QINA dual cases 137 and 29 sold, CaiCai 2-pack PU pouches 5.8K) and the viral sunglasses pouches are single-pair PU with an elastic opening. This one is real leather, slim enough for a purse, and comes in 21 colours. The scratch-protection line on the listing is AI-generated text, so we do not promise it.

## Target buyer
Women, mostly 35+, who carry readers or prescription glasses plus sunglasses every day, in a purse, a car or a carry-on. Buyers say "Just what I need for my readers and sunglasses" and "Perfect for those who have to wear glasses and also have sunglasses for the sun". A second buyer gets it as a gift ("I will be gifting one & keeping one").

## Pain points and desires
Pain: two cases or loose glasses in the purse, digging for the right pair, grabbing the wrong one, lenses rolling around ("how to get rid of sunglasses scratches"), glasses falling off a lap in the car. "Switching glasses gets tiring." Desire: both pairs in one place, easy to grab, and a case that looks nice: "Love being able to carry readers & sunnies together!", "Smells amazing and is leather."

## Buyer motivation
Now: Thanksgiving travel (2026-11-26, under two weeks away) and Christmas trips, when people pack sunglasses and readers into a carry-on. Holiday gift lists too (Grace used #tiktokshopblackfriday and #discoveryourgift). Emotionally: feeling put together instead of scrambling at the register or in the car.

## Big Fish (why ladder)
Their words: "Switching glasses gets tiring", "avoid endless searching", "They have fallen off my lap many times" (audience/voc.md).
- She carries two pairs and digs through her purse to find the right one.
- Why does that hurt? She pulls out the sunglasses when she needs to read, at the register or in the car, and the lenses are loose and scuffed.
- Why does that matter? It happens in front of people, and she feels frazzled.
- Why does that matter to her? She feels like she can never keep it together.
Biggest feeling: "I'm always scrambling." The enemy to blame instead: a purse that swallows everything and one case per pair.

## Overall verdict
People buy it to stop scrambling for the right glasses: one slim leather case, two sides, both pairs. Lead with the purse-digging or wrong-pair moment, show both flaps and the snap, prove it with 11K sold, close on holiday trips and a second one for the car or carry-on.
