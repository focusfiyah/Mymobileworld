# Audience: ili New York Double Flap Eyeglass Case (2026-11-13)
Data: research/audience/questions.md (452 autocomplete phrases), comments.md (6 TikTok comments), voc.md (buyer reviews, blog reader comments).

## Who
Women, mostly 35+, who carry two pairs of glasses every day: readers or prescription glasses for close up, sunglasses for outside. Buyers say it plainly: "Perfect for those who have to wear glasses and also have sunglasses for the sun", "Just what I need for my readers and sunglasses". They keep both in a purse or a car. A second group buys it as a gift: "I will be gifting one & keeping one!!" It is unisex on the listing, but every review and comment we found came from women.

## Pain point experience
Two pairs means two cases or none. "I ordered this to keep from having to carry two eye glass cases." Swapping wears them out: "Switching glasses gets tiring, one cute eyeglass case for both makes it easier for sure!" The pair they need is lost in the bag ("avoid endless searching"), or it falls: "They have fallen off my lap many times." Under it sits the worry about scratches; they search "how to get rid of sunglasses scratches" and "how to get rid of eyeglasses scratches", and a reader is glad "I don't have to worry about them falling out and getting dirty, or scratched." The top shop video in the niche says the same: "No more digging through my bag" (ys.findcozyhome, 1.3M), and @hollyroquemore1's caption: "I no longer have to dig for them and guess what case they are in".

## Communication style
Casual and short, girlfriend talk: "sunnies", "readers", "Oh my goodness, I need this color!", "what a great case for sunglasses". They love colour and the leather smell ("Smells amazing and is leather"). They describe the use, not the specs: "It holds my sunnies in one side and my progressives in the other." They search plain how-to phrases: "how to carry glasses and sunglasses", "double glasses case slim".

## What it means for the script
- Hook on the moment: digging in the purse for the right pair, or grabbing the sunglasses when you needed the readers. Never "if you're over 40": no age qualifier (outsider test).
- Say "readers" and "sunglasses" by name, the way buyers do. "Sunnies" is fine in the talk-to-camera.
- Proof: two sides, each snaps shut, one slim case; 11.2K sold. Show it, do not list specs.
- Scratches only as the pain (lenses loose in a purse), never a promise that it prevents scratches.
- Gift only as the more-units close ("keep one, gift one", from a real review) before the holidays, since the scripts are not about gifting.
