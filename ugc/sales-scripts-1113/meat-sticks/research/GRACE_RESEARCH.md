## 1. Understand the product
One hard box: 10 sticks (3 Original beef, 2 Hot Honey beef, 3 Pink Himalayan Salt turkey, 2 Pineapple Chili Lime chicken) plus 1 bag of Sweet Heat beef jerky (whole muscle beef, slow cooked in small batches over real wood smoke). Items come wrapped in kraft paper. TikTok exclusive. Maker: Anthem Snacks, Bozeman, Montana, founded by former Green Beret Nate Kouhana (listing). KPAX and KTVH report it was named Montana's Veteran Small Business of the Year (web search).

**What makes it different (TikTok Shop search, sold counts):**
- IOWA SMOKEHOUSE (23,947 sold): one flavor, bulk bag. No gift look.
- Righteous Felon variety (4,774) and Paleovalley (4,746): sticks only, no jerky, grass fed is their pitch.
- Mythical Meats D&D pack (6,872): fun theme, no story of the maker.
- JerkyDotCom gift basket (3,757): gift box, but a generic sampler with no maker story.
- ANTHEM: the only one in these searches with sticks AND jerky in one box, three meats, a veteran founder, and paper wrapped presentation.

## 6. Honest verdict
- **Selling potential: Medium, High through December 25.** 4.9 stars, 219 reviews, 1,564 sold is solid but small next to Iowa Smokehouse (23,947) and the crunchy jerky leaders (hundreds of thousands). It costs more than most single meat stick listings, so it sells as a gift, not a cheap snack.
- **Product market appeal:** strong for gifting, average as an everyday snack.
- **Strongest selling points:** sticks plus jerky in one box, paper wrapped presentation, veteran founder, four flavors.
- **Barriers:** price next to bulk sticks, "will it be dry", no ingredient list on the listing, "is it worth it for 11 items".
- **Competitive advantage:** the only box in the searches that is both a variety and a ready gift with a maker story.
- **Best audience:** women buying for the man who is hard to shop for.
- **Strongest angle:** the stocking stuffer gift for him, opened on camera.
