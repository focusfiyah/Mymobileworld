# Research: Asvivid Reversible Mock Neck Sweatshirt (Grace)

Built from research/product_facts.md, research/coach/ (shop, discover, video, viral board 2026-11-13), research/audience/ and Grace's own selling video (research/winner/).

## Product differentiation
A plain, solid-color mock neck sweatshirt that is reversible: wear it one way, turn it around for a second look ("Reversible design offers two stylish looks in one", "2-Way Wear"). Loose fit with drop shoulders, soft, pull-on, made for fall and winter daily wear, eight colors. Competitors: the TikTok Shop mock neck leader FISOEW (49,283 sold, 4.4 stars) is one-sided; American Eagle's Everyday Luxe mock neck drives the big videos but buyers complain it is expensive and the neck is tight; the other reversible sweatshirts on TikTok Shop are game-day graphics (Mntaor Bama, Parke) or hoodie blankets, not a plain everyday piece. Ours: 4.8 stars, 317 sold.

## Target buyer
Women who wear sweatshirts most days from fall into winter, over leggings, jeans or cargo pants, for errands, school runs and casual days. They watch mock neck try-ons, ask "what size did you get?", and want more than one color. The situation: mid November, the same few sweatshirts on repeat, cold weather starting.

## Pain points and desires
Pain: closet boredom ("I open my closet, and I can't find anything to wear"), wearing the same thing over and over, unsure about fit ("Do you feel like this is oversized or TTS?"), tight mock necks on other brands, name-brand prices. Desire: feel like they have a new outfit without buying a pile of new clothes, look put together while staying comfy ("soft and comfy", "Works great with leggings!"), and get the color they love.

## Buyer motivation
Cold weather is here and Black Friday shopping starts (Grace tagged #tiktokshopblackfriday). A reversible piece feels like more value for one purchase ("it's like getting 2 shirts for the price of one!"). It is an easy everyday upgrade and a good second color buy.

## Objections and purchase justification
- "Is it oversized or true to size?" -> listing: loose fit, drop shoulders, relaxed comfort; full-length shot shows it on her (her size only if true).
- "Is the neck tight?" -> mock neck is described as cozy warmth; the close-up shows the neck sitting loose on her.
- "Thick or thin?" -> not on the listing; do not claim. Say soft (title).
- "Is it in style?" -> mock necks are all over TikTok this fall (AE videos 222K to 597K views).
- "Worth it?" -> two looks in one sweatshirt, 4.8 stars, 317 sold.

## Overall verdict
People buy this because one plain sweatshirt gives them two looks, so their closet feels new without buying more. Lead with the closet boredom pain, prove it with the turn, then the cozy mock neck and loose fit, 4.8 stars, and eight colors.
