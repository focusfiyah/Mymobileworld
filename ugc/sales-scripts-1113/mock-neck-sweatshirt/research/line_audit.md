# Line audit: Asvivid Reversible Mock Neck Sweatshirt (Grace script method, PLAYBOOK §4, 2026-11-13)
GREEN = the video gets worse without it. YELLOW = keep, could be sharper. RED = cut. Domino test: each line sets up the next.

## Voiceover (H062)
Buyer: women who wear sweatshirts most days from fall into winter (broad, no qualifiers)
Pain: closet boredom, "nothing feels new" (Big Fish last rung)
Connection: one sweatshirt gives two looks, so the closet feels new without buying more
Outsider test: no age, mom or size qualifier in the hook; "you" opener
Common enemy: wearing the same sweatshirt every week (the closet, not her)
Contradiction check: no claim that it changes her whole wardrobe; only "two looks", shown by the turn
Repetition pass: "new look" in the draft echoed "nothing feels new" in the hook; changed to "two looks"
Pain points: 2
Selling points: 3
| Line | Mark | Why / shot that proves it |
|---|---|---|
| You know that moment when you open your closet and nothing feels new? | GREEN | Hook, biggest pain (voc "I open my closet, and I can't find anything to wear"). Shot: frame 0 wearing it |
| Same sweatshirt, every week. | GREEN | Pain beat of its own (PLAYBOOK §4). Shot: hands at the hem |
| This one gives you two looks. | GREEN | Connection. Shot: she starts to turn |
| Wear it one way, then turn it around. | GREEN | Selling point 1, proof on screen: the front/back turn (listing "2-Way Wear") |
| The mock neck is cozy. | YELLOW | Selling point 2 (listing "Mock neck adds cozy warmth"). Shot: neck close-up |
| It has 4.8 stars. | GREEN | Selling point 3 = trust proof (listing 4.8, 37 ratings). Shot: full length side |
| The hard part is picking one of eight colors. | GREEN | Sell-side confession, drives a second color (listing: 8 colors) |
| Cold weather is here. | GREEN | Real season (mid November) |
| It's in the orange cart. | GREEN | Close |
Cut: "The loose fit is easy to layer." (RED: the full-length shot already shows it, pushed the VO past 22 s); "Same look, all week" (RED: said the pain twice)

## Talk to camera (H017)
Buyer: women who wear sweatshirts most days from fall into winter
Pain: every sweatshirt feels the same by winter (Big Fish rung 2 to 3)
Connection: reversible, so one sweatshirt is two looks without buying a new one
Outsider test: no qualifiers; "I" opener is a desire she can say as her own (she owns and wears it), not a life story
Common enemy: sweatshirts that only have one look
Contradiction check: no "new wardrobe" claim; only reversible and a second look, shown by the turn
Repetition pass: "look" said in the hook and once in the body (second look), "sweatshirt" once in the body as "they"
Pain points: 1
Selling points: 3
| Line | Mark | Why / shot that proves it |
|---|---|---|
| I want a new look without having to buy a new sweatshirt. | GREEN | Hook (desire + money pain). Full length to camera |
| Girls, by winter they all feel the same. | GREEN | Pain beat, Grace's "girls" voice |
| This one is reversible. | GREEN | Connection (listing "Reversible") |
| Wear it this way, or turn it around for a second look. | GREEN | Selling point 1, proof: the turn in one take |
| The mock neck is cozy and the fit is loose. | YELLOW | Selling point 2, fit fact from the listing (loose fit, drop shoulders); her size only if true |
| It has 4.8 stars. | GREEN | Selling point 3 = trust proof |

| Get it before the real cold hits. | GREEN | Real season urgency, no fake scarcity |
| It's in the orange cart. | GREEN | Close |
Cut: "That's why I love this one." (RED: run-up, delayed the point); "Two looks." (RED: repeated the turn line); "over three hundred sold" (RED: second proof in one line; stars kept)
