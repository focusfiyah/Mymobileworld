## 2. Target buyer
Women who live in sweatshirts from fall into winter: moms on school runs, students, anyone running errands in leggings or cargo pants. They watch mock neck try ons and ask about size (C). What makes them spend: cold weather now, Black Friday week, and feeling bored with what they own. Proof they rebuy colors: "Awesome sweater, will buy again in other colors!" and "Would like to get more colors." (R).

## 6. Honest verdict
- Selling potential: **Medium.** Strong rating and buyer words, real season timing, an open angle. Held back by small proof (37 ratings, 317 sold), a quiet visual (plain fabric, the second look must be shown clearly) and a crowded mock neck field led by AE and FISOEW.
- Product market appeal: good; mock necks are everywhere on TikTok this fall, reversible adds a reason to pick this one.
- Strongest selling points: two looks in one; fit buyers call "just enough oversized"; legging friendly; colors.
- Objections: what size, how thick, is the neck tight, does the second side look different enough, is it a cheap dupe.
- Competitive advantage: the only plain everyday reversible mock neck found on TikTok Shop.
- Best audience: women 25 to 45 in leggings and sweatshirts all season (age range is my read, not data).
- Strongest angle: **closet boredom fixed by the turn: one sweatshirt, two outfits.**
