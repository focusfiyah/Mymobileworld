# Audience: Asvivid Reversible Mock Neck Sweatshirt (2026-11-13)
Data: research/audience/questions.md (1,050 autocomplete phrases, grace/audience.py), research/audience/comments.md (206 TikTok comments from 8 mock neck / reversible selling videos), research/audience/voc.md (reviews + web quotes with URLs).

## Who
Women who live in sweatshirts from fall to winter and want to look put together without trying hard: moms, students, anyone who runs errands in leggings or cargo pants. They follow mock neck try-on videos and want every color ("I can't keep up with all the mock necks I want them all"; "Mocknecks are the vibe when you can find a good one"). The searchers are still unsure the style is current ("are mock neck sweatshirts actually in style right now").

## Pain point experience
- Closet fatigue by mid fall: "I open my closet, and I can't find anything to wear." Repeating the same sweatshirt gets boring: "I was bored with my outfit the day before and I am bored with it this day too".
- Fit worry, the top question in the comments: "Do you feel like this is oversized or TTS?", "These run huge!! Def size down to the ppl who are wanting to get it", search "do mock neck sweatshirts run big".
- Mock neck worry: "the neck is so tight" (comment on a competitor).
- Fabric worry: "Is it a thick sweatshirt or is it more thin?"
- Price pain on the name brands: "why so expensive for american eagle" (553 likes).
- They want to see it all: "We need a further away look so we can see the bottom of it", "Can you do a video of how you would style it?!"

## Communication style
Short, excited, girlfriend talk: "girl", "so cute", "It looks so so comfy", lots of caps and emojis. The value line that buyers use for reversible pieces is simple: "It's reversible so it's like getting 2 shirts for the price of one!" Comfort words they use: "soft and comfy", "Works great with leggings!" No technical fabric talk.

## What it means for the script
- Lead with the closet boredom pain ("nothing feels new"), then the connection: one sweatshirt, two looks, so it feels new again. Show the turn (front, back) on that line: that is the proof they ask for.
- Answer fit and look in the full-length shot (loose fit, drop shoulders, covers the hip) with listing words only; Grace's size stays [only if true].
- Use "cozy" for the mock neck (listing) and never "thick" or "fleece" (not on the listing).
- Trust line: 4.8 stars and 317 sold (listing). No price, no brand comparison numbers.
- Close on the color count (eight colors) to drive a second color, then the season and "It's in the orange cart."
