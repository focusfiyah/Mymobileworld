Product description
The Shnuggle Toddler Bath is the perfect follow-on bath for when baby outgrows their baby bath tub.  Extending the fun of bath time and offering your toddler the space and confidence to be more confident in the bath. 
Fits most shower trays and baths, offering plenty of room to play in their own comfy space while saving water at the same time.
Features
·
Contoured base for added comfort and support
·
Foam backrest for warmth and comfort
·
Grippy inside surface 
·
Handy plug to empty or refresh bath water
·
Maximum fill line indicator
Recommended for use when baby has outgrown their baby bath and is confidently sitting.
