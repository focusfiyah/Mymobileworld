## Product differentiation
A one-piece toddler tub made for the stage after the infant tub: soft foam backrest to lean on, a contoured seat with a built-in "bum bump" so the child sits upright, a grippy inside and non-slip feet underneath, a plug and a max fill line. It sits inside the family bathtub or a shower tray and needs far less water than filling the big bath (brand). On the TikTok Shop "toddler bathtub" page (2026-11-13) almost every top seller is a collapsible infant tub with a thermometer (LUCKSONNY 7,159 and 6,185 sold, BabyBond 923, MoreFeel) or a grow-with-me infant tub (frida 918); the Shnuggle is one of the few made only for sitting toddlers, 4.9 stars from 39 reviews, 485 sold. It is the priciest on the page, so the value has to be shown, never priced.

## Target buyer
Moms of a baby who can sit up on her own and has outgrown the infant tub (about 9 months to 3 years), and grandparents who bathe the grandkids ("My grandson loves his new tub", "Very comfortable for my 10 month old grandson"). A second buyer only has a stand-up shower ("We only have showers at our house"; searches "toddler bathtub for stand up shower").

## Pain points and desires
Pain: the infant tub gets tight, the big tub is too big and scary, so the parent kneels over the tub holding the child up ("always with our hands on him") or gets in with them ("I have to jump in the shower with her"); crying at bath time; wasting a full tub of water; tubs bought before that did not work ("The one I got from target is not working out"). Desire: a calm bath where the child sits up happily in her own little tub and the parent can relax a bit: "She immediately jumped in . When it was bath time she didn’t cry at all ."

## Buyer motivation
Now: colder nights (a warm bath before bed), Thanksgiving 2026-11-26 and Christmas trips when families stay at grandma's house, and the moment the infant tub stops fitting (it happens to every parent, any month). Emotionally: wanting bath time to be the easy, sweet part of the night instead of a struggle on your knees.
