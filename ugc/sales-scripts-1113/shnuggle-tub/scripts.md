Why the original worked:
- Hook: "Never have I ever seen a true toddler size bathtub until now" tells parents a whole kind of product exists that they never knew about, and the text "POV: your toddler outgrew their baby tub" names their exact moment.
- Curiosity gap: "a toddler size bathtub? would my kid fit?" opens at 0 s and pays off when her daughter climbs in and sits in it.
- Story: her own daughter outgrew the infant tub but is too small for the big bathtub, then the tub comes out of the box, then each feature with what it does for the child.
- Pacing: about 3.4 words a second, a new shot every 2 to 3 seconds, 3 or 4 cuts in the first 3 seconds, her daughter in the tub at frame 0.
- Payoff: real proof (her toddler in it) plus a real season close (colder nights, a warm bath before bed) and the orange cart.
Mechanism kept: category gap discovery hook (a product made for a stage nobody serves) + the in-between pain + features each said with what they do + real season close.
