# Line audit: Bobbi Brown Prep and Brighten Duo (Grace script method, PLAYBOOK §4, 2026-11-13)
GREEN = the video gets worse without it. YELLOW = keep, could be sharper. RED = cut. Domino test: each line sets up the next.

## Voiceover
Buyer: women who wear concealer and have dark circles (broad, no age or mom qualifier)
Pain: looking tired because concealer cracks on dry under-eyes (Big Fish: "I look tired and older no matter what I do")
Connection: the cracking comes from dry, unprepped skin, and this duo starts with a hydrating base before the corrector
Outsider test: hook has no qualifiers; "your" opener; anyone who wears concealer is in
Common enemy: dry skin and skipping prep, not her and not her concealer
Contradiction check: no "removes" or "covers" dark circles; only "helps neutralize" (listing). "makeup goes on smooth" = listing "smooth, makeup-ready base". "you look more tired" is the pain, not a claim about the product
Repetition pass: "prep" said once in the body; "dark circles" once; "concealer" only in the hook; "cake/crack" each once
Pain points: 2
Selling points: 3
