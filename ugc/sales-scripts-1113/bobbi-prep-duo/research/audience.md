## Communication style
Girlfriend talk, a little funny and tired of it: "Concealer creasing deserves its own support group", "Under-eye creasing is such a universal struggle." They trust a before-and-after on real skin and a simple step order. The creator who fits this duo best (@carejohnston, 339.2K) blames the prep, not the woman: "your concealer creasing may not actually be your concealer's fault. Sometimes it's all about the under-eye prep."

## What it means for the script
Open on the pain they already feel (looking tired, concealer that cracks) and take the blame off them: the enemy is dry skin and skipping prep. Short trust beat (Bobbi Brown, the official shop, people's words "Makeup didn't cake under eyes."), then the two steps in order: base hydrates and primes, stick helps neutralize dark circles and lasts twelve waterproof hours. No "covers" or "removes" claim. Close on the holiday set and party season.
