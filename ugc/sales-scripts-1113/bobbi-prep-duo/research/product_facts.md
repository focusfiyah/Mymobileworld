## Flags (what the caption/text says vs what the listing backs)
- Grace's on-screen text "complexion prep duo for chronically tired eyes...": fine as a feeling line; the listing does not say it helps tired eyes or puffiness, so scripts never say it fixes tiredness, only that dark circles make you look tired (an opinion) and the stick "helps neutralize dark circles".
- Grace's caption "Dark circles and dry makeup? ... helps you prep for a smoother, brighter-looking finish": backed ("smooth, makeup-ready base", "brighter, more even-looking complexion"). OK as written.
- NOT used: "removes / covers / gets rid of dark circles" (a review says "covered", but the brand says only "helps neutralize"); "miracle worker" (review, banned word); "on sale" (review, price talk); any price or discount; "TikTok exclusive bundle" (kayladelight said it, the listing does not); "24-hour" said for the duo (that is the face base priming only, not said); caffeine, puffiness, fine lines, anti-aging.
- Sweat/humidity resistance is real but left out to keep 3 selling points.
