Product description
Prep and Brighten Duo
Prep, hydrate, and brighten your complexion with Bobbi Brown's Prep and Brighten Duo, featuring two makeup essentials to create a smooth, makeup-ready base while helping neutralize discoloration and dark circles.
CHOOSE YOUR SHADE
·
Skin Corrector Stick: Choose your shade
WHAT'S INCLUDED
1. Skin Corrector Stick (3 g)
Color correction at its most effortless. The glide-on stick helps to instantly neutralize discoloration and brighten dark circles with skin-true coverage. This creamy, buildable formula blends on contact and becomes one with skin for seamless concealer prep.
Benefits
·
Helps neutralize dark circles and discoloration for a brighter, more even-looking complexion.
·
Provides creamy, buildable coverage that blends seamlessly into skin.
·
Helps hydrate skin for a smoother, plumper appearance.
·
Delivers 12-hour waterproof, sweat- and humidity-resistant correction.
2. Vitamin Enriched Face Base+ (0.5 oz / 15 ml)
The all-in-one multi-vitamin makeup primer and hydrating moisturizer you know and love, now with 24-hour priming and our new SuperVitamin Blend with 5x the vitamins for a hydrated, glowy, makeup-ready base.
Benefits
·
Provides 24-hour makeup priming for a smooth, seamless base.
·
Helps hydrate and support the skin's moisture barrier.
·
Helps brighten the appearance of skin and even skin tone.
·
Hydrates and plumps for a fresh, glowing complexion.
HOW TO USE
Step 1. Vitamin Enriched Face Base+
Warm a pea-size amount between fingertips and gently massage onto freshly cleansed skin to hydrate and prime for makeup.
Step 2. Skin Corrector Stick
Swipe directly over dark circles or areas of discoloration. Gently pat with your finger to blend as needed.
