- **What it is:** a full size 3 by 5 foot Haitian flag (blue and red with the coat of arms and palm tree), printed on 150D polyester, dye sublimated for bright color, with a heavy canvas header and two solid brass grommets. Made for inside or outside (TikTok title, G128 store).

**Features to benefits**
- 3x5 ft -> big enough to wrap around you or fill a wall; it reads as a real flag, not a souvenir.
- Dye sublimated print -> colors pop and the coat of arms is sharp up close, the detail Haitians check.
- Brass grommets, canvas header -> hangs the same day, no sewing, no tape.
- Indoor/outdoor -> the porch, the window, the parade, the party.

## 2. Target buyer
- **Who:** Haitians and Haitian Americans in the diaspora, mostly women and parents.
- **Situations:** Flag Day parades (May 18), January 1 Independence Day and soup joumou at home, Vertières (November 18), school cultural days, graduations, a first apartment or dorm room, the car. Real searches: "where to buy haitian flag", "haitian flag for room", "haitian flag for car", "how to style a haitian flag", "haitian flag day outfits" (audience/questions.md).
- **What makes them spend:** pride and being seen. A mom buys for a kid: "My son ... always need a flag to represent" (@dnoellifestyle, same flag).

## 6. Honest verdict
- **Selling potential: Medium.** Real, proven demand (the same flag got 121.5K and 57.9K views as shop videos, and the category leader shows 833 lifetime sales), low impulse price, and a clear calendar (Nov 18, Jan 1, May 18). Held back by a niche audience, cheap look alike flags, a big May peak with a slower winter, and part of the crowd wanting the black and red flag.
- **Product market appeal:** strong inside the Haitian community, close to zero outside it.
- **Strongest selling points:** the size on her body, the bright colors and sharp coat of arms, ready to hang.
- **Objections, barriers:** "I can get one anywhere cheaper", "is it the right size", "I want black and red", "will it fade outside" (no fade claim exists: never promise it).
- **Competitive advantages:** Grace is Haitian and speaks to her own people; the flag is from a flag maker; brass grommets and a canvas header.
- **Best audience:** Haitian American women and moms, 20s to 40s.
- **Strongest angle to prioritize:** identity pride with a real date: "Ayisyen, are you ready for January first?", shown with the flag worn as a cape.
