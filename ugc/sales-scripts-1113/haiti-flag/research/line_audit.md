# Line audit: G128 Haiti flag (Grace script method, PLAYBOOK §4, 2026-11-13)
GREEN = the video gets worse without it. YELLOW = keep, could be sharper. RED = cut. Domino test: each line sets up the next.

## Voiceover
Buyer: Haitians and Haitian Americans who want to show where they come from (broadest buyer, no age or city qualifier)
Pain: being the only one with no flag when everyone pulls theirs out (Big Fish rung 2, feeling rung 3-4)
Connection: the flag is how we show where we come from, so having it fixes the empty-hands moment
Outsider test: hook has no qualifier ("everybody", "you"); "Haitian" is not even needed in the hook because the flag is on screen at frame 0
Common enemy: the moment itself (everyone else has theirs out), not the viewer
Contradiction check: no claim that the flag makes anyone prouder or more Haitian; only what it is (size, colors, grommets) and what she wants
Repetition pass: "flag" said once after the hook ("this one"); "where we come from" once; "bright" not repeated
Pain points: 1
Selling points: 3
| Line | Mark | Why / shot that proves it |
|---|---|---|
| You know that moment when everybody pulls out their flag but you? | GREEN | H062 hook, the pain in one line. Shot: flag swinging open at frame 0 |
| You're standing there with nothing to wave. | GREEN | Pain beat, makes the moment concrete. Shot: empty hands, then the G128 pack |
| Girls, this one shows where we come from. | GREEN | Connection (Grace's own caption words). Shot: she pulls it out and shakes it open |
| It's three by five, so it wraps around you. | GREEN | Selling point 1, answers "What size is this one ?". Shot: cape on her back |
| The colors pop, and the palm tree is sharp. | GREEN | Selling point 2, the detail buyers check. Shot: coat-of-arms close-up |
| Brass rings let it hang inside or out. | YELLOW | Selling point 3 (title: brass grommets, indoor/outdoor). Shot: grommet close-up |

| Hang it before January first. | GREEN | Real date: Haitian Independence Day. Shot: flag held high |
| It's in the orange cart. | GREEN | Close |
Cut: "Empty hands on Flag Day hurts." (draft, mixed a second date with January first); "This flag says it before you even talk." (repeated the connection); "big enough to wrap all the way around you" trimmed to "wraps around you" (length)
