# KIGO Baby Sunglasses: 5 talking-head scripts for Grace (V1)

Talk to camera with the sunglasses in your hand or on the little one, the whole video. [Brackets] = what to do or fill in. [Insert] = an optional 2 to 3 second close-up Ralph lays over your voice. Film the hook, body and CTA as separate takes, 2 or 3 takes of each hook.

## Angle 1: The back seat sun

Timely and a specific moment. Winter sun sits low, right at car seat height.

On-screen headline: "The back seat sun nobody mentions 😎" · Label: "winter sun 101"

**HOOK** [sunglasses already on the little one or in your hand, to camera]

"You know that moment when the low sun hits the back seat and your little one squints the whole drive?"

**BODY** [put the sunglasses on, or hold them up]

"Winter sun sits low, so it's right in their eyes. These are polarized, so they cut the glare, and they're UV400. The frame is soft and flexible, and it's sized for little faces. [Insert: the lens in sunlight] I know, sunglasses in November sounds extra. But this is the pair I'd keep in the car door."

**CTA**

"They fit babies up to six, so grab two colors, one for the car and one for the diaper bag. Holiday road trips are coming, so don't wait. It's in the orange cart."

Alternate hooks:

- "Does anybody else's toddler squint the entire drive?"
- "At first I thought baby sunglasses were just for the beach."

## Angle 2: Snow day packing list

Timely. Holiday trips and snow days. Film it with a coat or a packed bag beside you.

On-screen headline: "Nobody packs this for the snow ❄️" · Label: "winter trip list"

**HOOK** [holding up the sunglasses next to a hat and mittens]

"Before your winter trip, look at this."

**BODY** [slide them on, or hand them to the little one]

"Everybody packs the hat and the mittens. Nobody packs sunglasses, and snow bounces the sun right back up at them. These are polarized to cut the glare, they're UV400, and the frame is flexible, so it fits in a coat pocket. [Insert: lens close-up] I hate how easy these are to pack. Now there's no excuse to squint on the sledding hill."

**CTA**

"Get one for each kid, so nobody fights over the blue ones. Holiday travel is coming, so put them in your cart before you pack. It's in the orange cart."

Alternate hooks:

- "This is going to matter the first time the snow shows up."
- "You'll want these ready before the first snow day."

## Angle 3: The baby gift that isn't clothes

[Insert: the colors lined up] They're a baby shower gift and a first birthday gift, and the mom will say, wait, that's adorable. Then she'll actually use them."

## Angle 4: The bend test

Proof the reference skipped, with a little humor. Do the test off camera first, then film it, and say only what you saw. Use the baby pair (the soft TPEE frame). If your pair is the 3 to 6 year one, skip the 360 line and ask Ralph.

On-screen headline: "Can a toddler break these? 🤔" · Label: "bend test"

**HOOK** [sunglasses in both hands, to camera]

"I'm just gonna show you, because explaining it is harder."

**BODY** [bend the frame all the way, twist it, let go; then say what happened]

"Toddlers throw things and sit on things. Most sunglasses don't make it. This is a flexible frame that bends and twists, so watch. [Insert: the bend, close up] [Say what you saw: __] They're polarized and UV400 too. Honestly, I'm a little upset these are tougher than my own sunglasses. [only if true, else: Honestly, I'm kind of jealous of a toddler's sunglasses.]"

**CTA**

"Every kid is going to test this, so grab a second pair, because one always ends up under the car seat. And it's gift season. It's in the orange cart."

Alternate hooks:

- "I know I can't be the only one whose toddler breaks every pair of sunglasses."
- "There's one detail on these baby sunglasses that changes the whole thing. They bend."

## Angle 5: But it's not summer

The objection killer. For fall and winter buyers, and for everyone in a warm place.

On-screen headline: "Baby sunglasses in November? 🤨" · Label: "not just for summer"

**HOOK** [sunglasses in hand, to camera]

"My biggest question was, why baby sunglasses in November?"

**BODY** [put them on the little one, or on a stand beside you]

"So here's the thing. The sun doesn't clock out in the fall. If you're in Florida, Texas, Arizona or California, it's bright right now, and tons of families are flying somewhere warm for the holidays. In the cold places, the sun's low and snow bounces it right up. [Insert: lens in sunlight] These are polarized, UV400, and sized for little faces. Honestly, I'm kind of annoyed I thought of them as summer only."

**CTA**

"Get a pair before your holiday trip, and a second color so there's a backup in the bag. It's in the orange cart."

Alternate hooks:

- "The one thing I was unsure about was sunglasses in the fall."
- "Now I understand why these keep showing up on my feed."
