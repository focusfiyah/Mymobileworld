## Objections and purchase justification
1. "It's not summer." Answer: winter sun is low and sits right in a car-seat child's eyes; snow bounces the sun back up (AAP HealthyChildren.org: snow reflects up to 80% of sunlight, UV is there in cold weather); holiday trips go south (Florida, Texas, Arizona, California, theme parks, cruises); and many buyers live where it is bright all year. Not summer for everyone.
2. "Will my baby keep them on / will they survive?" Answer: soft flexible TPEE frame that bends and twists, child-size fit (listing).
3. "Are they safe / worth it?" Answer: polarized lens and UV400 (listing); say nothing medical.
4. "Another thing they'll outgrow." Answer: it covers 0 to 6 years (title) and is a gift for a baby shower or first birthday.
