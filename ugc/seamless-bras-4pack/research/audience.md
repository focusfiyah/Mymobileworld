# Audience research (2026-10-07): Seamless Wireless Bras 4-Pack
Data: `audience/questions.md` + `raw.json` (Google + YouTube autocomplete), `audience/comments.md` (TikTok comments), `audience/transcripts.md`, `audience/voc.md`.

## Who
- Women who want to quit underwire, many with a fuller bust or plus size. They search "do wireless bras give enough support", "do wireless bras work for large breasts", "push up bra no underwire plus size".

## Pain point experience
1. Support doubt: "are wireless bras supportive", "do wireless bras ride up", "do wireless bras make you sag".
2. Sizing fear: "Sizing is to confusing? Would love to order one but have no idea what size to pick!" and "they runsmalllllll".
3. Wanting to see real bodies: "Also need to see on plus size women. Who have belly’s. Do they roll?".
4. Padding questions: "Are these padded?" and "i see the cups come out.... is it annoying like other companies???".

## Communication style
- Fit facts first, like the winners: "For reference, I'm a 32 H, and I'm wearing a size large".
- Proof on camera: under a white tee, side view, arms up.
- No body shame, no medical claims.

## What it means for the script
- Every script carries Grace's height, size and how it runs (blanks she fills).
- Proof tests: arms up, jump, side view, white tee and deep V neckline.
- Show the four colors as four looks.
