# Audience: Earth Breeze Laundry Detergent Sheets (2026-10-30)

Data: research/audience/questions.md (1,068 phrases from Google, Bing, YouTube, Amazon, TikTok autocomplete), comments.md (298 TikTok comments from the 5 researched videos), voc.md.

## Who
Busy women doing the household laundry (moms, apartment and dorm living, small laundry corners) who want to swap the big jug for something lighter and cleaner, many with sensitive skin in the house. They already heard of sheets and ask "is earth breeze good" and "how to use laundry detergent sheets"; some want it for "college" ("are laundry detergent sheets better for college").

## Pain point experience
- Doubt first: the top question is "do laundry detergent sheets work" (4 sources), then "does earth breeze really work" and "are laundry detergent sheets worth it". A 2.5M-view video calls sheets a scam, so they arrive skeptical.
- The jug: "Okay but I don't want to buy detergent in a plastic jug" and "bulky, messy liquid laundry detergent".
- Residue and skin: "One sheet always leaves some residue on my darker clothes" and "are earth breeze laundry sheets toxic" (3 sources).
- How much: "How many do you need to put in the washer?"

## Communication style
Casual, quick, a bit skeptical, show-me ("Watch it dissolve" is the line the winners use). They respond to seeing the sheet melt in water, to the light little box vs the heavy jug, and to plain how-to (take, toss, wash). No chemistry lectures; the comment sections punish preachy eco talk (the beach video got "Why are you putting that in the ocean?").

## What it means for the script
- Open on the proof they want: the sheet dissolving in water (H094 "Watch what happens when I __"), like the 1.7M, 339K and 290K winners.
- Pain beat = the heavy, drippy jug; then the residue worry answered with the box's own words "no harsh chemical residue".
- Answer "how many": one sheet, toss it in. No eco sermon, no ocean.
- CTA for more units: a couple of boxes before the holiday guests (real season, more sheets and towels to wash).
