Throw Hands Rock Paper Scissors Card Game – The Absurdly Fast and Chaotic Party Game for 2 to 8 Players – 60 Second Game for Friends, Families, and Game Night – Ages 7+

The game where SPEED meets ROCK, PAPER, SCISSORS!

Throw Hands turns “Rock, Paper, Scissors” into an absurdly fast and absolutely chaotic card game for 2–8 players! With NO turns and NO time to think, players pile up “Rock,” “Paper,” and “Scissors” cards by throwing down the winning symbol again and again in a ridiculously funny race to shed all your cards first!

In this free-for-all card-shedding game, you’ll need a quick mind and even quicker hands to win. Watch the last card played, then be the first player to throw down a card with the symbol that beats it. Rock beats Scissors. Paper beats Rock. Scissors beats Paper. Don’t wait for anyone, because they’re not waiting for you!

With players shouting symbols, cards flying everywhere, and no time to hesitate, this 60-second card game hilariously pushes everyone’s mental reflexes to the edge!

Throw Hands is ridiculously easy to learn, insanely fast to play, and guaranteed to get laughs as it turns any room into a loud, competitive mess. It’s the perfect game for turning up the energy at family and friends’ game night!

2–8 Players | Ages 7+ | 60 Seconds Play Time
