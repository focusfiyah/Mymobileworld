# Audience: Throw Hands card game (Grace), 2026-10-13
Data: research/audience/questions.md (3,006 autocomplete phrases), comments.md (162 TikTok comments, 6 videos), voc.md (Reddit quotes via Exa).

## Who
Moms (and aunts, grandmas) who host family game night or holiday gatherings with mixed ages: little kids, teens, adults who rarely play. Searches: "how to play rock paper scissors card game", "are family game nights dying now", "are party card games fun for kids". Buyers in the comments are parents and relatives: "its so fun...the kids LOVE it", "Love this game!! My daughter loves to play it!".

## Pain point experience
- Screens win over the table: "Kids want their tablets, parents are on phones" (voc.md). TikTok searches even ask "are family game nights dying now".
- Long games and long rules: "our 'quick game' has become the entire evening" and "A box can say '8+' and still be too slow for a child" (voc.md).
- On THIS product the top reaction is confusion, not doubt: "Yall just throwin cards down" (262 likes), "How are 2 people throwing cards at the same time?", "how the hell do you play?", "Who goes first? What order is the game going in?" The winning videos never explained the rule.
- The fun is the chaos and the arguing: "The arguments.....oh my ....the arguments." and "Nope this game is an unresolvable fight waiting to happen".

## Communication style
Loud, laughing, family talk. Short lines, emojis, "the kids LOVE it". They compare to games they know ("this will never replace uno", "so its like taco cat goat pizza"). No technical words.

## What it means for the script
1. Pain first: phones at game night and games that drag (real quotes above).
2. Explain the rule in one plain breath ("no turns, everybody plays at the same time, slam the card that beats it"), because the top comments on both winners ask how it works. That also answers "how are 2 people throwing cards at the same time".
3. Sell the chaos as the fun, with the 60-second round as the brag-confession ("nobody stops at one").
4. Mixed ages: "two to eight players" and real holiday timing (Thanksgiving) with a second box for grandma's, so the CTA sells more units.
5. No comparison to UNO by name (comments push back on "replacing Uno").
