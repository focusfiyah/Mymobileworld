# Stocking Stuffer Finds: campaign look spec (draft for Ralph's OK)

Client: Grace. Series: "stocking stuffer finds", AI videos with voiceover, running until Christmas (Grace, Telegram 2026-11-06).
Grace's brief: "the aesthetics have to match the Xmas vibes .. and all the videos we do for this need to match."
References: Telegram 786406544 (mantel with velvet bow stockings) and 786406545 (burgundy/blush tree), Drive "Telegram Assets (Azariah)".

## What the screenshots show
- **Palette:** burgundy / wine velvet, blush pink, champagne and brass gold, cream and ivory, white painted woodwork, deep evergreen.
- **Textures:** crushed velvet (big floppy bows), satin ribbon, glass ornaments, frosted white sprigs, chunky cream knit, pinecones.
- **Props:** velvet bow stockings on a white mantel, evergreen garland with tiny fairy lights, small white ceramic houses, brass candlesticks with white pillar candles, wrapped gifts (burgundy, cream, blush paper, satin bows), matching wreath, cream sofa with a knit throw.
- **Light in the screenshots:** evening candle-glow, warm amber cast. This is the one point that clashes with our rule "bright and neutral, no golden or yellow grade" (Ralph 2026-10-04), so it is question 1 below.
- Both are AI-styled Pinterest images: we copy the look, never the images.

## The shared look (every video in the series)
| Part | Spec |
|---|---|
| Palette | burgundy velvet, blush pink, champagne gold, cream, white woodwork, evergreen. No primary "Santa" red, no lime green, no silver/blue, no tartan. |
| Signature beat | the **velvet bow stocking**: each video has one shot of the product going into or coming out of a blush or burgundy stocking (plain, no names: names are text). This is what ties the series together. |
| Props kit | garland with tiny fairy lights, pinecones, little white houses, brass candlesticks + white candles, blush/burgundy/champagne glass ornaments, gifts in burgundy/cream/blush paper with satin bows, cream knit throw. 2-4 props per shot, never clutter in front of the product. |
| Light | bright daytime light, neutral white balance (white walls stay white, cream stays cream); fairy lights and candles lit as small points and soft bokeh only, no amber wash; no dark, moody or vignette look. |
| Grade | bright neutral, then the AI finish (`ai_finish.sh`: grain, sharpen, saturation down a little). |
| Framing | 9:16, product mid-frame, close and medium shots, shallow depth so the lights become soft bokeh; slow camera walk-ins/dollies or a hand doing something; a third to half hand-free. |
| VO | warm, friendly, Grace's own pace; one voice for the whole series (Grace herself or the Grace clone v5, her call). |
| Script structure | hook from Grace's hook library word for word, pain, solution, honest brag line, real urgency (stockings get filled soon, Christmas is close), close "It's in the orange cart." |
| On screen | no text, no captions, no product cards (Grace's versions). |

## What changes per video (so TikTok never sees a repeat)
Each video gets its own **main set**, shot order, framing and product action, all inside the shared look:
- A. white mantel with the stockings (walk-in)
- B. tree base with wrapped gifts
- C. gift-wrapping table: paper, ribbon, scissors
- D. cream sofa corner with the knit throw and a mug
- E. console/sideboard with garland and candlesticks
- F. bedside or vanity with a small tabletop tree
Five products, five main sets; the stocking beat appears in every video but shot from a different angle and spot each time.

## Production route (per product, after Ralph's OK)
Real Pexels/Pixabay Xmas rooms first as references (free), still storyboard, product reference sheet from Grace's real photos, prompt-master prompts, Flow takes first for product clips, Wan 3.0 720P on Kie as fallback, Seedance 2.0 Mini for product-free shots (mantel, tree). Plan + one cost per product before any spend.

## Open questions for Ralph

2. Sets: one set family per video (above) vs one identical "hero" room for all five.
