# Audience: Ocean View Juice Boost Hair Growth Oil (2026-11-02)
Data: research/audience/questions.md (1,949 autocomplete phrases), comments.md (412 TikTok comments from 4 winner videos), voc.md.

## Who
Mostly Black women with natural / 4C hair worried about thin edges and dry scalp, who keep hearing batana oil is the thing on TikTok. Top searches: "does hair growth oil work", "which hair growth oil is best", "how to use batana oil", "batana oil vs castor oil".

## Pain point experience
- Raw batana is a paste: "batana oil becomes solid at room temperature. To use it, you'll want to liquefy it", "Mine is not melting but smells like burn coffe".
- They don't know how to use it: "Are you supposed to rinse it out ? Or can you use it as a grease ?", "how to use batana oil", "when to apply hair growth oil".
- Torn between oils: "batana oil vs castor oil", "batana oil vs rosemary oil".
- Fear of fakes and doubt: "I'm sick of buying fake stuff", "is batana oil legit", "My edges are same".

## Communication style
Casual, lots of question marks, emojis, "y'all", "girl". They trust seeing it applied more than claims; a dermatologist debunk got 266.8K views, so big promises get pushback.

## What it means for the script
- Sell ease: liquid oil (no melting) + a pointed tip that goes on parts and edges; show where it goes.
- Three oils in one answers the "batana vs castor vs rosemary" question without a comparison.
- No growth promise, no timeframe, no before/after.
- Close with a winter backup (dry scalp season) for more units.
