## Patterns we copy
1. Wig already on and MOVING at frame 0 (every winner). Grace's footage does this (hands framing her face, then swings).
2. The fake-looking fear answered by movement and light, not by words alone (@inkedmamafinds, @tatyanna_hkitty).
3. Colour for the season: highlights + fall / Halloween (@mrsasianna, @inkedmamafinds, @syeliseee, @mariaa_inc).
4. Easy for non-wig people / glueless (@inkedmamafinds, @mariaa_inc; the most-liked comment in the niche is glueless confusion).
5. CTA buys more colours ("I'm definitely gonna go back for more", "Don't sell this color out" style, without the scarcity).
6. Length: 19-43 s for the talking ones; ~3 words/s.
