# Audience: long layered synthetic wig with bangs, highlight brown (2026-10-21)

Data: `audience/questions.md` (2,885 autocomplete phrases from Google, Bing, YouTube, Amazon, TikTok), `audience/comments.md`
(531 TikTok comments from the 6 researched videos, 107 buyer questions), `audience/voc.md` (verbatim quotes with links).

## Who
Women, mostly Black women on wig TikTok (top searches: "synthetic wigs for black women", "wig with bangs for black women"),
two groups: (1) budget wig wearers who switch looks often and buy synthetic to save, and (2) people new to wigs
("synthetic wig for beginners", "I'm not a wig person, okay? I'm just getting into wigs") who want a look for fall,
Halloween or the holidays ("synthetic wig for halloween", "I’m only wearing a wig for Halloween").

## Pain point experience
- Synthetic looks fake. The top search question is "do synthetic wigs look real", with "synthetic wig too shiny"; buyers
  ask "Any tips to make it less shiny ?" and say "Mine looks so fake". The compliment they want is the question
  "Is that ur real hair?".
- Tangling and frizz: "Does it tangle easily ?", "most get really frizzy and tangled".
- Install and glue confusion: "I’m not understanding how this is considered glueless" (1,052 likes, the top comment in
  the niche). They want it on and off with no glue, no spray, nothing to wash off.
- The back gives it away: "my hair from the back keeps showing".
- Trust: "sometimes you guys think influencers are lying about these synthetic wigs", "Before I buy, did you modify it?".
- Bangs are wanted and hard to find: "I’ve been trying to find a valid wig w bangs for the longest."

## Communication style
Hype-girl, casual, fast: "baby tell me", "she ate", "OH MY GOSH?", "SIS HOWWWW?!???", "10 out of 10". Short reactions,
the wig is the "she". Proof over claims: show the back, the movement, the highlights in the light, the hair straight out
of the bag. Grace's own take in this footage is the same register: "I usually stay away from synthetic hair",
"look at the freaking highlights".

## What it means for the script
- Lead with the fake-looking fear and answer it on screen at once with movement (swing, shake, back view).
- Say what people ask: does it look real (show it moving), the back (show it), glueless (say it plainly: no glue),
  bangs that hide the hairline, the highlights for fall.
- Keep her voice: short, excited, her own phrases, no expert talk, no tangle-free or heat-number claims we cannot back.
- Real urgency: Halloween (Oct 31) and holiday pictures; CTA sells a second wig in another colour.
