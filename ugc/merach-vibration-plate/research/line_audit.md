# Line audit: Merach Slim Vibration Plate, round 3 (Grace script method, PLAYBOOK §4, 2026-11-09)
GREEN = the video gets worse without it. YELLOW = keep, could be sharper. RED = cut. Domino test: each line sets up the next.

## Script 1: Sitting all day
Buyer: women 30+ who sit most of the day and want to move more (broad, no qualifiers)
Pain: feeling out of shape and blaming herself (Big Fish rung 4, "I'm lazy")
Connection: her body needs movement, and the plate gives it movement while she just stands
Outsider test: no age, job or mom qualifier in the hook; "you" opener
Common enemy: sitting all day (not her)
Contradiction check: no claim that the plate gets her in shape; only that it gets her body moving (literal, shown on screen)
Repetition pass: round 2's "it shakes my whole body" and "moving" not said twice
Pain points: 2
Selling points: 2
| Line | Mark | Why / shot |
|---|---|---|
| You're not out of shape. | GREEN | Takes the shame off; Big Fish hook. Shot: whole body shaking from behind |
| You've just been sitting all day. | GREEN | Names the enemy, sets up the fix. Shot: plate on the floor, feet beside it |
| Your body needs to move, and this plate gets it moving while you stand on it. | GREEN | Connection. Shot: stepping on, overhead shake |
| It's slim, so you leave it out and actually use it. | YELLOW | Result + because of + feature (slim, 4.7 in). Shot: slim plate from the side |
| Over a hundred thousand people already have one. | GREEN | Proof: 148.8K sold (listing). Shot: side angle shaking |
| Start before the New Year. | GREEN | Real reason to act now |
| It's in the orange cart. | GREEN | Close. Shot: hand at the plate |
Cut: "Who actually has time to get to the gym? Not me." (round 1, RED: a question that only restated the pain); "I hate how easy this is" (RED: confession that did not move the story)

Outsider test: no qualifiers; "you" opener
Common enemy: the hour-at-the-gym idea of a workout
Contradiction check: "easy even on your tired days" comes from "low impact" (listing); no fitness result claimed
Repetition pass: "gym" and "workout" each said once
