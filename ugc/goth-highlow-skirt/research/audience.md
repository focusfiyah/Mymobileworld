# Audience: goth high-low skirt (Grace), 2026-10-22

## Who
Women dressing for Halloween and fall who like a goth / alt / pirate / witchy look but also want to wear the piece again after the 31st. The search map is full of long-or-short doubts: "should goth skirts be long or short", "can goth skirts be worn casually", "are high low skirts still in style". Styling searches pair it with boots: "goth skirt and boots", "high low skirt with boots".

## Pain point experience
Buying a costume piece that gets worn once. One buyer opened with "You ever bought something for Halloween but your kids will wear it year round?" The other doubt is length: too long for daytime, too short for some places ("you can go higher, but we're keeping it PG rated here"). AI videos that show a different item get called out: "It's not the same dress .. why do they do this .." on the 65K corset video.

## Communication style
Short, excited, visual. Comments on the inspo: "Oh I love that you can adjust this skirt and make different looking outfits", "That adjustable on both sides yes please", "Love that it’s adjustable". The winners in the category (65K, 60K views) are 9-12 s, music only, outfit on screen from frame 0, no talking.

## What it means for the script
- Music-only, ~15 s, the pull-the-strings length change is the whole story (that is what commenters react to).
- Show long, then pulled up, then the back, with boots: one skirt, several looks, no words needed.
- Keep the skirt exactly the real one (product lock): viewers spot a swapped AI item.
- Caption carries the Halloween reason and "wear it after" angle; optional Grace line if Ralph wants a voice.
