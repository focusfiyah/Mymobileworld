# Audience: Nighttime Magnesium Signature Trio (2026-10-30)

## Who
Women 30-60 who already know magnesium lotion from TikTok and want a night routine; moms and gift buyers (@katie.sleepyhead, 13.1K views, bought it for her mom). Search data: "does magnesium lotion help you sleep", "how to use magnesium lotion", "which magnesium lotion is best for sleep" (3-source agreement in questions.md).

## Pain point experience
Tired legs and feet at the end of the day and a bedtime that doesn't feel restful. They ask "will magnesium lotion help with leg cramps" and "does magnesium lotion help with restless legs". Many are tired of products that oversell: "lies doesn't work", "I just got some of this! Hoping it works for me!!".

## Communication style
Casual, first person, short. Practical questions about the feel and the scent: "Does it have a particular smell?", "does it leave your legs feeling sticky?", "Soooo if you are full of tension you cover entire body?". They respond to a friend sharing her routine, not a claims list.

## What it means for the script
- Show it in use on feet and legs from frame 0 (how to use, answered on screen).
- Name the three scents (answers the smell question) and keep claims to the listing's routine pitch; no sleep/cure claims for a skeptical crowd.
- Brag about the routine ("Winding down is my favorite part now"), gift angle for the holidays.
