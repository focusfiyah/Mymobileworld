# Research: Nello Superfocus 30 Serving Tub (Grace)
Built from research/product_facts.md, product_truth.json, coach_research.md, audience.md, audience/ (2026-11-03).

## Product differentiation
One scoop holds the stack people otherwise buy as separate pills: N-Acetyl-L-Tyrosine 1,000 mg, lion's mane 500 mg, Alpha-GPC 350 mg, rhodiola 200 mg, L-theanine 150 mg, B6 and B12, with 0 g sugar and 15 calories, and it tastes like fruit punch. Competitors: Micro Ingredients lion's mane powder (plain, one ingredient), Ultra / Neutonic focus pouches (nicotine-style pouch, caffeine), Taste Salud energy drink mix (caffeine first). The tub gives 30 servings with a scoop, cheaper per serving than the packets (creator @nicchooleee).

## Target buyer
Busy women 25-45 working from home, moms and students who hit a 3 pm slump and reach for a second coffee; many already know Nello from the packets and now want the tub. Situation: a long to-do list, forty open tabs, holiday season about to get busy.

## Pain points and desires
Pain: "I have so much to do, and somehow I'm doing none of it", rereading the same email, the guilt of rotting on the couch, coffee that leaves them jittery. Desire: to feel locked in and get the list done, in a drink that tastes good ("taste good and zero sugar"), without swallowing 15 different supplements.

## Buyer motivation
Right now: the afternoon slump happens every workday, and the holidays add more to the list. Practical: the tub is the cheaper way to keep it stocked; emotional: no more shame about the undone list.

## Objections and purchase justification
"Does it realllllly work?" → we never promise a result; we say what is in it (listing facts) and what people say. Taste ("Tastes horrible" vs "taste good") → fruit punch flavour, best ice cold, zero sugar (L9). "How do we measure the amount appropriately?" → one scoop, scoop included (L9). Too many pills → one drink holds the stack. Value → 30 servings in one tub.

People buy it to beat the afternoon slump without another coffee, in a drink that tastes like fruit punch. Lead with the 3 o'clock moment (hook), sell the one-scoop stack that tastes good (desire + objection killer), close on thirty servings and two tubs (justification).
