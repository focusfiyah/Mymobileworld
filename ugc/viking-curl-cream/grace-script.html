Script for Grace

Viking Revolution Curl Cream

Say these lines over your footage of putting the cream on your husband's beard. Keep it quick and natural, like you're telling a friend. The whole thing should land around 20 to 25 seconds.

  • 20–25 seconds
  • Hands only
  • Your own voice
On-screen text, first second he'd never buy this himself
  1. Hook0–4s

    "My husband would never buy curl cream for himself, so I got it for him."

    Hold the tube up, label facing the camera.

  2. Problem4–8s

    "His beard gets dry and frizzy, and it's scratchy when he kisses me."

    Your hand on his beard before the product.

  3. Product8–12s

    "Viking Revolution curl cream: shea butter, vitamin E and B five, sandalwood scent."

    Label close-up, squeeze a little onto your fingers.

  4. Experience12–17s

    "A little on his damp beard, work it in, and now it's soft with no crunch."

    Working it through his beard.

  5. Honest note17–20s

    "It's sold for head hair, but his beard's curly too."

    Calm, like telling a friend.

  6. Call to action20–25s

    "If his beard doesn't bother you, skip this. If it does, it's in the orange cart."

    Tube in frame. End on the picture, not the last word.
