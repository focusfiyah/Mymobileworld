Script for Grace

Viking Revolution Curl Cream

Say these lines over your beard footage. Talk straight to the viewer about their beard, quick and natural. The whole thing should land around 25 seconds.

  • About 25 seconds
  • Hands only
  • Your own voice
How the text works Every text overlay makes them ask "what?" Never name the product on screen. Each line answers one question and opens the next, so they keep watching.
  1. Hook0–3s

    Text: Nobody puts this on a beard

    "If your beard feels like sandpaper, it's probably what you're putting in it."

    Hand rubbing his beard before any product.

  2. Pain3–9s

    Text: what's really drying it out

    "Oil leaves it greasy. Balm dries hard and crunchy, and it's frizzy again by lunch."

    Close on the dry, frizzy beard.

  3. Solution9–19s

    Text: the head-hair trick

    "So steal what curly guys put on their hair. Viking Revolution curl cream. Shea butter, vitamin E and B five. A little on a damp beard and it dries soft with no crunch."

    Label close-up, squeeze onto fingers, then work it through his beard.

  4. Urgency19–25s

    Text: before November hits

    "No-Shave November is a few weeks out, and cold air dries a beard out fast. Get it before then. It's in the orange cart."

    Hand running over the soft beard, tube in frame. End on the picture.
