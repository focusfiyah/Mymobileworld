## (b) Breakdowns of the 5 shop videos (`video` run, transcripts from TikTok's captions)

  "doomsday pooping 101 💩"; word-by-word captions. First visual: her talking to camera in a home room, product NOT in
  frame at 0 s; the orange 12-pack box swings in at ~2.5-3 s. Hook type: listicle call-out + curiosity/taboo.
- **Beats:** 0-3.6 list → punchline; 3.6-13.7 problem ("everybody's forgetting... a huge sanitation issue nobody's
  talking about"); 13.7-30.4 product: "found this thing on TikTok, the original WAG bag", opens box, holds a kit,

- **Proof:** unboxing the full 12-pack, pulling one kit, reading the kit contents. No in-use demo.
- **CTA:** "If you're interested in this, you scared like me? Go ahead and grab this before you be 💩 in the woods.

## (c) Shared patterns

- **0 s:** the two highest-save videos open on a person talking straight to camera with the taboo said out loud
  (mamarashaah, prepperpress). camiheals (hands only) opens on the product box. The low performer
  (deediscovers360) has no text and no product until 16 s.
- **Winning hook = the forgotten item in a prep checklist.** "Food, water, electricity... somewhere to poop"
  (171k), "You got your food and water prepped, but what about your sanitation?", "You can store water, you can store
  food... but what happens when the toilet doesn't work?", "We thought about everything except the bathroom." Every
  winner names the normal prep items first, then the bathroom as the gap. Same pattern in the non-shop hits:
  "one of the biggest things people forget" (kelseymayvandy, 4.68% saves).
- **Taboo humor + permission:** 💩 emoji in text, "doomsday pooping 101", "Someone had to address it",
  "nobody's talking about this". The joke makes the topic shareable (mamarashaah 1,738 shares).
- **On-screen text is a question:** "WHERE are you gonna POOP‼️", "but are you poo prepped too?", the "how do you
  dispose?" comment bubble.
- **Kit contents shown, never used:** open the box to the row of 12 orange pouches, pull one, hold up big bag, zip
  disposal bag, TP, wipe/sanitizer; read "turns liquid to gel" and the NASA line from the pouch. Disposal = "regular
  trash" is the top objection (shewiththeglow made a whole reply on it).
- **The DIY alternative as foil:** bucket + cat litter / pool noodle (mamarashaah, prepperpress), and the toilet
  "stops flushing" or backs up (camiheals). The DIY bucket discover page has 1.6M/232k views, so viewers know it.
- **Close:** count/bundle ("this comes in a pack of 12", "you get 12 in this kit", "depending on your family you may
  need more than one box", "grab a box or 2"), the "better to have it and not need it" line, link "right by my name" /
  "down below" / "orange cart". Use cases listed at the end: power outages, emergencies, camping, road trips.
- **Length/pace:** winners 29-63 s, 3.4-5.4 words/s, original sound (no trending audio), one location, talking head or
  hands-only VO.
