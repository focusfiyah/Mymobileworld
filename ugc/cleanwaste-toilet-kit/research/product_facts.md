## Grace's claims

### 1. "Each kit has a waste bag with powder that gels liquid and helps control odor" → **VERIFIED**
- cleanwaste.com: "Waste bag pre-loaded with Poo Powder ® Gelling/Deodorizing Agent" and "the only kit equipped with a NASA-developed gelling agent that traps, deodorizes, and breaks down waste."
- Pouch back (kit_pouch_back.jpg): "Waste bag pre-loaded with Poo Powder gelling deodorizing agent (gels 32 oz.)." / "Natural odor control and decay catalyst."
- TikTok Shop + Amazon 12 ct: "Our NASA-developed proprietary blend Poo Powder is super-absorbent, designed to gel and encapsulate liquid and solid waste. Non-toxic, odorless, deodorizes, and traps odors."
- FAQ https://cleanwaste.com/faqs/: "Poo Powder® is a proprietary blend of a NASA-developed super-absorbent designed to gel and encapsulate liquid and solid waste, and a natural deodorizing agent and decay catalyst."
- Notes: the brand spells it **Poo Powder®** (not "Pooh"; "Pooh Powder" appears only in a third-party retailer snippet). It gels liquid AND solid waste and "breaks down" waste (decay catalyst). "Helps control odor" is the safe wording: one REI reviewer (search snippet) said used bags "still smell pretty bad"; do not say "no smell" or "odor-proof" (the TikTok title says "Odor & Leak Proof", but the brand's own copy for the Original says "deodorizes, and traps odors"; "odor-proof" is used only for the Platinum bag).

**What an image model may get wrong**
- Making the bags orange (orange is only the printed card and box; the bags are blue-grey).
- Printing a logo or text on the grey bags; turning the clear kit pouch into a solid orange bag.
- Old packaging: light-blue box with "WAG-BAG" or blue "GO anywhere toilet kit" pouch.
- A tall or square box (it is wide and low); misspelling CLEANWASTE (one word) or "WAG BAG" (two words, caps, ®); "Pooh Powder".
- Toilet paper drawn as a full roll; a branded wet-wipe pack instead of the flag sachet.
- Showing a toilet flushing, water in the bowl, or the bag going down the toilet.
- Implying the GO Anywhere portable toilet or the "Commode" comes in the 12 pack (it does not).

## Compliance risks
- **Never say or show flushing.** Do not say biodegradable / compostable / eco-friendly (FTC Green Guides; the brand only says "break down quickly under land fill conditions"). Say "regular trash" and, if needed, "check local rules".
- **Odor:** "helps control odor" / "deodorizes", not "no smell" or "odor-proof" (reviews say a used bag can still smell).
- **NASA:** attribute it ("NASA-developed super-absorbent"); never "NASA-approved" or "astronauts use this bag".
- **Health:** avoid disease-prevention claims (the brand's preparedness page says "prevent disease outbreaks"; do not repeat).
- **Safety:** box warning "To avoid suffocation, keep this plastic bag away from babies and children... This bag is not a toy."; powder "DO NOT allow pets or people to INGEST". No kids playing with the bag on screen.
- "40% more than other products" and "Odor & Leak Proof" (TikTok title) are the brand's comparative/absolute claims; don't repeat them in Grace's VO.
