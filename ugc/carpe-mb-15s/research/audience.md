# Audience: Carpe Mountain Breeze, 15s hands-only (2026-10-07)

## Who
Women who sweat a lot (stress sweat, everyday heavy sweat, not only hyperhidrosis) and are tired of regular deodorant: "I usually stress sweat a lot", "there's not a second of the day I'm not sweating".

## Pain point experience
The visible part hurts most: sweat showing through clothes. "does it stop the sweat from transferring onto like a grey shirt". Many tried and still failed: "I used it exactly like this for a couple days, and I still sweat through". Scent matters too: "smell fresh but dont clash with my perfume".

## Communication style
Short, casual, girl-to-girl, emoji and exasperation. Questions about how to use it (dry skin? night? how much?) are the most common buyer questions (31 of 123 comments).

## What it means for the script
Hook on the visible sweat-through moment (colored shirt). Pain gets its own beat. Product + exact label claim. One how-to line (pea-size, dry skin, at night) because how-to is the top question. Sell-side confession that drives a second unit, real season, "It's in the orange cart." 15s = about 50 words.
