# TIRTIR Mask Fit Red Cushion: 3 scripts for Grace

3 talking-head scripts, about 30 seconds each. Talk to camera with the cushion in your hand the whole time.

[Brackets] = what to do. [Insert] = an optional 2 to 3 second close-up Ralph lays over your voice while you keep talking.

Film the hook, body and CTA as separate takes, 2 or 3 takes of each hook.

## Why these should work

- The top cushion videos open on the coverage and the finish, and the product is in the hand from the first frame.
- The best saves come from videos that read what the product does, in order, in plain words.
- Short tap-it-on moments sell the "easy" part without a long demo.
- Every script uses the listing's own claims. No price and no made up scarcity. The close is always the orange cart.

## Quick rules

- Cushion in your hand the whole video, compact open at least once.
- Keep pauses under a second.
- Say claims the way the listing says them. Don't add "it lasts all day" or "it never transfers".
- Skip hype words. Say what the listing says and stop.

## Shot list

- One setup for all three: phone vertical on a stand at eye level, window light, waist up, cushion in hand.
