# Hooks v2: 3 options per script, spoken + on-screen text, each modeled on a named winner
Text hooks are curiosity only, 5-7 words, never name the product (PLAYBOOK §3). Winners were broken down with `tiktok.py video` on 2026-10-04 (transcripts, hook.png and sheet.png in research/videos/, every hook.png looked at).

## Script 1: Foundation, not concealer (reveal)
Model: @jawarshere https://www.tiktok.com/@jawarshere/video/7690680310077345025 (62.7k views, 225 saves = 0.36%, 47 s, 3.3 words/s). Frame 0: her talking to camera in the lens, puff in hand by second 2. Spoken hook: "can someone explain what is going on... are you seeing this coverage? You wanna know what concealer I'm using? It's not a concealer. It's a foundation." Proof beat: applies live ("watch in real time"). Close: "I never thought a foundation could give me coverage like this."
Also @luvphoebeee https://www.tiktok.com/@luvphoebeee/video/7686647173416570142 for the "build layers with the puff" line.
- Spoken: "You'd think this much coverage came from a concealer. It's a foundation."
- Spoken alt (Grace category gap): "There are a ton of full coverage foundations out there, but I've never seen one that's actually lightweight."
- Spoken alt: "Full coverage and lightweight in the same compact? Yep."
- On-screen: "Wait, that's not concealer??"
- On-screen alts: "Not a concealer, believe it" / "Guess what this is"
