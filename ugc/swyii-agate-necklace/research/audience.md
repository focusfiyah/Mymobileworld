# Audience: Swyii red agate + moonstone agate choker (2026-10-21)
Data: `research/audience/questions.md` (2,815 autocomplete phrases, seeds agate / crystal / boho necklace), `comments.md` (176 comments from 5 videos, mostly the 1.1M-view video selling this exact necklace), `voc.md`.

## Who
Women who like warm, earthy, vintage-looking jewelry and dress for fall: they see the colours first ("Love these colors for fall", "It looks vintage! I love the colors!"). Many say they do not wear much jewelry or do not know how to style beaded pieces. They buy in pairs and for friends ("I bought one for me and the other for my bff"), and they notice the second colour ("Oooo the moonlight color is cute too").

## Pain point experience
Not a problem product: the pain is styling and doubt. (1) They like beaded necklaces but do not know what to wear them with: "never know how to style these but if it was layered then i would love that". (2) They worry it is a cheap craft piece: "This looks very make able", "Just go to hobby lobby", and the search map asks "are agate necklaces real" and "can agate necklace be worn everyday". (3) Fall outfits feel the same every year (sweater weather, plain chain).

## Communication style
Short, warm, visual, a little playful: "okay this is gorg!!!!", "I have this & it’s GORGEOUS!". They react to the stones up close and to the colours, not to specs. They argue about stone names ("ITS CARNELIAN not red agate"), so naming fights lose them. Proof they trust: weight and real stones ("reliable and totally not cheap"), seeing it on a person.

## What it means for the script
- Open with the necklace up close (Grace's 19-35 s window) and a line that breaks the "cheap craft" read (winner: "This is not your daughter's beaded necklace").
- Proof in plain words: real stones, beaded by hand, weight people mention, extender to the collarbone. Never debate agate vs carnelian, never "genuine/certified".
- Give the styling answer they asked for: layer it over a thin gold chain, off one shoulder (exactly what Grace filmed).
- Colours + season: fall tones now, Thanksgiving and holiday gifts next.
- CTA = two units: red + moonlight, or one for you and one for a best friend/sister.
