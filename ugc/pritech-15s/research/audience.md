# Audience: PRITECH heated face scrubber

## Who
Women (and some men) who already wash their face every night and wonder if a tool is worth it. They search "are facial cleansing brushes worth it" and "is face scrubber better than hands". Grace's Haitian audience overlaps: the 110K PRITECH video is from a Haitian creator with Kreyol comments.

## Pain point experience
They wash with their hands and a towel and think that is enough ("Sure, it’s to keep your face clean"). The doubt is hands vs a brush ("facial cleansing brush vs hands"). Some fear breakouts ("i had one of these and it made me breakout sooo bad", "us ppl with sensitive oily skin is a no no") and ask about cleaning it ("Do I need to clean it or can I just rinse after I use it and before?"). The heat is a curiosity hook ("heated?").

## Communication style
Short, casual, a little teasing: the winning hook is a call-out (@ttspaulking on-screen text: You're still washing your face with your HANDS?). No lectures, no skin-science words. Product in use on a foamy face from the first frame.

## What it means for the script
Grace's script already opens with the call-out and names the hands + towel pain, then three features and a daily-habit reason. Keep it hands-and-towel visual (makeup left on fingers, a used towel), show heat on the metal end, no acne or "fixes breakouts" claims. Close on the brush rinsed and back in its stand.
