# Audience: ThunderFit silicone rings 7-pack (2026-11-03)
Data: research/audience/questions.md (2,722 phrases from Google, Bing, YouTube, Amazon, TikTok autocomplete), comments.md (42 TikTok comments on the 3 ring winners), voc.md (Reddit, forums).

## Who
Married or engaged women who take their real ring off for the gym, work, travel, the dishes or swollen fingers, and then go bare. They search "are silicone rings safe for gym", "are silicone wedding bands good for swollen fingers", "are silicone rings worth it". A big group are collectors: "What if I want them all?!", "I have 6 and I LOVE them!!", "I literally want them all I only have 2! I get compliments all the time!!!!". Pregnant women save these: "Pregnant and saving this for in case my fingers swell lol". Travel: "Travel ring!!!".

## Pain point experience
They lose or stop wearing the real ring: "You could be like me and just take the ring off while working out and then lose it at the gym." / "Then one day, somewhere between the gym and home, it came off the carabiner and is now lost forever." / "So I just constantly find myself not wearing it, and I hate that." Travel fear: "We also like to travel too and actually had a ring stolen before." The objection to silicone is looks: "I don’t like the rubber band tho. It takes away from the ring." and "You know how you hate the other silicone ones cause they're just ugly." Sizing worries: "are silicone rings true to size", "should silicone rings be tight".

## Communication style
Girlfriend talk, short and excited, lots of "girl" and "all of them": "How? Did you get ALL of THose!!!!!!!! Girl!!!!!", "Talk about a strong ring game!". They brag about how many they own and want more colours ("Ughh and now there’s a pretty blue", "No purple"). Plain words, no tech talk.

## What it means for the script
- Lead with the moment the real ring comes off (gym, dishes, travel) and the bare finger, then show a ring that looks cute, not "rubber": colour and print are the answer to "they're just ugly".
- The 7-pack is the point: one for every outfit and every day; the collector brag ("I have all seven") is the honest line that sells.
- Show it bend/stretch (the 261K winner's whole video is the stretch and the ring going on).
- Urgency is real: holiday travel and holiday gifts (bridal party, sisters), not fake scarcity.
- CTA = more units: one set for you, one for your sister or bridesmaid.
