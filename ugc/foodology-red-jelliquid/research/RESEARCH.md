# Research: FOODOLOGY Red Jelliquid Bundle (Grace)
Built 2026-11-03 from product_truth.json, product_facts.md, coach.md + discover/videos, audience.md + audience/ (questions, comments, voc).

## Product differentiation
Three Korean daily sticks in one set, each with its own time slot: Coleology Drink Mix PRO (pomegranate powder, one stick in 17 oz cool water, 15 sticks), Coleology Cutting Jelly (chewy pomegranate jelly with chia seeds, 5 g fiber, one after a meal, 10 sticks) and ACV Apple Jelly (6,250 mg apple cider vinegar in a tangy-sweet jelly, 5 calories, zero sugar, 10 sticks). Competitors: liquid ACV (burn, smell, mixing), ACV gummies (the search map is full of "are acv gummies worth it"), and the single Cutting Jelly or the 3-in-1 jelly bundle (red, purple, green) that the big creators sell. This is the only set that pairs the famous red Cutting Jelly with an ACV jelly and a morning drink.

## Target buyer
Women 20s-40s who have seen the Korean cutting jelly videos everywhere and want an easy daily habit, plus ACV-curious people who cannot stand drinking vinegar. The moment it becomes relevant: they bought or nearly bought the jelly and asked "which one, and when?", or they quit liquid ACV because of the taste.

## Pain points and desires
Pain: liquid ACV "hurts my throat", the sticks come with no plan ("It says to take 1 a day after meal but WHICH MEAL?!"), and doubt about taste ("How does it taste??"). Desire: a routine that feels like a treat, something sweet after lunch that is not candy, and the "Korea figured it out" glow-up feeling the winners sell.

## Buyer motivation
The holidays are coming and people want a simple daily routine set up before January. The cutting jelly is already trending (5.5M, 3M, 1.9M, 1.7M views), so they want in, and a set that tells them what to take when removes the guesswork. Two sets cover home and work.

Every winner leans on body claims we will not make.

## Objections and purchase justification
"Which one do I take, and when?" -> the set's own routine: drink mix in the morning (listing: 17 oz cool water), Cutting Jelly after a meal (listing), ACV whenever (listing). "ACV tastes awful" -> tangy-sweet jelly "without the strong vinegar burn" (listing). "How does it taste?" -> pomegranate and apple, chewy; people say it tastes like a snack (VOC). "Is it a lot of sugar?" -> ACV jelly zero sugar, 5 calories; drink mix 0 g added sugar (panels). "Does it really work?" -> we do not promise results; we sell the easy routine and taste.

## Overall verdict
People buy this to make the trending Korean jelly sticks easy: three tastes, three times of day, no vinegar burn. Lead with the ACV candy surprise, then answer "which one, when?" with the routine; that routine is what sells the full set over one box.
