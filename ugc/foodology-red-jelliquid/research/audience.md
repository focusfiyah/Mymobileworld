# Audience: FOODOLOGY Red Jelliquid Bundle (2026-11-03)
Data: research/audience/questions.md (2,316 autocomplete phrases, seeds "apple cider vinegar jelly", "cutting jelly", "acv gummies"), comments.md (319 TikTok comments from the 4 top Foodology videos, 71 buyer questions), voc.md.

## Who
Women, mostly 20s-40s, who already watch the "Korean cutting jelly" videos (5.5M, 3M, 1.9M, 1.7M views) and want a daily wellness habit that does not feel like taking pills. Many are ACV-curious but hate the liquid. Search shows the same doubt over and over: "does cutting jelly actually work", "are acv gummies worth it", "are acv gummies as effective as liquid", "is cutting jelly good for you".

## Pain point experience
1. Liquid ACV is miserable: "its quite acidic and hurts my throat", "caused a burning sensation in my throat for the better part of a day".
2. They buy the sticks and then do not know how to fit them into a day: "It says to take 1 a day after meal but WHICH MEAL?! Lol I got the red one", "Do you eat both together, and when do you eat it?", "Wait so what do all of them do ?!". This is the most repeated buyer question in the comments.
3. They wonder about taste before buying: "How does it taste??".
4. Skeptical of hype: "Does is really work though?????".

## Communication style
Casual, friend-to-friend, colour-coded and short: the 1.7M bundle winner says "Green is all for the skin. That purple is gonna get all that crud out your system" and closes "It's in the cart." They love the snack angle ("Korean cutting jelly, it taste like a snack to me", 1.9M). They respond to "Korea figured it out" framing and to people passing on what others say ("These ACV jelly sticks are a really convenient alternative to traditional liquid ACV"). No science words.

## What it means for the script
- Lead with the ACV surprise (apple cider vinegar you chew, no burn): it names the pain and opens a curiosity loop.
- Answer "which one, when?" in the body: drink mix in the morning, Cutting Jelly after lunch, ACV jelly whenever. That routine IS the reason to buy the 3-piece set instead of one box.
- Taste words they use: chewy, tangy-sweet, pomegranate, snack.
- No weight, fat-loss or "debloat" claims: the audience asks for them, but the listing's numbers (Lost 20lbs, GLP-1) are not things Grace can say; TikTok flags them.
- Video with no VO: the visuals must carry it with the sound off: jelly coming out of the stick at frame 0, powder turning water pink, all three boxes at the end.
