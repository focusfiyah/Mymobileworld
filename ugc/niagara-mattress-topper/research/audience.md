# Audience: Niagara bamboo mattress topper (2026-10-30)
Data: research/audience/questions.md (1,782 search phrases, Google/Bing/YouTube/Amazon/TikTok autocomplete), research/audience/comments.md (340 TikTok comments from the 3 researched videos, 58 buyer questions).

## Who
People with an old or hard mattress who don't want to buy a new one, hot sleepers, couples, college students ("this good for college?"), parents doing a kid's bed. They search "are mattress toppers worth it" and "which mattress topper should i buy".

## Pain point experience
The mattress is hard, old or hot: "I have a California king bed and my mattress is super old and I always wake up with back pain. Will this help make it more comfortable?", "Has anyone tried this an old indented spring mattress?". They doubt the cooling: "I just need to know from someone if its cooling?", "You said it don’t get hot?". They are unsure how it goes on: "Do u put sheets over it?", "Does it have things to attach it securely though?". Searches: "do cooling mattress toppers work", "how to wash bamboo mattress topper", "can mattress toppers be washed".

## Communication style
Casual, joking, quick questions ("5,000 dollar mattress? sure ok"), price-skeptical, trusting other buyers. Short plain words.

## What it means for the script
1. Open on the hot mattress (their pain), then "skip the new mattress".
2. Say breathable + washer in plain words; don't promise "cooling" outright.
3. Show it going on like a fitted sheet (answers "does it attach").
