# Audience: Bare Anatomy Rosemary & Rice Water Hair Spray (2026-11-02)
Data: research/audience/questions.md (1,756 autocomplete phrases), comments.md (268 TikTok comments from 5 winner videos), voc.md.

## Who
Women (and some dads and moms buying for kids) with thinning, slow or dry hair who already heard rosemary water is the thing on TikTok. Many tried making it at home. Top searches: "does rosemary water grow hair", "how to make rosemary water", "rosemary water vs rosemary oil".

## Pain point experience
- They doubt it: "does rosemary hair spray work" and "Ok yall I keep seeing this does it actually work??"
- They don't know how to use it: "Do you rinse it out or leave on?", "should i put it on my roots or ends or both?", "do i use it while my hair is wet?"
- Homemade is a hassle: "how to make rosemary water", "Can we just make it at home and not pay? Because really???", "can rosemary water go bad".
- Oil worries: "rosemary water vs rosemary oil", "does rosemary water dry out hair".

## Communication style
Short, casual, lots of question marks and "girl"/"y'all". They trust seeing it used more than being told. The 71.5M-view video in this niche has no voice at all: one question on screen and the spray in use.

## What it means for the script
- Show exactly how to use it (damp roots after washing, rub in, no rinse): answers the top question with pictures only.
- Pain = the homemade pot and the jar in the fridge; the spray is the shortcut (listing: "alternative to homemade remedies").
- No "grows hair" promise and no timeframe; the label says "Helps Hair Appear Fuller & Voluminous".
- Water-based, not greasy answers the oil worry.
- 3-pack close: three bottles, three places.
