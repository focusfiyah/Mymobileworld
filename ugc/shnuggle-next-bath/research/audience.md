# Audience: Shnuggle toddler tub, "next bath after the infant tub" (2026-10-21)
Data: research/audience/questions.md (763 autocomplete phrases; seeds outgrew baby bath / when to stop using infant tub / toddler bath tub), research/audience/comments.md (136 TikTok comments, 7 videos), research/audience/voc.md (forum + comment quotes). Earlier job data: audience_2026-10-16/.

## Who
Parents (mostly moms) of babies around 6 to 18 months who are outgrowing the infant tub. Forum moms say it plainly: "At 5.5 months, LO is starting to get too big for her infant tub." They search "how to bathe baby when too big for baby bath" and "are toddler bath tubs worth it". Some are planners who save gear before they need it: "Do I have a kid? No did I add this into my cart for the future?YES".

## Pain point experience
1. The jump to the big tub feels too soon: "Does that mean she goes straight into the big-girl tub (ie. bathtub)?" and "She still seems so little!". One mom's son was "too big for his little whale tub, but still seems scared to be in the big tub unless we're with him."
2. Doubt the in-between product exists or fits: "do toddler bath tubs exist", "Does it actually fit in the bathtub? it looks bulky. I want to get one for my daughter".
3. Bought the wrong size: "I just ordered the baby version for mine because I thought this would be too big… but maybe I should’ve grabbed the toddler tub😵‍💫".
4. The stage itself is emotional: "Watching your baby move into a new stage is such a bittersweet feeling. They truly grow faster than our hearts are ready for." (353 likes).

## Communication style
Mom-to-mom, short, warm, a little funny. They react to seeing size side by side ("so helpful to see the tub size infant ratio 💐 thanks so much for posting!"), to plain feature proof, and to the milestone feeling. No expert talk, no hype.

## What it means for the script
- The brief's hook names this exact gap (outgrew the baby tub, not ready for the whole tub): keep it word for word.
- Add a pain beat right after it about the big tub (huge, lots of water), the forum worry.
- Show size: the infant tub next to the toddler tub, then the toddler tub inside the big tub ("does it fit" question).
- Features one by one with the part on screen (backrest, grippy floor, room for toys).
- A warm, bragging honest line ("they won't want to get out"), a real seasonal reason (holidays: gift buyers = more units), then "It's in the orange cart."
