Welcome to Ruiz Property Group

Ruiz Property Group is a real estate investment company that buys, renovates and sells residential properties across the New York tri-state area. We take distressed, outdated and neglected houses and turn them into modern homes with new value and purpose.

Some properties need small improvements and others need a full renovation. Either way, we look for the potential in a house that others pass over, then plan the work and carry it out with quality craftsmanship.

What we do

We buy houses
We buy residential properties in many conditions: houses that need repairs, outdated homes, vacant properties, inherited properties and distressed real estate.

We want selling to be simple for you. If repairs, money concerns or unexpected circumstances are making a sale hard, we will work with you to find a solution that fits.

We renovate and restore
After we buy a property, we improve its condition, how it works and how it looks. Our projects can include:

- Kitchen remodeling and upgrades
- Bathroom renovations
- Flooring installation and replacement
- Interior and exterior painting
- Roofing and exterior improvements
- Plumbing and electrical upgrades
- Landscaping and curb appeal
- Structural and general repairs
- Full interior and exterior renovations

Good renovation is more than appearance. It creates safe, functional and attractive homes for the people who will live in them.

We fix and flip houses
We start by finding properties with renovation potential. We evaluate the house, build a renovation plan and make the repairs and upgrades that add the most value. When the work is done, we prepare the property for sale, so buyers get a refreshed home without taking on the renovation themselves.

Our process

1. Find the opportunity. We look for properties with potential: homes that need repairs, updates or a full transformation.
2. Evaluate the property. We assess its condition, the renovation needed and its investment potential to decide if it is the right project.
3. Purchase the property. We work toward a purchase agreement that makes sense for both the property and the seller.
4. Renovate and improve. We coordinate the repairs and improvements that raise the home's function, appearance and market appeal.
5. Transform and resell. We prepare the finished home for the market and connect with buyers who want a move-in-ready house.

Our vision

To improve neighborhoods one property at a time, by turning neglected houses into attractive, functional homes that create new opportunities for owners, buyers and communities.
