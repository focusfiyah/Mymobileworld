# PA senior care home: license, rules and cost check (researched 2026-11-10)

Regulation text is from pacodeandbulletin.gov, which says it is current through 56 Pa.B. 6006 (Sept 5, 2026). Ch. 2600 means 55 Pa. Code Chapter 2600 (Personal Care Homes); Ch. 2800 means Chapter 2800 (Assisted Living Residences). Base URL for a section: `https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2X00/s2X00.NN.html`.

## Bottom line
The plan offers private baths, "treatments", "nursing care as permitted" and a nurse on every shift. That describes an **Assisted Living Residence (ALR, Ch. 2800)**, not a Personal Care Home. Only an ALR may offer supplemental health care services, and only an ALR can take Medicaid through CHC. The ALR building standard is much higher, though: units of 225 sq ft or more in new construction, a bathroom in each unit, and a kitchenette. A PCH is cheaper and faster to open, but it is a non-medical setting. The plan needs to name the license before any design work starts.

## 1. PCH vs ALR
| | Personal Care Home (Ch. 2600) | Assisted Living Residence (Ch. 2800) |
|---|---|---|
| Licensed by | DHS Office of Long-Term Living, Bureau of Human Services Licensing (BHSL), for both types ([pa.gov PCH/ALR licensing](https://www.pa.gov/en/agencies/dhs/resources/licensing/pch-alr-licensing.html), checked 2026-11-10) | Same |
| Who it serves | 4+ adults who "do not require the services in or of a licensed long-term care facility" and need help with ADLs or IADLs ([§2600.4](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2600/s2600.4.html)) | Must "provide or arrange" supplemental health care services through staff, contractors or outside providers, each licensed as the law requires ([§2800.142](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.142.html)) |
| Nursing | No nurse required and no supplemental health care services. Whether PCH staff nurses may give skilled care beyond medications is UNCONFIRMED (outside home health agencies are the usual route) | A licensed nurse in the building or on call at all times, employed or contracted ([§2800.60(d)](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.60.html)) |
| Medications given by non-nurses | Allowed after a DHS-approved med course with a competency test passed in the last 2 years. Covers oral, topical, eye, ear and nose drops, and epinephrine. Insulin also needs diabetes education within 12 months ([§2600.190](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2600/s2600.190.html)) | Same rule ([§2800.190](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.190.html)) |
| Mobility and evacuation | Residents with mobility needs are allowed. All residents must be able to leave the building within the time a fire safety expert sets in writing. Drills: monthly, plus one during sleeping hours every 6 months ([§2600.132](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2600/s2600.132.html)) | Same drill rule ([§2800.132](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.132.html)) |
| When the resident needs a nursing home | A person who needs long-term care facility level of care is outside the PCH definition | Excluded unless DHS grants an exception: ventilator, stage III/IV ulcers not healing, continuous IV, NG tube, restraints, or "continuous skilled nursing care 24 hours a day". Temporary or intermittent skilled care is allowed ([§2800.229](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.229.html)) |

Past these limits the resident needs a nursing facility, which the PA Department of Health licenses (28 Pa. Code Ch. 201-211). That citation was not re-checked this session. The client's admission profile (walks and eats on their own) fits either license. Its service list fits only an ALR.

## 2. Physical plant
| Item | PCH | ALR |
|---|---|---|
| Room size | Single bedroom 80 sq ft. Shared room 60 sq ft per person. 100 sq ft per person with mobility needs. Up to 4 per room ([§2600.101](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2600/s2600.101.html)) | New construction (after 2011-01-18): **225 sq ft** single or 300 shared, not counting bathroom or closet. Buildings that existed before that date: 160 single or 210 shared. Max 2 per unit, sharing only by choice. Lockable door and phone jack ([§2800.101](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.101.html)) |
| Bathroom | 1 toilet and 1 sink per 6 users. 1 tub or shower per 10 users ([§2600.102](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2600/s2600.102.html)) | A toilet, sink and tub or shower **in each living unit**, plus one wheelchair-accessible public restroom ([§2800.102](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.102.html)) |
| Kitchen | None in the room | New construction: cabinet, bar sink with hot and cold water, and outlets for a microwave and small fridge. The fridge and cooktop are supplied if the resident wants them (§2800.101) |
| Common space | Not pulled this session | 2+ wheelchair-accessible common rooms. Lounge space of 15 sq ft per unit, large enough to seat all residents ([§2800.98](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.98.html)) |
| Fire alarms | Smoke detector within 15 ft of each bedroom. Interconnected detectors at 9+ residents. Fire department or 24 h monitoring link at 5+ residents with mobility needs ([§2600.130](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2600/s2600.130.html)) | Smoke detector in each unit. Same interconnect and monitoring rules ([§2800.130](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.130.html)) |
| Sprinklers and building code | Neither chapter sets a sprinkler rule. A written fire safety approval from L&I, DOH or the local code official is required before licensing, again after renovation, and every 3 years ([§2800.14](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.14.html); PCH §2600.14) | Same |

**10 vs 20 residents:** PA's UCC moved to the 2021 ICC codes on 2026-01-01 ([AIA Philadelphia](https://aiaphiladelphia.org/news/advancing-architecture-and-design/1578/1578-BUILDING-CODE-UPDATE-2021-International-Codes-will-take-effect-on-January-1-)). The model IBC classes care homes with 6-16 residents as Group R-4. Above 16 a home generally becomes Group I-1, which is institutional and brings tougher construction and sprinkler rules (NFPA 13/13R) ([NFSA R-4 guide](https://nfsa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/R-4-4.pdf); [Pittsburgh PLI bulletin](https://www.pittsburghpa.gov/files/assets/city/v/1/pli/documents/pli-bulletins/15612_bulletin_-_care_in_sfd-tfd.pdf)). Pennsylvania's own classification is UNCONFIRMED and needs a code official's ruling. **Design for 20 now**, because a 10-bed R-4 building may not legally grow to 20.

## 3. Staffing minimums
| | PCH | ALR |
|---|---|---|
| Direct care hours | 1 h/day per mobile resident. 2 h/day per resident with mobility needs. 75% of those hours during waking hours ([§2600.57](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2600/s2600.57.html)) | Same: 1 h and 2 h, 75% while awake ([§2800.57](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.57.html)) |
| On site at all times | A direct care worker aged 21+ | Same |
| Awake at night | All staff awake at 16+ residents. At least 1 awake if any resident has mobility needs ([§2600.58](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2600/s2600.58.html)) | All direct care staff awake at all times ([§2800.58](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.58.html)) |
| Nurse | Not required | In the building or on call 24/7 (§2800.60) |
| Administrator | 21+. RN, LPN plus 1 yr, NHA, or associate degree/60 credits. For 8 or fewer residents, HS diploma plus 2 yrs ([§2600.53](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2600/s2600.53.html)). 100-hour course, competency test, 24 h/yr ([§2600.64](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2600/s2600.64.html)) | 21+. RN, LPN or NHA each plus 1 yr, or associate degree plus 1-2 yrs ([§2800.53](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.53.html)). 100-hour course, competency test, 24 h/yr ([§2800.64](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.64.html)). | Staff training | Fire orientation on day 1. Orientation within 40 work hours. DHS course and competency test before unsupervised care. 12 h/yr ([§2600.65](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2600/s2600.65.html)) | First aid and CPR. 18 h of training plus the competency test before unsupervised care. 16 h/yr plus dementia training ([§2800.65](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.65.html)) |

**Background checks (OAPSA, Act 169 of 1996):** a PA State Police check for every applicant. An FBI fingerprint check, run through the Dept of Aging (vendor IDEMIA, $26.20 since 2024-06-15), for anyone who has not lived in PA for the past 2 years ([Aging: criminal history checks](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/aging/resources-for-aging-professionals/criminal-history-bkgnd-check); [fee circular](https://www.aging.pa.gov/organization/advocacy-and-protection/Documents/Information%20Circular%20-%20Fee%20Increase%208-1-2022.pdf)). The fee amount came from search snippets and the pages were not opened.

The plan's 48 staff-hours a day is far above the minimum: 10 h/day for 10 mobile residents, 20 h/day for 20.

## 4. Licensing process and fees
- **Forms:** HS 633 application, Verification of Affiliations, HS 2126 civil rights form, pre-licensure checklist by type. Pay the first fee by check or money order. The ALR per-bed fee is paid only after DHS sends a fee letter ([DHS application page](https://www.pa.gov/en/agencies/dhs/resources/licensing/pch-alr-licensing/app-for-license.html)).
- **Fees:** PCH $15 for 0-20 beds ([§2600.11](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2600/s2600.11.html)). ALR $300 application plus $75 per bed, which can rise each year with CPI ([§2800.11](https://www.pacodeandbulletin.gov/Display/pacode?file=/secure/pacode/data/055/chapter2800/s2800.11.html)). For an ALR that is **$1,050 at 10 beds and $1,800 at 20**.
- **Steps:** zoning, then building permit and certificate of occupancy, then fire safety approval. Next the administrator course and test, then the application. DHS inspects before the home may open, and re-inspects an ALR within 3 months of licensing (§2800.11(b)).
- **Timeline:** UNCONFIRMED. DHS publishes none.
- **10 to 20 beds:** a PCH needs DHS approval before admitting residents above its licensed capacity ([§2600.13](https://d1tvxue8o4w0fl.cloudfront.net/regulation/pennsylvania-code-rules-and-regulations/title-55-human-services/part-iv-adult-services-manual/subpart-e-residential-agenciesfacilitiesservices/chapter-2600-personal-care-homes/general-requirements/section-260013-maximum-capacity)). A home licensed for 8 or fewer cannot use self-inspection to go to 9+ ([DHS self-inspection procedures](https://dhs.pa.gov/Licensing/BHSL-Licensing/Documents/Procedures_for_Self-Inspection_to_Increase_Maximum_Capacity.pdf)). The parallel ALR rule (§2800.13) is UNCONFIRMED. Either way, expansion needs a new fire safety approval, the added per-bed fees, and the code issue in section 2.

## 5. Market numbers and staffing cost
**PA private-pay median, CareScout 2025 survey** (published 2026-03-02, [tables](https://assets.carescout.com/x/8fcb50422f/282102.pdf)): assisted living community **$6,480/mo** ($77,760/yr). Nursing home semi-private room $11,954/mo, private room $13,688/mo. CareScout's assisted living figure does not separate PCHs from ALRs, so a PCH-only median is UNCONFIRMED.

**PA wages, May 2025 OEWS** (PA Dept of Labor & Industry statewide table, [PDF](https://www.pa.gov/content/dam/copapwp-pagov/en/dli/documents/cwia/products/occupational-wages/pa_ow.pdf)):
| Job | Average hourly | Median annual |
|---|---|---|
| RN | $45.20 | $96,430 |
| LPN | $31.70 | $63,940 |
| Nursing assistant (CNA) | $21.14 | $44,590 |

**1 LPN + 1 CNA around the clock (8,760 h each), plus 25% payroll burden:**
- LPN: 31.70 × 8,760 = $277,692
- CNA: 21.14 × 8,760 = $185,186
- Wages together: $462,878. × 1.25 = **$578,598 a year**, or $48,217 a month
- **Per resident per month: 10 residents = $4,822. 20 residents = $2,411.**
- With an RN instead of the LPN: 45.20 × 8,760 = $395,952, plus the CNA = $581,138, × 1.25 = $726,423 a year. That is $6,054 per resident per month at 10 residents and $3,027 at 20.

At 10 residents, the two nursing posts alone take about 74% of the $6,480 median rate, before the administrator, cook, housekeeping, relief cover and overtime, food, rent or insurance. At 20 residents they take about 37%.

## 6. Who pays
- **Medicaid (CHC):** since 2025 the CHC plans may place nursing-home-eligible members in an **ALR only** as an "In Lieu of Service". PCHs are excluded. Medicaid does not pay room and board. In 2025, 15 people used it, and 18 of PA's 64 ALRs contract with a CHC plan ([PHLP, 2026-05-01](https://www.phlp.org/en/news/for-the-first-time-medicaid-can-help-pay-for-assisted-living-in-pennsylvania); [OLTL ILOS policy, 2026-03-11](https://paproviders.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/OLTL-LTSS-3.11.2026-ILOS-OPTION-IN-CHC-Policy-Final-V4.pdf)). HB 821 (2025) would add assisted living as a covered CHC service; its status is UNCONFIRMED.
- **SSI State Supplement:** for a personal care boarding home it is $639.30/mo, which with the federal $994 makes **$1,633.30/mo** in 2026 ([SSA POMS SI PHI01415.058](https://secure.ssa.gov/poms.nsf/lnx/0501415058)). That is income for a low-income PCH resident, not a rate paid to the home.
- **Private pay is the norm.** This is inferred from the facts above: Medicaid pays only for services in a few ALRs, and nothing pays a PCH directly.
