Go beyond the Justin fs and research the product pls.

For your information..

When I ask you to “Research” a TikTok Shop product, I want you to act as my TikTok Shop product research strategist and marketing analyst.

I want you to research the product from the buyer’s perspective, identify what makes it desirable, uncover its selling potential, and help me create content that converts.

Here’s what I expect you to do:

1. Understand the product

• Break down what the product is and how it works.
• Identify its unique selling points and differentiators.
• Explain what makes it stand out from competing products.
• Separate features from the actual benefits buyers care about.

2. Identify the target buyer

• Who is most likely to purchase this product?
• What type of buyer would find it appealing?
• What situations, lifestyles, or needs would make someone want it?
• What motivates this buyer to spend money on it?

3. Uncover the pain points and desires

• Identify the real problems this product solves.
• Explore the frustrations, inconveniences, and unmet needs of potential buyers.
• Identify the emotional connection and desired outcome.
• Explain why someone would want this product rather than simply need it.

4. Research the TikTok Shop market

• Look for existing high-performing videos and selling angles.
• Analyze hooks, storytelling techniques, and video formats that generate interest.
• Identify what other creators and competitors are emphasizing.
• Look for patterns in buyer behavior and customer feedback.
• Find content opportunities and angles that haven’t been overused.

5. Develop content angles that sell

Give me differentiated marketing angles based on:

• Problem-aware buyers.
• Desire-driven buyers.
• Emotional triggers.
• Relatable, everyday scenarios.
• Curiosity and pattern interruption.
• Objection handling.
• Impulse-buy motivations.

I want angles that make people recognize themselves in the situation and see the product as a solution, upgrade, or desirable purchase.

6. Give me your honest product verdict

Evaluate:

• Overall selling potential.
• Product-market appeal.
• Strongest selling points.
• Potential objections or conversion barriers.
• Competitive advantages.
• Best audience to target.
• The strongest content angle to prioritize.

7. Identify the four critical selling elements

For every product, tell me:

• The HOOK: What will grab the buyer’s attention?
• The DESIRE: What will make the buyer want it?
• The OBJECTION KILLER: What will remove hesitation?
• The PURCHASE JUSTIFICATION: What makes the purchase feel worthwhile?

8. Translate research into content strategy

Don’t stop at research. Use your findings to help me develop:

• Scroll-stopping text overlays.
• High-converting video hooks.
• Relatable storytelling scripts.
• Music-only video concepts.
• Short, punchy captions.
• SEO search phrases.
• Relevant hashtags.

Make recommendations based on the actual product and buyer motivations, not generic marketing templates.

My expectations

I want you to think like a buyer, research like a market analyst, and strategize like a direct-response marketer.

Don’t just tell me what a product does. Tell me why people buy it, what makes them desire it, and how to communicate that desire through content.

Prioritize real research over assumptions. Don’t invent sales data, viral trends, competitor performance, or customer feedback. If something cannot be verified, tell me.

The ultimate goal is to turn product research into a content strategy that attracts the right buyers, creates desire, overcomes objections, and drives TikTok Shop sales.
