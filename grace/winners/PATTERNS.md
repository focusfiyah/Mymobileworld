## Shared patterns (what to keep)
1. **The product is on screen in the first second** in all 6 (the dilator, the glasses on the dash, the bag, the box, the bottle, the pendant). Nobody waits for the product.
2. **A short text line on the first frame** in all 6 ("POV: someone's snoring is ruining your sleep 😭", "blinding headlights at night are SCARY 😩", "the last thing you want in your pantry... 😳", the comment sticker, "the necklace for your abundance era...✨"). Grace added these in TikTok. Our rule stays no burned-in text unless Ralph says yes (Ralph 2026-10-20: no change for now); the versions list optional lines Grace can type.
3. **One everyday problem the viewer can picture**, said in the first sentence: snoring partner, headlight glare, mice in the pantry, phones at game night, messy oil. The lowest views per sale (glasses, 463) has the most specific scene.
4. **A real deadline**: holiday shared rooms, clocks go back Nov 1, cold weather, Thanksgiving, "this is your year". 5 of 6 have one; the one without (BodyJ) is the reply video, where the comment does that job.
5. **The CTA asks for more than one**: "grab one for a friend too", "grab two bags and do the whole house", "one for your house and one for your grandma's". 3 of 5 Grace videos.
6. **The main objection is answered in one line, before the CTA**: "fit right over your regular ones", "safe around kids and pets", "no traps to empty", "no waiting your turn", "no pouring, no oily fingers".
7. **Practical, giftable products under one clear use**: sleep, driving, pests, a family game, a beauty tool. Easy to understand in one sentence.
8. **Faceless works**: 5 of the 6 are hands + voiceover, no face. All AI made.
9. **Grace's own wording on top of our draft**: 4 of the 5 Grace videos were re-worded when posted (new opener like "Listen," or "I wish someone thought of this sooner", the trial stat and jokes cut, a warmer CTA: "I linked it in the orange cart for you", "I'mma do y'all a favor"). Her version is the one that sold. The BodyJ video is her own script word for word.

## Things to watch
- **Update 2026-10-20: BodyJ (the reply video) is up to 3 sales** (1,578 views, 526 views per sale): the reply-to-a-real-comment format is the strongest lead so far.
- **Views per sale is the number to beat**, not views. Mute had 8 times the views of the glasses for the same one sale: a broad pain brings reach, a specific scene brings buyers.
- **The reply video started fastest** (BodyJ: 763 views a day). It answers a real comment on her viral cradle-cap video, so the audience already wanted castor oil. Reply videos to real comments are worth more tests.
- **The brands paid to promote 4 of the 6** (Mute, glasses, SoFyre, BodyJ: TikTok's ad label; Grace paid nothing, Ralph 2026-10-20). Their views are partly paid, so compare views per sale within the same group (promoted vs not). The two without promotion: Throw Hands (679 views) and Tiger Eye (61). The sheet's Promoted column shows which.
- **Hooks**: only Mute used a library hook word for word (H061). The others are Grace's rewording, so the new versions use her looser wording on top of a library hook (Ralph 2026-10-20).
- **Claims**: the Tiger Eye video says "these keep selling out" (can't back it, on the gate's banned list) and the posted BodyJ says "hair growing longer" (growth claim the listing can't back). Both are left out of the new versions.
- **Tiger Eye**: 1 sale on 61 views is the best ratio but the smallest sample; one more sale or none changes the picture a lot.

## What the 12 new versions test
Each version changes one thing from the list above (see `VERSIONS.md`): a sharper single scene, the deadline as the
hook, the objection as the hook, a gift angle that asks for two, show-don't-explain, the proof moment in the first
second, a 15 s cut. Log every new video in the tracker with its version id (for example `mute-A`) in the Note column, so
the sales show which change paid.
