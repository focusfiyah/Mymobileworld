## What she wants (the rules we take from it)
1. **Real product in her real hand opens and closes the video.** The hook is her real footage (the LED lighting up
   reads as "this is real"), and the CTA is a fast run of real shots ending on the real box. AI clips carry the middle.
2. **AI clips for the lines a real phone shot can't show well:** the pain (hands under the tap, the dirty towel) and
   each feature as it is said (bristles, vibration button, heat). Her Oct 23 post had only product shots over the
   pain lines and did not sell; the sold one shows the pain.
3. **Every cut lands on the start of its phrase.** One shot per phrase in the middle (1.2-3 s each), then very fast
   cuts (0.2-0.6 s) over the CTA lines. Average shot 1.5 s; our V1 clips were 4 s each.
4. **The face-use shot goes on the "spa feel" line, not the hook.** Lather on the cheek is the payoff, after the
   features, not the opener.
5. **Pretty hand-free shots get cut.** She dropped our counter dolly and end shot. Over the close she wants motion
   in her hand, not a still-life.
6. **Her own text line on the first frame** ("you're still washing you face.. without THIS???"). She types it in
   TikTok; we still burn in nothing unless Ralph says yes.
7. **Her own voice, recorded once and reused** on both posts. She does not use our ElevenLabs track.
