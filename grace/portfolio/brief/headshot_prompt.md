UGC PORTFOLIO HEADSHOT ENHANCER
I’m uploading a photo I want to use as my UGC creator portfolio headshot.
Enhance this exact photo and optimize it into a clean, premium 4:5 creator headshot.
MOST IMPORTANT: Do not change my physical appearance.
My face and identity must remain as close to the original uploaded photo as possible. Do not beautify, reinterpret, regenerate, or “improve” what I look like.
Keep my:

* Facial features and face shape
* Eyes, nose, mouth, smile and teeth
* Skin tone and natural skin texture
* Hair, hairline and hairstyle
* Body proportions
* Age and natural characteristics
* Jewelry/accessories unless necessary for a minor cleanup

This should feel like the same photo was professionally enhanced, NOT like an AI-generated headshot.
Improve only what makes the existing photo more portfolio-ready:

* Increase image quality, sharpness and resolution naturally
* Improve exposure and lighting
* Correct distracting shadows, glare or color casts
* Make skin tones look natural and accurate
* Improve overall clarity without excessive sharpening
* Clean up minor background distractions when needed
* Make the environment slightly cleaner, brighter and more premium
* Preserve realistic phone-camera texture and natural imperfections
* Crop/reframe to 4:5 when possible without awkwardly cutting off the subject

Keep the original environment and context. Do not randomly move me into a studio, office, luxury home, outdoor location, or completely different setting.
If the environment needs improvement, make a believable upgraded version of the same environment. For example, if the photo was taken in a car, keep me in a car. You can make the interior cleaner, improve the natural lighting, or subtly improve details such as the windows or sunroof, but it must still look like a real photograph taken in that setting.
Any environmental changes must make physical sense. Avoid warped architecture, impossible reflections, duplicated objects, strange windows, broken car interiors, malformed furniture, or other AI artifacts.
Do NOT:

* Reshape or reconstruct my face
* Smooth away real skin texture
* Make me younger or more conventionally attractive
* Change my eye shape or eye color
* Change my teeth or smile
* Change my nose, jawline, cheeks or lips
* Add unrealistic makeup
* Change my hairstyle unnecessarily
* Change my body shape
* Give me artificial-looking skin
* Add dramatic cinematic lighting
* Create excessive background blur
* Make the image look like a corporate AI headshot
* Make the environment unrealistically perfect

Target aesthetic: authentic, high-quality UGC creator. Approachable, natural, confident and premium. It should look like I happened to take an exceptionally good photo of myself with a high-quality phone camera in flattering natural light.
Final test: Someone who knows me should look at the finished image and think, “That’s a really good photo of you,” not “That looks AI-generated.”
Use the uploaded image as the source of truth and make the minimum necessary changes to achieve that result.
