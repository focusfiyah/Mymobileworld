# Reply templates (Grace, draft for Ralph's OK)

From the course's Brand Response Templates (calls 10/14, 10/21). Rule: get the details before giving a price.

## The four questions (always first)
1. How many videos do you need?
2. How will you use them: your page, or as ads?
3. How long do you want to use them for?
4. What budget do you have for this?

## 1. A brand messages with no details
Hi [name], thanks for reaching out! I'd love to hear more. How many videos are you thinking, and will they run on
your page or as ads? Do you have a budget in mind? Then I can send you a few ideas.

## 2. Gifted only (free product, no pay)
Hi [name], thank you for thinking of me! I only take paid projects right now, starting at $500 a video. If that works,
I'd love to send you two or three ideas for [product].

## 3. A brand she sells for on TikTok Shop, turned into a paid client
Hi [name], my video for your [product] sold [N] units on TikTok Shop. I have a few new ideas I think would do even
better as ads. Would you be open to a paid video, or a flat fee plus commission?

## 4. Always add (new brands)
Quick question: where did you find me? (log the answer in LEADS.md)

Every reply is checked for CAN-SPAM basics when sent by email (real sender, honest subject) and goes out only after
Ralph's OK.
