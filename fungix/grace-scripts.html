Fungix Scripts for Grace

Say this line exactly, in every body take

It's made to target fungal growth and support healthier-looking nails.

Never say cures, kills, gets rid of or fixes. Never say how fast it works.

Before you film

  • Say numbers as words: "twenty-five percent", "two to three times a day".
  • Keep every body take whole, start to finish. We don't mix pieces from different body takes.
  • Send the camera's original HD files (1080p .MOV), not exported copies.
  • Normal, energetic pace. All the pauses get cut in the edit.
  • Say the "only catch" line calmly, like a friend telling you. It's what makes the rest believable.
  • Close-ups (bottle label, brush, socks, boots) can be anyone's hands and feet.
Why these scripts work
Pain first.The hook names something the viewer already hates, like tucking toes under the table or always picking dark polish.
Sell the moment.Each angle puts them in one specific scene: shoes off at a friend's house, the salon polish wall, the beach.
Move away from pain.Escaping embarrassment works faster than promising pretty nails. The product is the way out.
Clarity, not convincing.A calm line on why hiding or painting over it doesn't help. No pressure words.
Remove uncertainty.Every body says what's in it, how to use it and how often, before anyone has to ask.
Detachment builds trust.Every body names the catch (it's every day). Every CTA lets people scroll on if it's not for them.
Edit and compliance notes (for Ralph)
  • Every body keeps "This oil has…" so the approved search-result card lands on "has" automatically.
  • Hooks with "yellow, thick, funky" bring back the condition photo collage (in on yellow or thick, out on funky).
  • No before/after photos. Condition-only photos are fine.
  • No "selling out" unless it's true. Use "don't put it off."
  • No personal-use lines. Grace never says she uses it, so nothing depends on her own story.
  • The brand's "results within a month" line is left out on purpose, because timeframes aren't allowed.
  • Use a different on-screen text bubble per video so combos read as different ads.

The moment: a whole summer of toes tucked under the table, then relief when boots come back.

Why it works: pain first, then a low-pressure reason to start now. Nobody's looking, so there's no risk. Urgency without fake scarcity.

Hook

Hook A

If you spent all summer tucking your toes under the table so nobody saw the yellow, thick, funky nail, I see you. And now that boots are back, you're about to forget about it until next June.

Hook B

Nobody is looking at your feet for the next six months. That's exactly why now is the time to deal with your toenails, not the week before sandal season.

Clarity

It's not one-and-done. You have to keep up with it.

CTA

CTA B

CTA B
