## #1 Missed calls and slow replies

**Who has it:** Local service businesses where the owner is busy with customers when the phone rings: HVAC, plumbers, roofers, auto repair, cleaners, tax preparers, insurance agents, med spas. Brooklyn businesses like Jean's are the easiest place to start.

**Proof it hurts:**
- Invoca looked at 70 million business calls this year. 44% went unanswered (https://justcall.io/blog/missed-call-statistics.html).
- CallRail asked 1,000 US buyers. 78% have given up on a business after nobody picked up. Only 42% leave a voicemail, and 21% call the next business right away (https://beside.com/blog/missed-calls-cost-small-business).
- A 2026 survey of 500 marketers found only 3% reply to a new lead within 5 minutes (https://neilpatel.com/marketing-stats/response-time-new-leads/).
- An owner on r/smallbusiness this January: "we answer the phone, and return calls and emails within 24 business hours. That's quickly becoming the 'outside the box'" (https://www.reddit.com/r/smallbusiness/comments/1q71vdk/most_hvac_companies_compete_on_price_i_competed/).

Skip the "85% never call back" number you'll see in ads. Nobody can find where it came from.

**The offer:** "Every call answered on the second ring, day and night. The assistant books the job or takes the details and texts or emails you in under a minute. Each week you get a list of every call and what it was worth." We set it up, write its script from their business, fix it every week, and send the report. They never touch the tech.

**Price:** My estimate: $500 setup plus $297 to $497 a month. Self-serve AI receptionist apps cost $50 to $150 a month (https://www.myaifrontdesk.com/blogs/how-much-does-an-ai-receptionist-cost-a-straight-answer-for-2026). So we charge for the setup, the custom script, the weekly tuning and the report, things the owner would never do in an app. A human answering service runs from about $130 to $1,600 a month (https://www.getjobber.com/academy/plumbing/plumbing-answering-service/).

**How we deliver it:** The same setup as Jean's line: an ElevenLabs agent on a phone number, transfer to the owner during hours, a message after hours, every call logged to a sheet. ElevenLabs charges about $0.08 a minute plus the AI model and phone line (https://www.getmacha.com/blog/elevenlabs-agents-pricing-explained). My estimate: a business with 300 call minutes a month costs us $40 to $60, so a $397 plan keeps more than $330. I build and tune it in cloud sessions. You only do the sales calls, and those work from your phone.

**First client:** Jean's line is the demo: anyone can call 718-576-2667 and hear it. Then a free "missed call check": I pull 30 Brooklyn service businesses from Google Maps, you call each one yourself once after 6pm, and we write down who didn't pick up. Those owners get a short message: "I called you Tuesday at 7:40pm and nobody answered. Here's what that call would have sounded like with us." Each one gets a 2-minute demo agent built on their own business (a few cents each to test, I'd ask first). Offer the first three a free 14-day trial in exchange for a testimonial.

**Watch out:** This market is crowded with people sending cold "AI agent" emails, and keeping clients is harder than getting them (https://ciela.ai/blogs/is-ai-agency-market-saturated-reddit). Two things help: a demo built on their business, and the weekly report that shows the money. The agent only answers calls. It never calls anyone, which keeps us away from the calling laws.

## #2 Product sellers need new video ads all the time

**Who has it:** TikTok Shop, Amazon and Shopify sellers spending on ads. The best target is brands with $20k to $200k a month in sales and no in-house video person.

**Proof it hurts:**
- In a Meta test, one ad set with 25 different videos got 17% more sales at 16% lower cost than 5 ad sets with 5 videos each. Ads now wear out in 2 to 3 weeks instead of 6 or more (https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-update-creative-strategy-2026).
- Agencies now tell brands to launch 10 to 20 new ads a week (https://billo.app/blog/meta-andromeda-update/).
- A human UGC video costs about $175 (https://influee.co/blog/ugc-price). At 10 a week that's over $7,000 a month.
- TikTok Shop US sales should reach about $23.4 billion this year, and sellers are already switching to AI videos because they're fast and cheap (https://aiweekly.co/alerts/tiktok-shop-sellers-turn-to-ai-avatars-sidelining-creators).
- A hair care seller on r/TikTokShop in September: "$50k a month," ads costing more than they bring in ("ACOS>150%"), asking how to get more videos made (https://www.reddit.com/r/TikTokshop/comments/1wamw9d/hello_sellers_need_help_increasing_video_count/).

**The offer:** "10 new ad videos a month for your product, each one different (new hook, new room, new shots), made from your real product photos so the product never changes." That's the same system we use for Grace.

**Price:** My estimate: $1,000 to $1,500 a month for 10 videos, or $99 to $149 each. That's under half what human UGC costs.

**How we deliver it:** The Grace pipeline: scripts, product sheet, stills, Omni or Flow clips, Grace's QC rules, AI finish. Kie cost per finished Grace video this month ran $2.09 to $6.60. The real cost is your review time. Most Grace jobs took several rounds.

**First client:** Brands that send Grace free samples already know her. We make one free sample ad for their product from the footage we already have, and pitch: "We made this one for you. Want 10 a month for your own ads?"

**Watch out:** Buyers are cooling on AI creator content. One report says liking for it fell from about 60% in 2023 to about 26% this year (https://www.metaintro.com/blog/ai-avatars-tiktok-shop-23-billion-creator-jobs-2026). Our sales proof is thin so far: 6 AI videos with 1 sale each in Grace's tracker. This takes more of your time than #1, so I'd add it second.

## #3 Reviews go stale

**Who has it:** The same local businesses as #1, so it's an easy add-on.

**Proof it hurts:**
- BrightLocal's 2026 survey of 1,002 US adults: 74% look for reviews from the last 3 months. 31% only use businesses rated 4.5 stars or higher, up from 17% a year before (https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/).
- 80% are more likely to use a business that replies to every review, but half are put off by copy-paste replies (https://www.pinmeto.com/news/brightlocal-local-consumer-review-survey-2026/).
- So a business with great reviews from 2023 now looks worse than one with fresh reviews.

**The offer:** "Fresh reviews every month, and a real reply to every review in your own voice." After each job the customer gets a short email, a WhatsApp message or a QR card asking for a review. We write a reply to every new review for the owner to approve.

**Price:** My estimate: $197 a month alone, or $150 when added to #1.

**How we deliver it:** Email and QR cards cost almost nothing. Texts need business texting registration first (the same rule as Jean's). The replies are drafted by Claude, and the owner approves them.

**First client:** Every #1 client after their first month.

**Watch out:** We ask every customer, never only the happy ones. Google bans picking who gets asked (https://searchengineland.com/google-review-guidelines-say-discouraging-negative-or-selectively-soliciting-positive-reviews-is-not-allowed-388541), and the FTC bans fake reviews and hiding bad ones (https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials). No fake reviews, ever.

## Is done-for-you the best model?

| Model | Money speed | Grows without you | Fits a phone-only setup | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Affiliate links (Day One AI now) | Slow | Yes | Yes | Needs big traffic; 34 videos have 3,047 views so far |
| Coaching or a course | Medium | Some | Yes | Needs trust and results to teach from; we're not there yet |
| Software (sell the app) | Slow | Yes | No | Up against $50 apps with big teams |
| Custom agency work | Fast | No | Yes | Every client is a new project; burns your time |
| Productized done-for-you, monthly fee | Fast | Partly | Yes | Same setup for every client, fixed price, paid monthly |

**My pick:** Productized done-for-you on a monthly fee. It's done-for-you, but with one fixed setup we copy for every client instead of building something new each time. That's how 10 clients stay manageable. My estimate: 10 clients on #1 at $397 is about $3,970 a month, and about $3,400 after our costs.

Something better than plain done-for-you: bundle #1 and #3 for the same owner as one monthly "front desk" plan. One sale covers two problems, and clients cancel less when we handle more of their day.
