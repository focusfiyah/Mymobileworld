# The offer: one fixed plan (draft for Ralph, 2026-10-26)

Working name: **Always Answered** (Ralph picks the final name; options at the bottom).

## Front Desk plan

**Price:** $397 a month, $497 one-time setup. Month to month, cancel any time.

**Founding offer:** the first 3 businesses pay no setup fee and get the first 14 days free, in exchange for a short testimonial if they're happy.

**Calls (pain #1):**
- An assistant answers every call, day and night, on the business's own number (they forward calls to us; no new number for their customers).
- During the owner's hours it can pass the call to the owner. Otherwise it books the job or takes the caller's name, number and what they need.
- The owner gets the details by email within a minute of the call ending.
- It answers as the business's assistant and says the call may be recorded. If a caller asks, it says it's an AI assistant. It never pretends to be a person.
- It only answers calls. It never calls anyone.
- Up to 500 call minutes a month are included. Over that: $0.25 a minute (my estimate; our cost is about $0.10 to $0.20).
- Every Monday: a report of every call, who it was, what they wanted and what got booked.
- We write its script from their business (services, prices, hours, area) and fix it every week from the real calls.

**Reviews (pain #3):**
- After each job the customer gets a short email or WhatsApp message, or scans a QR card at the counter, asking for a Google review.
- We ask every customer the same way, happy or not (Google bans asking only happy customers).
- We write a reply to every new review in the owner's voice. The owner approves it before it posts.
- Monthly count of new reviews and the star average.
- No fake reviews, ever. Text-message requests come later, after business texting registration.

**What the business gives us:** a 15-minute call or a form with services, prices, hours, area, and who gets transferred calls; call forwarding turned on (we send steps for their phone company); a list of finished jobs each week (or access to their booking tool) for review requests.

**What it costs us (my estimate):** ElevenLabs about $0.08 a minute plus the AI model, Twilio number $1.15 a month plus $0.0085 a minute. A business using 300 minutes: about $40 to $60. Review emails: about $0.

**Money (my estimate):** 10 clients x $397 = $3,970 a month, about $3,400 after costs.
