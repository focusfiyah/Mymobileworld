# Wag Bag scripts for Grace

Approved by Ralph 2026-09-30 and sent to Grace as a PDF. Reference: @mamarashaah TikTok, 50 s, one talking-head take,
headline "WHERE are you gonna POOP‼️💩" + label "doomsday pooping 101 💩" + word-by-word captions.

5 talking-head scripts for the Wag Bag 12-pack, about 30 seconds each. Talk to camera with the box in hand the whole time.

[Brackets] = what to do or what to fill in. [Insert] = an optional 2 to 3 second close-up Ralph puts over your voice.

Film the hook, body and CTA as separate takes, with 2 or 3 hook takes each.

## Why the reference video works

- In the first 3 seconds she lists food, water and electricity, then asks where you're going to poop.
- She says nobody else is talking about it.
- The box stays in her hands the whole time.
- She names what's in the kit and jokes about it.
- She compares it to a bucket and cat litter.
- She ends on a joke.

## Quick rules

- Box in your hands the whole video.
- Keep pauses under a second.
- Say "hand wipe," not "hand sanitizer."
- Skip "so cute," "obsessed" and "you need this."

## Angle 1: The prep list everyone forgets

Closest to the reference: same beats, new words.

On-screen headline: "Your emergency bin is missing this 💩" · Label: "emergency kit 101"

**HOOK** [to camera, box under one arm, counting on your fingers]

"Water. Food. Flashlight. Batteries. And where are you gonna poop?"

**BODY** [pull one kit out and hold it up]

"If the water's off, your toilet gets one flush, the one in the tank, and that's it. So this is the Original Wag Bag. The powder's already in the bag and turns pee into gel. You seal it in the zip bag and it goes in the regular trash. Toilet paper and a hand wipe come in every kit, and you can use a bag more than once."

## Angle 2: Power's out, water's off

For apartments and storm season (hurricane season runs through November). Film it in your bathroom so the toilet is behind you.

On-screen headline: "Power's out. Water's off. Now what? 🚽" · Label: "apartment emergency 101"

**HOOK** [to camera in the bathroom, box in hand]

"Power's out, the water just stopped, and you have to go. What's the plan?"

**BODY** [hold up one kit]

"In a lot of apartment buildings the water runs on electric pumps, so when the power goes, the water can go too. This bag goes right in your toilet, under the seat." [thumb over your shoulder at the toilet] "The powder gels it, you seal it in the zip bag, and it goes in the regular trash."

[Insert: the bag set in the dry bowl under the seat]

"Twelve kits for [price], and each bag takes more than one use."

**CTA**

"Don't wait for a storm on the news. Orange cart."

Alternate hooks:

- "If your apartment loses power, you might lose water too."
- "Your toilet has exactly one flush left when the water shuts off."

## Angle 3: The road trip bathroom emergency

## Angle 5: The gel test

The reference only describes what the powder does; this one shows it. Before filming, pour two cups of plain water into one bag and time how long it takes to gel. Then talk to camera holding that bag and say only what you saw.

On-screen headline: "Does the powder actually work? 🧪" · Label: "tested it so you don't have to"

**HOOK** [to camera, holding the tested bag upside down]

"Everybody says this powder turns pee into gel, so I poured two cups of water in to see."

**BODY** [still holding the bag]

"It took about [__], and now it's [what you see]." [squeeze or tilt it toward the camera] "The box says it holds up to 32 ounces, so that was half. Every kit also comes with a zip bag to seal it in, toilet paper and a hand wipe. What I don't love: [your con]."

[Insert: 2 to 3 seconds of the pour, if you filmed it]

"Twelve kits for [price]."

**CTA**

"Orange cart if you want some before you need them."

Alternate hooks:

- "I poured water in a wag bag to see what the powder really does."
- "Before you buy these, look at what happened when I got one wet."
