# TikTok Shop slideshows (shoppable photos / photo carousels)

A swipeable photo post with TikTok Shop products tagged on it. Viewers swipe, or TikTok auto-advances after a few
seconds. No filming or video editing, so it is a cheap way to post a product, but it is still a sales post: it needs
a hook, proof and a close like any video. Learned from Ralph 2026-10-19; facts checked that day against the sources
below. Where Ralph's notes and the sources disagree, the source wins and the difference is listed.

## Facts (checked 2026-10-19)

| Fact | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Access | Beta, "available for select sellers"; shop approved, product active, the option shows in the mobile composer | Seller University, attentionclaw |
| Photos | At least 2; Seller University recommends 5 to 7; photo mode takes up to 35 | Seller University; postfa.st / third-party guides |
| Format | 9:16 (1080x1920), JPEG or WebP | Seller University; third-party guides |
| Products | Several per post (attentionclaw: up to 10). App: tap "Products" again for each link. Seller Center: Content Hub > Post on TikTok > Photo Post (multiple products, single product, or shop) | Seller University, attentionclaw |
| Sound | Background music optional but recommended; check the sound's rights | Seller University, attentionclaw |
| Creator commission | Defaults to **60% of the short-video commission rate** | Seller University |
| Daily limit | None on the number of carousels | Seller University |
| Analytics | Photo-level and product-level views, clicks, orders, GMV (selected sellers) | attentionclaw |

Not confirmed by any source (from Ralph's notes, treat as leads): a text-to-speech voiceover on a photo post;
a follower minimum for access; a penalty for "static image / autocut" posts.

## Building one

- **Order = the script.** Slide 1 is the hook (strongest image), then detail, proof (what people say), then the close.
  attentionclaw's worked order: opening, context, detail, in the box, objection, action. Photos show in the order added.
- **Product fills the frame**, about 40 to 60 percent, from different angles (Seller University).
- **Text:** Seller University suggests overlay text with 1 to 3 selling points and warns against "overloading photos
  with text or stickers". For Ralph's clients, text on slides needs his yes first (his no-text rule covers videos;
  slideshows are text-led by nature, so ask once per client).
- **Post it as a real photo post, not an mp4 of stills.** Ralph's notes say lazy video exports of still pictures get
  flagged, and his own video rule already bans photo slideshows inside videos (no Ken Burns, no freezes).
- **Never tell viewers to tap a product link the post does not have.** Check by opening the post as a shopper,
  swiping every slide and tapping the product (attentionclaw).
- Caption and slide text get the same script checks as a video: humanizer and readability (`tiktok.py readability`).

## Done when

Every slide is 9:16 with the real product filling the frame, slide 1 hooks, the last slide closes, the products are
tagged and tested from a shopper's view, text (if any) is approved, humanized and short, and the music is licensed.
