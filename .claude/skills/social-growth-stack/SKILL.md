# Social growth stack

Ralph wants his TikTok and Instagram posts to reach more of the right people and turn them into buyers (2026-10-29:
"boost engagement and attract customers"). Twenty-three borrowed skills each know one part; this skill is the order
they run in, the facts they can't know about Ralph, and what done looks like. It holds no copy of their advice.

## The order of work

1. **Know who it's for.** Read the brand profile first; every skill below reads it too.
   - AI on Autopilot / Day One AI: Dayone-ai `products/ai-on-autopilot/brand/brand-profile.md`.
   - A new brand or client with no profile: run `brand-profile`, then `content-pillars` (3 to 4 lanes) and
     `audience-research`. Grace has her own playbook instead (grace/PLAYBOOK.md); don't write her a profile.
2. **Find out what's broken before fixing anything.** Pull the real numbers (below) and run `tiktok-growth` or
   `instagram-growth` to name the one stuck stage. At under ~200 views the stage is reach (hook, completion, niche),
   not the script's selling lines.
3. **Write.** `hook-writer` for the first second, `tiktok-script` / `reels-script` for the body,
   `caption-writer` + `social-seo` for the caption and the search phrase, `hashtag-strategy` for tags,
   `carousel-writer` / `tiktok-photo-mode` for swipe posts. Then Ralph's own layer, always: `humanizer`, the
   reading-level check and the job's gates.
4. **Sell.** `social-selling-and-dm` and `lead-magnets-and-funnels` for the comment-to-DM step, `offers` +
   `marketing-psychology` + `copywriting` for the product page, `creator-monetization` for what to sell next.
5. **Learn.** Every 2 to 3 days run `viral-reverse-engineering` on our own posts and `tiktok-shop-coach` (compare,
   viral board) on the top posts in the lane; write what won into the job's brain note and the brand profile.

## What the borrowed skills can't know

- **Accounts.** AI on Autopilot posts on TikTok @azariah2244 (Composio `tiktok_bac-stouth`) and Instagram
  @realgoodhustles (`instagram_bedder-setier`). Ralph keeps these accounts and builds them up rather than starting
  new ones (2026-10-29). House of Rameau is `tiktok_buck-flunk`; Grace posts from her own phone.
- **Tools.** The social-media skills name WoopSocial (the maker's paid scheduler) for publishing and analytics. We
  don't use it. Numbers come from Composio (`TIKTOK_LIST_VIDEOS`: views, likes, comments, shares;
  `INSTAGRAM_GET_IG_MEDIA_INSIGHTS`: views, reach, saved, shares) plus Ralph's TikTok Studio screenshots for average
  watch time and "watched full video", which the API does not give. Publishing: TikTok drafts via Composio (Ralph
  posts them), Instagram from his app. Ignore any step that needs a paid tool unless Ralph OKs it with the cost.
- **Content mix (Ralph 2026-10-29).** Not every post sells. Fill the feed with posts on the buyer's lane: "AI helped
  me do this" stories, quick AI wins, myth vs truth, before/after, empowering lines with an AI angle. Pure motivation
  quotes with no AI pull a crowd that doesn't buy an AI guide (the 2025 motivation posts on @azariah2244 got
  286-390 views from that crowd). Text-over-video posts are fine when each one uses a different real stock clip, layout and colour.
- **Standing rules that bind every post** (their home: Mymobileworld CLAUDE.md and the live rules doc): no price in a
  video; TikTok captions never say "link in bio"; every video looks different (no reused footage or one template);
  the AI label goes on; no income claims; ask before anything paid with the exact cost.

## What done looks like

- Every post has a named lane and a search phrase, and its numbers get read back after 48 hours.
- The reel routine's own checks pass (for AI on Autopilot: `reels/ROUTINE.md`, including `tools/engage_check.py`).
- A change that worked or failed is written down with its numbers, so the next post starts from it.
