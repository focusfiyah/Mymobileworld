# Read the reference until you could rebuild it blind

For your own reading only. Describe the reference in your own words; never put its frames, creator name or exact lines into our
cut or a generation prompt. Inputs: `sheets/`, `full/`, `measure.txt`, the transcript.

1. Concept in one sentence (what it does to the viewer), then the skeleton: numbered beats with the topic stripped out.
2. Why it holds attention: mechanism | seconds | effect. Name the hook and how fast it lands, each open loop (plant and payoff
   time), the escalation, the personal hooks, the twist, the ending that asks something of the viewer.
3. Per-second table: t | words | picture | camera (from measure.txt) | sound.
4. Measured style: cuts per minute, hold length, words per second, a new fact every n seconds, palette, where captions sit.
5. Keep and swap: what we keep (rhythm, camera grammar, structure) and what is ours (product, shots, words, grade, room).
6. The three weakest seconds of the reference and how ours fixes each.
