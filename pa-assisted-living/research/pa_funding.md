## 2. Verdict

**There is no Pennsylvania state startup GRANT for a for-profit personal care home or ALR.** The only care-home grant found (the DOH Quality Investment Program, up to $2,500 per licensed bed) pays homes that are already licensed, and its 2026 round closed October 1. Most DCED grants (Keystone Communities, NAP) go to towns and nonprofits.

**Realistic stack for 10 beds (20 later):**
1. Owner equity, 10-20% of the project (lenders treat care homes as special-purpose property).
2. SBA 7(a) for build-out, furniture and 6-12 months of operating cash; SBA 504 if buying or building the property.
3. PIDA Small Business First alongside the bank loan, if the CEDO says a PCH qualifies (up to $1M or 50% of real estate cost at ~5%).
4. USDA B&I guarantee instead of SBA if the site is rural.
5. A CDFI or SBA microloan for the gap (up to $50K).
6. Free training money after opening: CareerLink OJT (about half of new aides' wages for months), the local Industry Partnership's aide training, WEDnetPA if the 2025-26 guide now allows assisted living.
7. Once licensed: watch for the next DOH Quality Investment round and county grants.

## 3. What an application needs

- **Business plan:** services, PCH vs ALR license choice, market (seniors 75+ nearby, local competitors and their prices, occupancy), private-pay vs SSI/Medicaid mix, staffing plan (ratios, administrator with PA PCH administrator training), marketing, management bios.
- **Financials:** 3-5 year projections (monthly year 1), ramp-up to occupancy, break-even month, sources & uses table, startup budget, cash reserve.
- **Jobs:** number of full-time jobs created/retained in 3 years and wages (PIDA ~1 job per $75K).
- **Site control:** deed, lease, or purchase agreement; zoning OK; building/fire code review.
- **Licensing status:** DHS license application or timeline (PCH: DHS Bureau of Human Services Licensing; ALR: DHS Office of Long-Term Living).
- **Owners:** personal financial statements, 3 years of tax returns, credit reports, resumes; collateral list; personal guarantees (20%+ owners for SBA).
- **Company papers:** formation documents, EIN, operating agreement, insurance quotes.
- **Bids:** contractor estimates and equipment quotes.
- **Letters of support:** county, area agency on aging, local hospital or doctors, referral sources.
- **Federal money:** SAM.gov registration and UEI number (any federal grant).
- **Grants:** project narrative, budget justification, outcome measures.

## 4. Why applications get rejected

- Not an eligible applicant (for-profit applying to nonprofit/municipal programs; urban site for USDA).
- No licensed operator or no license path; no site control or zoning.
- Too little owner equity or weak personal credit; no collateral.
- Projections not believable (fast fill-up, low staff costs, no reserve); no market proof.
- Job numbers too low for the loan size.
- Missing documents, missed deadline, or no SAM/UEI.
- Grant asks for things the program does not fund (startup costs when it funds existing licensed beds).
- No management experience in senior care.
